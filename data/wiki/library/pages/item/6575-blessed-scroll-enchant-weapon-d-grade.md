# Blessed Scroll: Enchant Weapon (D-Grade)

- Source: https://masterwork.wiki/lu4-b-w-c/item/6575-blessed-scroll-enchant-weapon-d-grade
- Path: /lu4/item/6575-blessed-scroll-enchant-weapon-d-grade
- Type: item
- Key: item:6575

## Stats
- Effect: Increases P. Atk. of a D-Grade weapons such as sword/one-handed, blunt/one handed, dagger, spear, sword/two-handed, blunt/two-handed, dual swords, and fists, etc. type will increase by 2 and bow type by 4. The M.Atk of all weapons will increase by 2. From +4, the added bonuses for P.Atk/M.Atk are double the above value. When fail on enchanting, item does not get crystallized but it's enchant value will return to 0.
- Weight: 120
- Item Skills: Scroll: Enchant Weapon (Grade D) Lv. 1
- Restrictions: Private Warehouse Clan Warehouse Transfer within Game Account Trade Drop Private Store Enchant Attribute Olympiad Use
- Grade: NG
- Type: Other / Blessed Weapon Enchant Scroll
