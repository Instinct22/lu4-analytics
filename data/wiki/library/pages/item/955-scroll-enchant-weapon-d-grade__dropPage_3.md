Other / Weapon Enchant Scroll
When enchanted P. Atk. of all D-Grade weapons will increase by 2 with the exception of bows, which will increase by 4. M. Atk. of all weapons increases by 2. From +4, P. Atk. and M. Atk. bonus will double for all weapon types. It can be safely enchanted up to +3.
