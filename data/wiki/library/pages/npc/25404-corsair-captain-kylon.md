Raid Boss
Lv. 1
This pirate once ruled the South Sea of Giran along with Pirate King Zaken. Calling himself Zaken's only rival, he raided the Pirate King's headquarters after hearing the news that Zaken was missing. Instead of treasure, he and his mean were struck down by Zaken's curse. Now an undead, his sole aim is to take his revenge on Zaken someday.
