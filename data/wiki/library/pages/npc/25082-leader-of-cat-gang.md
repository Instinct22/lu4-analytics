The leader of a gang of cats, all pets long abandoned by their masters. They are known for snatching fresh fish from the very nets of the Fishermen, and the Fish Traders' Association has offered a great bounty for them. Many hunters have tried to catch the cats, but ultimately gave up their chase because the cats were too cute.
