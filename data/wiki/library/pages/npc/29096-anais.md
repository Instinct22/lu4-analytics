Although he appears human, he is actually a high-ranking Angel who directly interacts with Einhasad and worships the Grail. Recently an ancient religious document found in the Tower of Insolence provided proof that the Grail is hidden in the Monastery of Silence. Since then, adherents of the Pagan Temple have frequently tried to steal it, but none has avoided the judgment of the Lord of Splendor.
