The chieftess of the Langk Lizardmen, she serves the evil spirit Bifrons in exchange for great power. At the command of Bifrons, she captured and slaughtered priests and the Disciples of God traveling on the south road. Recently, she has begun devoting herself to expanding the tribe's power in the north by taking advantage of the chaos in Gludio.
