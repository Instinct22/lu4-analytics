Raid Boss
Lv. 1
Artificial creature made from Hardin's magic experiment. Although it has the body of a monster erupting lava all over, it used to be an obedient golem that followed Hardin like a father. It went crazy after Hardin left this Swamp of Flames with the thought that it was deserted. When King Astaire invaded Aden Kingdom, some Elmore soldiers trapped in the Swamp of Flames witnessed a giant covered with lava.
