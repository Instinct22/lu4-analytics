Raid Boss

Once the General Construction Manager in charge of building the Tower of Insolence at command of Emperor Baium, he was executed by the enraged Emperor after numerous construction delays. His lingering spirit became a powerful ghost when the Energy of Life poured out of the Tower when it collapsed. He still wanders near the Tower of Insolence, waiting to take his revenge on the imprisoned Emperor.
