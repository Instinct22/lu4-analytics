This executioner once served the great Pirate Zaken, even putting to death Zaken's rebellious crew members. Ironically, he later decapitated Zaken himself during the riot. Following his resurrection as an undead because of Zaken's curse, he swore to never again disobey his captain's commands. He now guards Zaken's treasure in the Pirate Tunnel.
