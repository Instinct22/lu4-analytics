This merciless and reckless warrior has led the Ketra Orcs for many years. A born gambler, he prefers direct action to elaborate plans, and personally leads his troops from the front line when attacking. He has faced Varka's Commander Mos twice, winning one of the contests, and is eager for a rematch.
