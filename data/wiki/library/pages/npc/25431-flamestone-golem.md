Created by the ancient mystics of the Ivory Tower while they were investigating the Secrets of Infinite Flame contained in the flame stone, the Golem seemed to be a failed experiment and was discarded. Reawakened recently, it now wanders near the Ivory Tower and interferes with Spirits of Flame. Not surprisingly, it has become a major headache for the mystics.
