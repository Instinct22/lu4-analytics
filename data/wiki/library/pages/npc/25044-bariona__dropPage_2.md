The Knight of Elmoreden, the Commander of the soldiers studying the Tower of Insolence, under order of General Karte. As the right arm of General Karte, he is a brave knight willing to go to Hell if ordered, for like the general, he has an immortal body. He received his secret powers from the Devil Rahha, who also warned him to watch Tirak closely.
