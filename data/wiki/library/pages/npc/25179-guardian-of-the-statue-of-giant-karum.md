Raid Boss
Lv. 1
Another subordinate of Glaki. Like Glaki, he also guards the statue of the giants but he does not consider this work a sacred duty. According to legend, the Statue of the Giants can resurrect an incomplete body of a giant to regain a complete body. It is said that the reason he is guarding the statue is to earn the trust of Glaki but to fulfill his secret ambition to obtain a perfect and powerful body.
