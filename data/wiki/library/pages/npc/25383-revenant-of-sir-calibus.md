This Knight was executed after siding with the peasants during the Dion Peasant Riot against the brutal Duke Ashton. He slew Guillotine after the latter killed the captured peasant rebels and their families, and for that was brutally executed. He became a ghost because of his undying rage over Ashton's injustice.
