Raid Boss
Lv. 1
The mechanical golem that the Black Anvil Guild is desperately looking for. It is the most advanced golem design. It animated and ran amok because the artisan accidentally installed a defective component. It partially destroyed the workshop, crushed many Dwarven Artisans who tried to restrain it, and escaped the town. It wanders the woods aimlessly, and attacks any who come nearby.
