A monster plant that has grown by absorbing the poison in the Sea of Spores. It is a follower of Orfen, the Ruler of the Sea of Spores. It was given a task by Orfen to kill trespassing humans. Ivory Tower mystics are sent to tend to the magic force field that prevents spores from spreading, but many have been slain by the Rotting Tree.
