Raid Boss
Lv. 1
Gracia's Great Noble who was not able to return to his country after losing in the Aden-Gracia War. During the war, he became lost inside of Cruma Tower with his troops. While inside the tower he had the opportunity to recruit a few Lesser Giants to his cause. When he finally escaped from the tower, the war was already over. The only thing he was left with was a few loyal servants and an enormous bounty on his head. Presently, he is desperately trying to flee to Elmore Kingdom to preserve his life.
