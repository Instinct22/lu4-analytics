During the golden years of Elmoreden, this King of Beasts was captured and brought to the Coliseum as a spectacle to entertain the Emperor and his court. When the empire fell, he was sealed beneath the Coliseum by mystics afraid of his vengeance, but late escaped. His gigantic arms can shatter rocks, and he is able to rule all beasts.
