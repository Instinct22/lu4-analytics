A servant of Summoner Sejarr, dispatched from the Ivory Tower to block the Devil Beleth. Sejarr used dark magic to imprison Beleth, but was killed by the devil's reflexive powers. Phantom alone survived of Sejarr's servitors, but was corrupted by the dark magic of Beleth and the Ivory Tower and reconciled with the Wasteland. Although he thinks his owner's death was unjust, he distrust other mystics.
