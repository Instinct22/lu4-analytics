Raid Boss
Lv. 1
This powerful Chaos Golem was made from Star Stones gathered from the Ruins of Pavel by the mad scientist Dr. Chaos, who styles himself the Emperor of Evil. Once a Golem Maestro, Dr. Chaos was expelled from the Black Anvil Guild for trying to steal the blueprint from the elders. He swore revenge on the Black Anvil Guild, conquering the Ruins of Pavel with the Horrifying Golem Army he created. He views all outsiders as assassins sent by the Guild and will attack immediately. His ultimate goal is 'world domination.'.
