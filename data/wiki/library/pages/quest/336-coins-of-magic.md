Basics
The Quest Chain Coins of Magic is one of the main quests for obtaining C-grade Armor, Jewelry
and Weapon Parts. The Lu4 team has reworked the quest chain and rewards while maintaining
the overall concept.
The quest involves hunting monsters and collecting Magic Coins, which can be exchanged
for Equipment at an NPC.
Lu4 introduces important changes to a quest familiar to many players. Features of the quest on Lu4:
The list of monsters for hunting has been reworked
All types of Magic Coins drop from all monsters included in the hunting list
Coin exchange is only required to increase the Coin Collector rank
Increasing the Coin Collector rank grants access to more expensive goods and continuation of the quest chain
To purchase Equipment, only Coins obtained from monster hunting are required
Coins can be exchanged and traded with other players
In the subsequent quests of the chain, you can obtain B-grade weapon recipes.
To purchase them, you will need Magic Coins, which can only be obtained in this quest.
Join the Coin Collectors and collect Magic Coins!
