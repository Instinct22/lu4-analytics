1: Repose of the Dead
Priest Ross asks you to go to the Field of Massacre and destroy monsters in order to collect Fallen Warrior’s Insignia and Fallen Knight’s Token for a prayer for the repose of the souls of fallen heroes. If you encounter Einhasad's Altar of Mercy, you can offer the prayer yourself. Monsters to be hunted - Grave Lich, Dismal Pole, Doom Scout, Doom Trooper, Doom Warrior, Doom Archer, Doom Guard, Doom Servant, Doom Knight, Spiteful Soul Leader, Spiteful Soul Wizard, Spiteful Soul Fighter.
