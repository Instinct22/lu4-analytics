Trial of the Pilgrim Quest guide
1. Starting NPC
Santiago
Hermit
is located next to the house east of the teleport point
in the Orc Barracks. Talk to Santiago to start the quest.
Santiago will give
Voucher of Trial
NG
2. Teleport to Orc Village. Find
Tanapi
Seer
on the first floor of the Hall of King and talk to him.
3. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials.
Move inside the cave and always keep the right side. Inside the cave find
Ancestor Martankus
and talk to him.
4. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Forgotten Temple.
Head inside, to the fire part of the temple.
In the last room turn left. Find and kill monsters:
Lava Salamander
Quest Monster
Lv. 35
Until you receive
Essence of Flame
NG
5. Return to
Ancestor Martankus
and talk to him.

Spirit of Flame

Tanapi
Seer

Gauri Twinklerock

Tag of Rumor
NG

Dorf
Wanderer
and talk to him.

Gray Badge
NG

Primos

Petron

Uruha
