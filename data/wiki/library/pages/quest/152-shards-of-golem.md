1: New Tools
A few days ago the roof of the temple was wrecked by a storm. Magister Harrys has asked Blacksmith Altran for tools with which to repair the roof. He asks you to pick up the tools from Blacksmith Altran.
2: Stone Hammer
Blacksmith Altran asks you to obtain Golem Shards for him so he can make a new hammer. Hunt in the Talking Island, North - Stone Golems.

3: Return to Blacksmith Altran
You have obtained 50 Golem Shards. Take them to Blacksmith Altran and ask him to make a hammer for you.
4: Delivery of a Tool Box
Blacksmith Altran asks you to deliver the hammer and a tool box containing other tools to Magister Harrys.
