Dark Elven Tetrarch Thifiell asked Abyssal Celebrant Kartia to translate an ancient manuscript. He now asks you to retrieve the translated copy of 'Kaysha's Revelations'. You must be very discreet.
2: In Search of the Stolen Revelations
You hear that 'Kaysha's Revelations' was stolen by Tumran Orcs! Kill the Tumran Orc brigands that were last seen camping near the Altar of Rites, and recover the original copy of 'Kaysha's Revelations' and a clay tablet.
