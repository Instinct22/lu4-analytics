A Silenos who was a victim of an experiment gone awry, conducted by the Ivory Tower's mystics. Shacram gained supernatural power, but his appearance was so disfigured that he could not return to his tribe. He moved to Oren where he hides in a deserted valley in the south, where rugged mountain paths discourage most from ever setting foot in the region. He harbors a deep hatred towards the mystics of the Ivory Tower who made him like this.
