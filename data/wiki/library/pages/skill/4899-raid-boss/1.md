This immortal creature was created by Dark Elven dark magic. During the early days of the war between the Elves and Dark Elves, the Dark Elves found themselves on the defensive. Experimenting with the power of Dark Arts they had embraced, they resurrected a number of slain warriors. Once a brave elven knight, Ferkel became of those revived warriors turned into bloodthirsty undead beasts. He slaughtered countless fellow Elves during the war and even now thirsts for the blood of living beings.
