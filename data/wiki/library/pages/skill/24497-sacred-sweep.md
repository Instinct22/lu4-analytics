## Stats
- Effect: Releases a wave in front of the caster. Inflicts physical damage on multiple enemies. Power: 1082 in PvP, 1407 in PvE. When enemy in PvP is affected by 'Seal of Judgment', applies to enemy: P. Def. -10%. Duration: 5 sec. When enemy in PvE is affected by 'Seal of Judgment': Applies 'Seal of Judgment' to 3 targets around enemy. Ignores Shield Defence. Over-hit and Critical hit are possible.
- Consumes: 50 MP
- Reuse Time: 8 seconds
- Can be used on Olympiad: Yes
- Attribute: None
- Trait: trait_holy
- Cast Range: 70 (400)
- Available For: ProphetLv. 52
