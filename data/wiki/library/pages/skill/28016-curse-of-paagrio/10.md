Curse of Pa'agrio
Lv. 10
Magical / Magical
A curse that deals damage with power starting from 82 and has a chance to trigger a random debuff on each affected target from those available to the character. The assortment of debuffs depends on the level of the skill Curse of Pa'agrio, and the levels of the debuffs depend on their levels in the character.
