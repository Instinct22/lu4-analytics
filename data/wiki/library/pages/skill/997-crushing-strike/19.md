Crushing Strike
Lv. 19
Physical / Physical
Impacts the enemy with 5050 Power added to P. Atk, decreasing the target's P. Def. by 10% and M. Def. by 10%. Decreases the target's Stun resistance by 10%. Over-hit is possible. Cancels Resist Shock effect from the target with 35% rate. Cancels Improved Resist effect from the target with 25% rate.
