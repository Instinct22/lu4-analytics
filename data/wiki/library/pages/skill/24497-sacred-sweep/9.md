Sacred Sweep
Lu4
Sacred Sweep
Lv. 9
Physical / Physical
Releases a wave in front of the caster.
Inflicts physical damage on multiple enemies. Power: 2288 in PvP, 2974 in PvE.
When enemy in PvP is affected by 'Seal of Judgment', applies to enemy:
P. Def. -10%.
Duration: 5 sec.
When enemy in PvE is affected by 'Seal of Judgment':
Applies 'Seal of Judgment' to 3 targets around enemy.
Ignores Shield Defence.
Over-hit and Critical hit are possible.

69 MP
Reuse Time
8 seconds
