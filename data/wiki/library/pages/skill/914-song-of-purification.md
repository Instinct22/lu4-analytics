# Song of Purification

- Path: /lu4/skill/914-song-of-purification
- Type: skill
- Key: skill:914

## Stats
- Effect: Removes up to 3 debuffs from party members. Applies to party members: Resistance to debuffs +30% Incoming Heal Power +30%. Additionally increases MP Consumption when used while Rhythms are in effect. Can't be used for 30 sec after joining a party. Can't be applied more than once every 120 seconds. Effect only works while in a party with the caster.
- Consumes: 60 MP
- Reuse Time: 4 minutes
- Duration: 30 seconds
- Can be used on Olympiad: Yes
- Attribute: None
- Trait: trait_none
- Available For: SwordsingerLv. 60
- Type: Rhythm / Buff
