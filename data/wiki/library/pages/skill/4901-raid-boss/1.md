This servant of the Fairy Queen was dispatched to protect the hot springs, a resort that Fairy Queen Timiniel loves. With power almost equal to the Queen's, he is a little disgruntled at what he feels is so menial an assignment. To pass the time and ease his boredom, he often 'plays' with travelers passing by. Unfortunately for them, his hijinks are more than most humans can endure.
