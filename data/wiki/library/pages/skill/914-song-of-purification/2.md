Song of Purification
Lv. 2
Rhythm / Buff
Removes up to 3 debuffs from party members.
Applies to party members:
Resistance to debuffs +30%
Incoming Heal Power +30%.
Additionally increases MP Consumption when used while Rhythms are in effect.
Can't be used for 30 sec after joining a party.
Can't be applied more than once every 120 seconds.
Effect only works while in a party with the caster.
