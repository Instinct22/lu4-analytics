This powerful Death Lord rose up from Purgatory at the behest of Death Lord Hallate. Unlike Hallate, who exhausted most of his power breaking the seal of the Tower of Insolence, Ipos has preserved most of his. He prefers conspiracies and subtle plots to direct action and is currently keenly interested in the situation near Aden Castle. He seems to have some kind of relationship with several high-ranking nobles in Aden Kingdom.
