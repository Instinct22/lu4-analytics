Crokian Chief who rules Alligator Island. As a native Crokian who has lived in Innadril before humans and elves came, he hates outsiders and tries to protect his territory and tribe from them. Although he is not happy about the Water Couatl Ateka living on the same island, he cannot do anything about it because he is afraid of Water Dragon Fafurion. He dreams about the day when Crokians can rule Innadril again.
