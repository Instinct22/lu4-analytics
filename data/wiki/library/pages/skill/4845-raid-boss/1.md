An Evil Spirit worshipped like a god by the Langk Lizardmen who live in the south of Gludio. He looks like a snake and has ruled the spiritual world of the lizardmen for hundreds of years. However, he resents the cursed body the gods in the heaven created for him, and he tries to slaughter the messengers of the gods in retribution. He is very violent and burns to destroy the entire area of Gludio one day through the Langk Lizardmen.
