The brave and great Tiger King. Despite his beastly looks, he rules the Silenos thanks to his divine nature and bravery. Originally, he was one of the Evil Spirits that Shilen created, but he has learned not to hate the gods and endeavored to live up to his position as Tiger King after accumulating wisdom over the years. Among the Silenos Tribe, he is accompanied by loyal shamans.
