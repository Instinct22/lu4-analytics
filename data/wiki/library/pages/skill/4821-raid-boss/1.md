The mechanical golem that the Black Anvil Guild is desperately looking for. It is the most advanced golem design. It partially destroyed the workshop, crushed many Dwarven Artisans who tried to restrain it, and escaped the town. It wanders the woods aimlessly, and attacks any who come nearby.
