Basics
Completing the "Alliance with Ketra Orcs" quest allows you to obtain high-level resources,
parts and recipes for S-grade equipment.
The quest is divided into five stages. As your alliance level rises, you unlock more valuable
rewards and gain access to additional features in the Ketra Orc Village.
To increase the alliance level to 4 and 5, you have to complete additional quests and kill raid bosses:
