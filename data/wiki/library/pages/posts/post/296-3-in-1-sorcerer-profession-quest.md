Quests to change class
27.10.2025
Basics
A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests
specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession
Sorcerer
:
Quest Trial of the Scholar
Quest Testimony of Trust
Quest Test of Magus
On MasterWork, all quests are available
from the character's level 35.
This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Sorcerer profession together.
After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession.
