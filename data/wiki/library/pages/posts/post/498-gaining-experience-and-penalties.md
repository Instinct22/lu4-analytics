Farming tips
Monsters
For efficient levelling, it is best to choose locations with monsters no lower than 5 levels below,
and no higher than 4 levels above your character.
In cases where you cannot find a free spot with "white" monsters, you may farm "blue" monsters
with small penalties. Going to a location with "red" monsters makes no sense, due to high penalties for both
levelling and drop/spoil.
At each stage of the game, you will have a sufficient choice of locations, both regular and improved by our team,
as well as completely new ones, to find a comfortable levelling spot with monsters of appropriate levels.
Bosses
On Lu4, it makes no sense to farm "red" and "blue" RBs, because in addition to the high difficulty of the farm,
the drop will be reduced, up to its absence.
The current boss level window relative to the character is from "4 levels below" to "3 levels above".
