Basics
Quest "1000 Years, the End of Lamentation" — one of the ways to obtain
Weapons, Armor, Jewelry, C-grade Enchant Scrolls, various resources, and Adena.
The Lu4 team has reworked the quest and rewards while preserving the overall concept of the quest.
The quest consists of hunting monsters and obtaining special items —
Items of Dead Heroes. These items can be exchanged for a random reward from an NPC.
Lu4 introduces important changes to the quest familiar to many players. Features of the quest on Lu4:
Changed chances of obtaining a Rare Reward
Increased chance for 50 items
Maximum chance limited to 70%
Significantly expanded reward list
Various types of Mid and Top C-grade Weapons
Top C-grade Armor
C-grade Jewelry
C-grade Enchant Scrolls
Various materials
