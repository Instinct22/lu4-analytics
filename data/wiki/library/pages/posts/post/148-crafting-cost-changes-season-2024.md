Crafting cost changes (season 2024)
Custom mechanics
26.09.2024
Basics
Adding Adena to the equipment crafting recipe of some grades was one of the innovations of the MasterWork 2023
season. A small amount of Adena per craft improved the economy by hundreds of millions Adena, which reduced the
inflation rate of the main game currency.
In MasterWork 2024, we are expanding the list of recipes that include Adena as a crafting material.
In addition, the required amount will depend on the server stage, making mid-tier equipment more affordable.
During the early stages of the game, the higher crafting cost challenges those who like a difficult game to the
edge of accessible content. If you want to get something out of reach for others, you must put in even more effort.
Pay attention!
D and C grades have no additional cost.
