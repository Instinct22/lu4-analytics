On this Primeval Isle you'll find primitive creatures called dinosaurs -- you'll never see anything like them
in Aden, believe me! According to wise wisards, even the Gods and Giants were afraid of the dinosaurs. They
cannot be found in Aden -- thankfully -- but are still plentiful on that strange island.
Basics
In 2018 MasterWork team implemented and now successfully handling a phased development system for the
Primeval Isle.
Players must perform certain actions to boost the stage and explore better farming spots.

How to improve the stage
To improve the location to stage 2 you must kill the raid boss
Sailren
Sealed Evil Power
Lv. 80
5 times.
To improve the location to stage 3, you must kill the raid boss
Sailren
Sealed Evil Power
Lv. 80
once during stage 2 (6 kills total).
To improve the location to stage 4, you must kill the raid boss
Sailren
Sealed Evil Power
Lv. 80
once during stage 3 (7 kills total).
How to downgrade the stage
If within 24 hours after improving the stage
Sailren
Sealed Evil Power
Lv. 80
was not killed,
then the stage downgrades automatically.
From the moment a location improved to stage 4, it will automatically decrease to stage 3 after 3 hours.
Players have 4 hours to kill
Sailren
Sealed Evil Power
Lv. 80
.
If the boss is not defeated within 4 hours, the stage of the location will be lowered.
