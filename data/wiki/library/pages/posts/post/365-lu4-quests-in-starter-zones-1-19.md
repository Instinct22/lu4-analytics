Useful quests
09.05.2026
Basics
The developers of Lu4 paid close attention to character development and progression at low levels.
Quests in starting locations have been revised.
Several outdated quests have been removed, while most remaining quests now offer improved rewards.
The overhaul also affected the mechanics of quest item accumulation:
Items accumulate without limits, and the reward depends on the number of items collected
Items drop for a random player in the group, which allows support classes to complete quests
This article compiles all the quests for characters of levels 1–19 on Lu4.
