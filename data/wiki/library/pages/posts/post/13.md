Basic information
Masterwork items or rare items are pieces of equipment that have additional options.
Rare items are part of a set of armor on par with common ones. Getting rare items is
possible +crafting or +recognizing Unidentified items obtained during monster hunting.
Rare equipment has been updated:
Bonuses have been improved
Mammon’s Varnish Enhancer has been removed
Rare upper and full armor are now part of a set
For detailed information about rare weapon bonuses, read the article
