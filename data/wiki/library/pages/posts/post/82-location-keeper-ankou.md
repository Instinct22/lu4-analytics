Location Keeper Ankou
Locations
06.01.2026
Basics
Location Keeper Ankou is a new mini-event designed specifically for MasterWork.
Don't sleep on the spot, otherwise Ankou will come and take your life!
Ankou is a pretty strong monster with many nasty skills and surprises. But the winners will get expensive rewards,
such as masterwork spheres, buff slot books and boxes with enchanting items.
Where to find?
The location guardian Ankou appears when a certain number of monsters die in the location.
Ankou level depends on the location:
Cemetery - Level 55
Paver Ruins - Level 58
Dragon Valley - Level 58
Forbidden Gateway - Level 62
Forest Of The Dead - Level 68
Blazing Swamp - Level 72
Wall of Agros - Level 75
Hot Springs - Level 78
Imperial Tomb - Level 80
Silent Valley - Level 80
Hellbound - Level 85
Pay attention!
Now, Ankou patrols the area and does not vanish until brave warriors defeat him in a fair fight.
