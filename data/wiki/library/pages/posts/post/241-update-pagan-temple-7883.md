Update: Pagan Temple (78~83)
Update: Pagan Temple (78~83)
Locations
14.08.2025
Have you ever seen a pagan temple? Anyway, you've heard of it, right?
It looks more like a Clan Hall for Pagans than any other temple I've ever seen!
The Pagans are always lurking behind the scenes, secretly pulling the strings that control Rune's castle.
Nobody knows what they are doing there, since they never let strangers inside...
I'm sure they're keeping some terrible secret...
Basics
On MasterWork location Pagan Temple is presented in an updated form:
Expanded spots
More monsters
Raid Boss has been strengthened and drop have been improved
Reworked barriers between spots
All internal doors except Anterooms have been removed
Passing quests have been eliminated
Direct teleport to the Temple from cities and villages
All ordinary monsters are level 80. The location is suitable for a single players, small groups and
players in full groups that can handle large numbers of monsters.
In the Pagan Temple equipment of A and S grade is available to farm.
Location Map
Pagan Temple consist of 2 main parts:
Entrance — for solo players and small groups
Inner Part — for small and full groups
Teleport points, number of monsters and their respawn time is shown on the map.
Entrance
Location part that is suitable for solo players and small groups.
While hunting monsters in a central hall you can receive an
Anteroom Key
NG
.
With this key you can open one of eight doors that lead to the rooms with monsters.
Most of the monsters in the location are undead. Healer classes are effectively leveling in this location
by using skill:
Turn Undead
Lv. 10
enchanted from +1 for Chance + Attack
You can take a humanoid monster out of a room through a door and leave him there by using skills
with the hold or sleep effect.
