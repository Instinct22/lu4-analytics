Basics
The clan system is the foundation of social interaction between players. Clans allow players to unite
to achieve goals that are unattainable for individual players — capturing castles and killing epic bosses.
Creating a clan
To create a clan, a character must be level 10 or higher. Speak to the High Priest in a temple or the
Grand Master in any guild. Later, these NPCs will help you level up the clan and learn clan skills.
A clan name can include up to 16 letters or numbers, spaces and special characters are not allowed.
Pay attention!
Squad and Academy are not available on Lu4.
Clan Reputation system is not available.
Clan Crest
A unique logo (Crest) can be installed by clan members of level 3 and above.
Installing/changing a clan emblem is available to the clan leader and other members who have the appropriate privilege.
To install the emblem, open the clan window (Alt+N) and click the “Management” button.
In the opened window that appears, select the “Clan emblem” tab, then click the “Add Clan Crest” button and
specify the path to the emblem file. The file must be an image in the .png or .bmp format, 16 by 12 pixels in size.
If the image is larger than 16 by 12 pixels, it will be automatically resized to the correct dimensions, but
the result may not be satisfactory.
Transferring Clan Leader Rights
To transfer clan leadership rights to another member, the current clan leader must visit a special NPC
and submit a request for the transfer. The rights will be transferred after the server's technical restart.
The transfer can be canceled at any time before the restart.
Pay attention!
On Lu4 servers, clan leadership can be transferred without a restart for

Leaving a Clan
A player can leave a clan at any time. However, there are several penalties associated with this:
A player who leaves a clan voluntarily receives a 4-hour restriction on joining another clan.
If a player is expelled by the clan leader, the player receives a 4-hour penalty on joining another clan.
Additionally, the clan receives a 4-hour penalty on recruiting new members.
The clan leader cannot leave the clan.
Clan Disbandment
To disband a clan, the Leader must speak to the High Priest in the temple or
the Grand Master in any guild.
The Leader can disband the clan without the consent of other clan members.
A clan cannot be disbanded if it is participating in a clan war.
Disbandment takes seven days, during which the request can be canceled.

Active skills
The Lu4 team added active clan skills to the game to diversify and increase the effectiveness of clan activity.
New clan skills are available to clan Leaders level 2 or higher.
Only the Leader can use the skills
Active clan skills are automatically learned by the Clan Leader
The effect of the skill only works around the Clan Leader
Skills only apply to clan members
