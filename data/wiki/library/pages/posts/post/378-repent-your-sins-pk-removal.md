The Black Judge gives sinners a chance for redemption. Will you walk the path of penitence, sinner?
Beware, it is filled with trials and tribulations!
Starting NPC
Black Judge
Starting location
Wasterland, Gludin Territory
Floran Village, Dion Territory
Hardin's Private Academy, Giran Territory
Level
1 ~ 85
Type
Repeatable
Condition
PK count ≥ 1
Reward
Reduces PK counter by 1 - 10 per pet level
If you obtain or change subclass during the quest or while leveling up your pet, the quest
will be canceled and the manacles will disappear.
Quest guide
1. Talk to
Black Judge
to start the quest.
You can find her in three locations:
Wasteland
. Teleport to the Wasteland from Gludin. Turn your back to the
Wasteland entrance and run along the right side past the vendors. The Black Judge is standing near a rocks.
Screenshot with map
Floran Village
. Teleport to Floran from the Newbie Helper or Club Manager. Leave the village
through the southern gate and run down the road to the mill. The Black Judge is standing near the mill.
Screenshot with map
Hardin's Private Academy
. Teleport from Giran to Hardin's Private Academy.
Turn your back to the cave entrance and run deeper into the area. On your right, you'll see
a rocks between the trees — the Black Judge is standing on one of the rocks.
