Devil's Isle Survivor
Basics
Pailaka is a one-time quest with a strictly limited level. To complete it, players must pass an instance.
The reward is Experience and SP points, useful items, and Vitality points.
Pay attention!
In the basic game
Pailaka — Devil's Legacy
is available to characters of level 61 ~ 67
On MasterWork servers, this quest is available to players level 58 ~ 70
Quest guide
Talk to NPC
Devil's Isle Survivor
Pailaka - Devil's Legacy (Level 58-70)
on the left of the Giran temple to start the quest.
He will teleport the character to an instanced copy of the Devil's Isle.
You have 90 minutes to complete the instance, and 180 minutes to re-complete it if you fail.

"The enemies who invaded Devil's Isle are Lematan's Followers. Four of them are especially dangerous:
Kams, Hikoro, Alkaso and Gerbera. Some of them will have Scrolls of Enchant Weapon.
Defeat them and you can enhance your sword and Tigress... and become powerful enough to destroy Lematan."
