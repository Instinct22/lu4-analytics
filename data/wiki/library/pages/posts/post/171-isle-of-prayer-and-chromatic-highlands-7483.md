Isle of Prayer and Chromatic Highlands (74~83)
Locations
23.12.2025
An island north of Innadril where Fafurion's beloved Witch Parme lives. It was once a small and unprepossessing
island, but Fafurion transformed it into a majestic and beautiful island fit for a queen.
Basics
Isle of Prayer and Chromatic Highlands was significantly rebuild
to increase their exping and attribute farming attractiveness:
Number of monsters increased
EXP and SP gained amount increased
New spots added at both zones
Added new path to Chromatic Highland
Added new passive quest to obtain S-grade gear
Added new club teleport spot to Chromatic Highland:
Chromatics Highlands - I (original teleport)
Chromatics Highlands - II (new teleport)
Location mechanics
Defencive mechanic at Isle of Prayer "no more the three people" do not allow players
to farm in big groups.
In basic game, if your party have more than 3 players, and you kill monsters, you will be attacked
by very strong location guards —
Witch Warder
Lv. 79
. On MasterWork realms safe number of
party members increased to 5 players.
At Chromatic Highlands that mechanics doesn't work.
MasterWork team improved dragon spawn mechanics at Chromatic Highlands
for better farming and exping at the location.
At whole Chromatic Highlands territory
Dark Water Dragon
Lv. 82
lives. On their own dragons are not very
interesting, but their guards, monsters
Shade
Lv. 82
, drops
Earth Stone
NG
.

Fafurion Kindred
Lv. 82
spawns same as 4 monsters
Water Dragon Detractor
Lv. 82
.
Fafurion Kindred keeps no loot, but while he's alive, dragons will spawn next to him, that
allows efficient exping for full party. Besides that dragon drops
Wind Stone
NG
.
Fafurion Kindred has 2 traits:
Weakened Life
Lv. 1
— deadly poison, that gradually decreases health level
Disease
Lv. 9

Spirit of the Lake
NG

Water Dragon Detractor
Lv. 82

and you have to find a new victim.
In the original game
Fafurion Kindred
Lv. 82
appears at spawn
Dark Water Dragon
Lv. 82

that makes farming less comfortable.
Pay attention!
On MasterWork this mechanics changed — Fafurion Kindred spawns at Dark Water Dragon death point,
if he was killed inside his area. Otherwise, will appear at original spawn, same as before.
Although his living time increased.

Lv. 82
stats and acquired experience increased.
