Life Stones on Masterwork
Life Stones on Masterwork
Custom mechanics
09.03.2026
Basics
Life Stones — one of the ways to improve your gear and get some extra stats and (or) skills.
Besides that, installed Life Stone protects you from dropping your gear, after being killed by other players.
Specially for MasterWork augmentation system was rebuilt for player's convenience. In a basic
Life Stones are divided by level, but on MasterWork we abandoned this division. There is now a single type
of Stones, that can be used for augmentation at any level.
Best way to farm Life Stones — instances and bosses of any level. As the reward for killing bosses,
and, with a low chance, monsters you will receive

Combat skills from Life Stones work according to High Five mechanics, however, they have restrictions
on Power and Level, depending on the stage of the server.
Life Stone Skills divided by level — 56, 60, 64, 68, 70, 72, 74, 76, 79, 83.
After changing the stage, skills automatically increase in level. Passive and active buffs always have
Level 10. This way you will always have the skill at the highest available level.
Insertion and Extraction
You no longer need to find a blacksmith to insert the Life Stone — using the new game client allows you
to augment equipment directly from your inventory.
Press Augment button in inventory window
Select required Life Stone
Select an item. The number and type of gems required for augmentation will be indicated at the bottom of the window
Insert the Stone. If you are not satisfied with the result — you can immediately insert the next one.
Additionally, you will need adena to automatically extract an unsuccessful Stone
To remove Life Stone without installing a new one, ask a blacksmith at any city.

Jewelry skills
Two similar skills
in a same type
of jewelry don't stack — the character will only be affected
by the effect one of them. For example: two rings for protection from Paralysis will not give double
protection, only one, of two identical effects, same as the Ant Queen's ring.
Two similar skills
in different type
of jewelry stack — both effects will apply to the character.
For example: an earring with protection against Paralysis + 7% and a ring with protection against
Paralysis + 5% will give a total of +12% protection.
Two different skills in one type of jewelry stacks — both effects will apply to the character.
For example: an earring with protection from Paralysis + 7% and an earring with protection from
Shock attacks + 7% will give a total of +7% to protection from Paralysis and +7% to protection from Shock.
Pay attention!
