Useful quests
17.02.2026
Basics
The Quest Chain Coins of Magic is one of the main quests for obtaining C-grade Armor,
Jewelry, and Weapon Parts.
The Lu4 team has reworked the quest chain and rewards while maintaining the overall concept.
The final part of the chain — the quest Kail's Magic Coin involves hunting monsters and
obtaining Magic Coins, which can be exchanged with an NPC for C and B-grade weapon recipes.
Lu4 introduces important changes to a quest familiar to many players. Features of the quest
on Lu4:
The character level requirement has been reduced
The list of monsters to hunt has been changed
Join the Coin Collectors and collect Magic Coins!
