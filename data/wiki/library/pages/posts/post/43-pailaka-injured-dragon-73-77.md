"...I percieve darkness and chaos... The protector of our race, the great Red Dragon, lies imprisoned and in torment there.
Darkness, came from across the sea, now spreads its black shadow over our land. The Humans call it Kamaloka, and the shadow it casts — Pailaka.
Now even oru great protector is helpless before it! We must summon all oru strenght to defeat the darkness!"
Ketra Orc Shaman
Basics
Pailaka is a one-time quest with a strictly limited level. To complete it, players must pass an instance.
The reward is Experience and SP points, useful items, and Vitality points.
Pay attention!
Pailaka — Injured Dragon
is only available to characters level 73 ~ 77
Completing this quest does not affect the Alliance with the Varka Silenos
Quest guide
Talk to NPC
Ketra Orc Shaman
Pailaka - Injured Dragon (Level 73-77)
near the western gate of Goddard to start the quest. He will teleport
the character to an instanced copy of the Varka Silenos Outpost.
You have 90 minutes to complete the instance, and 180 minutes to re-complete it if you fail.

Valakas emerged — Latana. This powerful dragon chose to stand with the Ketra Orcs and turned its flames
against the Varka Silenos. The Orcs fought valiantly to defend Latana, but the Silenos proved stronger.
These horned beasts captured the Fire Dragon — our tribe’s sacred symbol!"

"The evil Silenos shamans, who possess a powerful force, are trying to change Latana's mind. Even if you
manage to get through the Silenos army to Latana, it is unlikely that he will be of sound mind.
In this case, you must free his spirit from his body so that he can turn into a Spirit of Fire. That is the desire of the Orcs.
The Fire Dragon Latana commands the fierce power of the fire element. To challenge him, you must wield the
Spear of Silenos — an artefact we reclaimed from the enemy. Though its true power remains dormant,
the Varka Silenos possess the knowledge to uncover an item that can awaken its full potential. Use the spear to vanquish your foes and liberate Latana's spirit!"

Behind the fourth wall of Varka Silenos is a braided dragon

Lv. 75

The dragon does not move but uses a long-range fire attack.
We recommend clearing the location of Antelopes and Flowers before fighting Latana
to collect health and invulnerability potions.
If your health is low during a fight with a dragon, move to a safe distance and use healing
potions, then return to the battle.
After Latana's death,
Ketra Orc Supporter
will appear. Talk to him to get a reward for completing the task.

Though we wish you could remain with us, the shamans have spoken — your path leads elsewhere, and you must return...."
