Basics
The Lu4 team has made a number of changes to the Raid Boss mechanics.
The core concept remains unchanged, but some aspects of gameplay have been reworked:
The location of some Raid Bosses has been changed
The respawn time of Raid Bosses has been changed
The drop of Raid Bosses has been reworked and updated
The "flag" mechanic applies when attacking all Raid Bosses
This article provides information about all Raid Bosses on Lu4.
Attention!
On the Boss page, you can view the marker on the World Map and the respawn time of the Raid Boss!
