Dear players, we’re excited to introduce the Lu4 Game Store.

Adventurers' Guide
Lu4 Manager

Master Coin
NG
Foundation
— the currency you receive for donations in your Personal Account
10 Master Coins = €1 (Euro). The Master Coin rate is pegged to the euro and may
vary against other currencies, depending on the exchange rate of these currencies against the euro
Coins are transferred between players and can be listed for purchase or sale in a personal store.
This allows you to use the Game Store without investing real money
Coins can be selected as a currency for transactions through personal shops and manufactures along with Adena
The project has a loyalty system — bonuses for topping up your balance and cumulative discounts in the
Personal Account store.

have access to free teleports to cities and villages.
