Basics
Pailaka is a one-time quest with a strictly limited level. To complete it, players must pass an instance.
The reward is Experience and SP points, useful items, and Vitality points.
Pay attention!
In the basic game
Pailaka — A Song of Ice and Fire
is available to characters of level 36 ~ 42
On MasterWork this quest is available to players level 35 ~ 45
Quest guide
Talk to NPC
Inspector Adler
Pailaka - Song of Ice and Fire (Level 35-45)
near the temple in Gludin to start the quest.
He will move the character to an instance — copy of the Forgotten Temple.
You have 60 minutes to complete the instance.
