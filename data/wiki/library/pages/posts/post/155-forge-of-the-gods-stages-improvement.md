The volcanic region in the northeastern part of Goddard is known as the gods' cauldron. It's said that rivers
of lava flow there! I don't think it's a place you'd want to spend your vacation!
Basics
Back in 2018, the MasterWork team created, and now successfully using a phase-based
system for the Forge of the Gods. To open new stages of the location, players need
to perform certain actions.
