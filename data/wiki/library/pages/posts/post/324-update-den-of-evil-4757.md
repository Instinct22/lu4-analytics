Update: Den of Evil (47~57)
Update: Den of Evil (47~57)
Locations
06.05.2025
After their defeat in the Great War, the orcs made peace with the elves and humans, retreating to the
Immortal Plateau. However, the Ragna orcs refused to accept this humiliating peace and remained in the city to
fight the elven army. After crushing the remnants of the orc army, the elves ruined the town and slaughtered
the surviving orcs. Only a small group of survivors escaped. The remaining Ragna orcs eventually returned to
the ruins and settled there.
It was to these ruins that a shaman named Varangka was banished. Obsessed with the idea of conquering all the
orcs, he sought the power of the spirit of Kasha. Varangka used Kasha's power to summon monsters and began
preparing for an attack on the Plateau of Immortality. Since then, the ruins where Varangka took refuge have
become known as the Lair of Evil.

Basics
The MasterWork team continues to rework locations to diversify your gaming experience.
The Den of Evil location in the lands of Stuttgart has received a quality update - there is now a division into
medium and high-difficulty spots, new unique mechanics, and the strengthened raid boss
Dark Shaman Varangka
Cursed Chieftain
Lv. 66
.
The location is designed for different party compositions — single players and small groups to full groups with
good equipment. Monsters received increased stats and learned to use skills during combat.
The battle with the raid boss is non-trivial. You will have to comprehend mechanics and skills to win.
We wish players to study everything independently. A detailed description of the boss battle will appear after
the content release.
Unique mechanics
Elite Monsters
In the 5th MasterWork season, we added a gradation of monsters by difficulty into the game:
Common monster
Ordinary monster type.
Elite Monster
Have advanced intelligence and skill set, both attacking and strengthening surrounding monsters.
They have increased attack and defence stats, resistance to various debuffs, and invulnerability
to lethal attacks.
Unique Monster
It's a stronger version of an Elite monster. Have a unique name and can be rarely found in locations.
Raid Boss
An extremely strong monster, it is immune to a number of control abilities.
Reworked locations in the lands of Schuttgart populated by Elite monsters and designed for groups of players of
any composition and quality of equipment. A large amount of experience for killing, valuable drop/spoil and
increased experience rates in a group create excellent conditions for gathering random groups to farm in the Den ov Evil.
Beacon Tower Manager Torrant
Torrant
Beacon Tower Manager
watches over the Ragna orcs and is ready to help adventurers who dare to fight the orcs
and shaman Varangka creatures.
Torrant sells special items that will help you in battles on the Den of Evil domain.

Amulet of Kasha
Amulet of Kasha is a powerful artefact that allows you to strengthen the demons summoned by Varangka.
Temporary
Amulet of Kasha
NG
can be purchased from NPC
Torrant
Beacon Tower Manager
.
The Eternal Amulet can be obtained by hunting "Cruel Vanul" monsters.
Target "Cruel Vanul" and use the Amulet to apply the
Seal of Kasha
Lv. 1
effect to the monster.
Under the effect, the monster will receive:
Full restoration of health
Increase physical/magic attack and defense by 15%
Increase speed by 15%
Increase EXP/SP for killing a monster by 15%
After the death of the enhanced monster, 3 to 6 ghosts will appear.:
Vengeful Spirit

Kharun
