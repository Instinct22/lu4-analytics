New: Fairy Settlement (78~85)
New: Fairy Settlement (78~85)
Locations
05.01.2026
Kimerian, who was at odds with Fairy King Obelius, took his followers to Magmeld and lived peacefully in
the beautiful land until present. However, the Fairy Settlement has been contaminated by the corruption
of the Decaying Darkness, and the Fairies that have fallen sway to the Seed of Darkness are on the rampage.
Basics
Fairy Settlement — innovation from MasterWork team. Location consists of 3 zones with different difficulty,
intended for exping and farming for solo players, mini-groups and full parties.
As the reward for hunting at Fairy Settlement you can get:
Resources, SP scrolls, attribute stones, S gems
Various codex for skill enchant
Enchant scrolls, including Blessed
Recipes, parts and whole S-grade items
To reach this location ask
Liri
Fairy Settlement Guide
located on the left from Gatekeeper
in Arcan city.
Everywhere at hunting zone large and small spheres, that restores HP and MP spawning.
