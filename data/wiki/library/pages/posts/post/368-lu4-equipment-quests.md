Basics
Equipment — a crucial part of your character's strength. Getting a full piece of equipment,
just like recipes or parts, from a monster or raid boss is very rare. If relying on a lucky drop/spoil isn't your
path, and you are willing to get a specific item — quests can help you acquire the necessary recipes, parts,
and even full pieces of equipment. However, be aware that in some quests the reward is random,
and you may need to complete them multiple times.
In the world of, there are many ways to obtain equipment. We have gathered all the quests
in one place to save you time.
