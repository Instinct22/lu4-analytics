Basics
One of the innovations of the MasterWork 2023 season was the rework of “Surrender to..." skills,
which reduce resistance against a specific attribute. Now "surrender" has a chance to cancel the
resistance buff of the corresponding attribute.
All 6 types received a 15% chance to cancel the effect of elemental resistance from the target.
The action is similar to the
Crushing Strike
Lv. 1
,
that cancels
Resist Shock
Lv. 1
and
Improved Resist
Lv. 1
.
This works in the early stages (Interlude - Gracia) and the later chronicles (High Five - MW Eternal).
Skill
Crushing Strike
Lv. 1
has also been redesigned:
Cancels the effect of
Resist Shock
Lv. 1
with a 35% chance (from 50%)
Cancels the effect of
Improved Resist
Lv. 1
with a 25% chance (from 35%)
