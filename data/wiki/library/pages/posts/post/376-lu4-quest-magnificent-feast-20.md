Quest guide
1. The starting NPC
Ranspo
Warehouse Chief
is located in Dion's Warehouse.
Talk to Ranspo to start the quest.
2. Head to the northwest wall of the city. Before going up to the Temple, find
Harlan
Guard
and talk to him.
Harlan will start the quest
Fantasy Wine
.
Complete the quest to receive
60 Year Old Wine
NG
.
3. Head to the east gate, opposite the Grocery Store. Find
Barbado
Musician
and talk to him.
Barbado will start the quest
Jovial Accordion
.
Complete the quest to receive
Musical Score - Theme of the Feast
NG
.
4. Head to the Central Square. Find
Swan
Bard
and talk to him.
Swan will start the quest
Bard's Mandolin
.
Complete the quest to receive
Musical Score - Theme of Journey
NG
.
5. Head west of the city. Past the Dark Elf Guild find
Nanarin
Musician
and talk to her.
Nanarin will start the quest
Sorrowful Sound of Flute
.
Complete the quest to receive
Musical Score - Theme of Solitude
NG
.
6. Head to the Warehouse. Find
Jonas
and talk to him.
Jonas will start the quest
Adept of Taste
.
Complete the quest to receive
Jonas's Sauce Recipe
NG
.
7. Head to the Temple. Find
Rollant
Magister
and talk to him.
Rollant will start the quest
Bring Out the Flavor of Ingredients!
.
Complete the quest to receive
Ritron's Dessert Recipe
NG
.
8. Complete all tasks and head to the Warehouse. Find
Ranspo
Warehouse Chief
and talk to him.
To complete the quest you will need the following items:
60 Year Old Wine
NG
Musical Score - Theme of the Feast
NG
Musical Score - Theme of Journey
NG
Musical Score - Theme of Solitude
NG
Jonas's Sauce Recipe
NG
Ritron's Dessert Recipe
NG
Ranspo will complete the quest and reward you with a random D-rank piece of jewelry.
