Overlords are born, not made. to become an Overlord who also holds the position of Chief, the candidate
must pass the Test of the Lord. The test to determine if you are worthy to become an Overlord has begun!
Pay Attention!
1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ
from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.
2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously.
Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.
3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests,
and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:
Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as
