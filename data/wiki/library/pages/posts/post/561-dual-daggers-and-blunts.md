The E-GLobal project presents a unique addition for Lu4 — dual daggers and dual blunts.
Starting at level 20, all players can create and equip dual weapons. Special passive skills are not needed,
all game classes can equip new duals.

Daggers VS Dual Daggers
Daggers and dual daggers are weapons for different purposes. Dual daggers are less diverse, but have some
advantage over one-handed weapons. What are the advantages of each type of daggers?
One-handed daggers provide a rich selection of SA effects to choose from
the most effective for PvP
options.
For example, a one-handed dagger with SA Critical Damage or Mortal Blow is the perfect
choice for the young assassin.
On the other hand, dual daggers give
more damage in the distance and have an advantage in PvE battles.
In addition, they have more damage from modification than common daggers. On a farm or RB killing
it is better to take duals.
The base attack speed of dual daggers has been reduced from 433 to 400.
