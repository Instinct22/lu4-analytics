Beleth on MasterWork
NPC
13.11.2025
The most formidable mage and malevolent demon ever to walk the earth. Empowered by Hellbound, he has ascended
to a level of power so unfathomable that even the gods themselves dare not comprehend it. Recently, Beleth has
begun delving into forbidden experiments — melding Chaos with the essence of humanity. But something has shifted.
Hellbound trembles under the weight of his actions, and the balance of power teeters on the brink. None can predict his next move.
Basics
Beleth is a majestic Epic Boss introduced to the game with the Hellbound update.
Beleth's abode is located on the Hellbound.
Players who defeat the Boss will receive an Epic Jewelry reward — Beleth's Ring.
In the basic game, Beleth's Throne Room is on the top level of the Steel Citadel.
To enter the Throne Room, players must complete all levels of the Steel Citadel.
The Steel Citadel location is unavailable on MasterWork.
The entrance to Beleth's Throne Room has been moved to the Sand Swept Dunes
location and is marked on the map as Mystical Device.
The battle with Beleth is not available to MidCompany members.

Beleth will appear 5 minutes after the first player enters the Throne Room.
The fight with Beleth has four stages.
Depending on the players' actions in Stage 1, Stage 2 can be skipped, or the entire fight can be reset.
Beleth's Throne Room is a NoPK zone.
This means that a player can kill another player without affecting their reputation.
Stage 1
Leona Blackbird
Fire Dragon Bride
Lv. 82
will appear in the center of the hall.
At the same time, three illusions of Beleth will appear at the foot of the throne.
Attack one of Beleth's incarnations.
The stage will end when one of the copies reach
50% HP
.
Each of the illusions triggers one of three scenarios:
Stage 2 Activation
Stage 3 Activation —
Best Option
End of the Battle —
Worst Option
Each illusion follows its own scenario. Only the illusion with the worst scenario can be identified,
and Leona will target it.
If you assisted Leona and killed the illusion with the worst scenario — the Throne Room will close,
and all players inside the room will be teleported to the Continent.
To successfully complete the raid, you must re-enter the Throne Room.
