06.05.2025
...after escaping with the stolen blueprints, Doctor Chaos made his way to the ancient laboratory of the

Basics
The MasterWork team continues to rework locations to diversify your gaming experience.
The Archaic Laboratory in the lands of Schuttgart has undergone a quality update,
introducing unique mechanics and a complete overhaul of the raid boss
Doctor Chaos
Kaeldor
Lv. 74
battle.
The location is designed to accommodate various party compositions. The monsters outside are suited for solo
players, while smaller rooms cater to smaller or incomplete groups. The main halls of the laboratory are
intended for full parties. Monsters have enhanced stats and have learned to use skills during combat.
The battle with the raid boss is non-trivial. You will have to comprehend mechanics and skills to win.
We wish players to study everything independently. A detailed description of the boss battle will appear after
the content release.
Unique mechanics
Elite monsters
In the 5th MasterWork season, we added a gradation of monsters by difficulty into the game:
Common monster
Ordinary monster type.
Elite Monster
Have advanced intelligence and skill set, both attacking and strengthening surrounding monsters.
They have increased attack and defence stats, resistance to various debuffs, and invulnerability
to lethal attacks.
Unique Monster
It's a stronger version of an Elite monster. Have a unique name and can be rarely found in locations.
Raid Boss
An extremely strong monster, it is immune to a number of control abilities.
Reworked locations in the lands of Schuttgart populated by Elite monsters and designed for groups of players of
any composition and quality of equipment. A large amount of experience for killing, valuable drop/spoil and
increased experience rates in a group create excellent conditions for gathering random groups to farm in the Archaic Laboratory.

Chaos Transmuter
A new device has been installed in the three large halls of the Ancient Laboratory -
Chaos Transmuter
.
Killing monsters in the hall increases the Transmuter's charge level, the progress is displayed in the device's
title and during dialogue with it. The number of monsters required for charging may change during the season.
A fully charged Transmuter allows you to activate different modes that change the type of monsters
in the room:
Mode: Heavy Module Activation — greatly increases the strength of monsters in the hall and increases the loot value
Mode: Reserve Activation — significantly increases the number of monsters in the hall
In addition to the standard Modes, "pirate" Protocols are available for activation. Only two can be activated
simultaneously - the Resonance for strengthening characters and one of the two Protocols that weaken monsters.
System Resonance
Lv. 1
—
The operation of an experimental circuit, enhancing objects within the area of effect.
Protocol: Disintegration
Lv. 1
—
An experimental protocol by Pavel designed to weaken defensive systems.
Protocol: Weakening
Lv. 1
—
An experimental protocol by Pavel designed to lower the combat potential of entities.
You will need specific items to activate the protocols, which can be obtained by hunting monsters in the
Laboratory halls or purchased from NPC
Gutenhagen
Collector
:
Pavel's Power Cell
NG
Broken Rotor
NG
Pay attention!
Special Modes are only active while the Transmuter remains charged. Once the charge level reaches zero,
all Modes and Protocols will be deactivated.
To reactivate special Modes and Protocols, you must charge the Transmuter to 100%.
