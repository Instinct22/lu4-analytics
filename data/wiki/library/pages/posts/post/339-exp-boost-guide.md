Basics
Levelling up your character is a crucial aspect of gameplay. The primary factor influencing the rate of
experience gain is the EXP rate. In MasterWork, this rate varies based on the season stage and the character's level.
In the second half of the season, we add weekend rates:
+ 50% EXP when killing Monsters
+ 100% SP when killing Monsters
You can find the current rates for your character on the forum in the latest patchnote.
In addition to the rates, some items and the Vitality level affect the levelling speed.
In this article, we will tell you how to increase the experience gained using all available game methods.
Vitality
On the MasterWork project, the Vitality system has been redesigned to ensure equal opportunities for all players.
The experience bonus from Vitality has been slightly reduced to narrow the gap in levelling speed between players
who use items to restore Vitality and those who progress without purchases. For the same reason, we have also
removed the Nevit system.

Vitality increases the experience gained from killing monsters.
It does not affect the experience gained from killing bosses or completing quests.
The experience bonus from Vitality is calculated after applying rates to the base EXP and SP
gained from killing a monster.

As a result, for killing a monster, the character will receive 58 660 + 87 990 = 146 650 EXP.

Interaction of bonuses
All available methods for increasing experience are calculated after applying rates to the base
EXP and SP earned from defeating a monster, and their effects are then combined.
An example:
Character — Level 50
Monster
Sepulcher Inquisitor
Lv. 52
— 11 732 EXP
Rates at the current stage of the season — EXP x5
Bonus from 2 Vitality level — +150% EXP
Rune bonus — +50% EXP
Bonus from Achievement effect — +30% EXP
How much EXP a character with the Achievement effect receive for killing a monster:
Rates — 11 732 * 5 = 58 660 EXP
Vitality bonus — 58 660 * 1,5 = 87 990 EXP
Rune bonus — 58 660 * 0,5 = 29 330 EXP
Bonus from Achievement effect — 58 660 * 0,3 = 17 598 EXP
As a result, for killing the monster the character will receive 58 660 + 87 990 + 29 330 +
17 598 = 193 578 EXP.
Based on the examples given, it is essential to retain two key points:
All experience bonuses are summed up after applying rates, not multiplied
Bonuses from Runes and Vitality are also combined but calculated separately.
Runes are applied to the base experience with the rate, excluding Vitality,
while Vitality is calculated independently of Runes.
Any experience bonuses are personal.
They apply only to the experience your character gains and are not shared with the group.
Premium, Vitality, Runes and Daily Online Reward are exclusively your personal boosts.
