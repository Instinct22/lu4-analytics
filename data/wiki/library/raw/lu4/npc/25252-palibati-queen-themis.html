Raid Boss Lv. 1
The Queen of the Palibati protects the Seal of Shilen. According to Shilen's Priest Hisilrome, who is also a Palibati, Themis is a living piece of the Queen of Evil Spirits, Shilen. Themis is one of the monsters birthed by Shilen during the War of the Gods, and as a result her authority nearly equals that of the Death Lords. Capable of summoning and ruling over both dead and devils, she employs the low-Lv. devil, Succubus, as her maid.
