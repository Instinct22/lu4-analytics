Level 80 Skills: "Symbols"

Skills 10.12.2025




Basic information

At level 80, characters in almost every profession receive a unique skill, which players named the "dome" for the characteristic animation. The distinctive feature of these skills is the mass superimposition of positive and negative effects or the attribute damage to the area. Using skills requires special items - Battle Symbol and Magic Symbol

Players often underestimate these skills and rarely use them in the game. However, the timely used "dome" can change the course of the battle, because many positive skills work not only on members of the group, but also on the clan with the alliance. For example, the ability of tanks significantly increases all damage and debacle defences by 90 seconds for all friendly targets. A full 1.5 minutes can defend a bottleneck with a significant advantage!

Don’t put aside valuable skills for the right moment. Use them when they can help you win. Even in instance, even during the exping on the spot, and especially during the battle for this spot - the dome is a big bonus to win!
