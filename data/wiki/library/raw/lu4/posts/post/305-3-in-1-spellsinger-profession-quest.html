3 in 1 Spellsinger profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Spellsinger:

  • Quest Trial of the Scholar
  • Quest Testimony of Life
  • Quest Test of Magus

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Spellsinger profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Scholar NG
  • Mark of Life NG
  • Mark of Magus NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Dion. Find Rukal Bard in the Temple and talk to him.

Rukal will give Rukal's Letter NG

2. Talk to two NPCs in the Temple of Dion in a sequence.

2.1. Talk to Mirien Magister . She will give Mirien's 1st Sigil NG

2.2. Talk to Sylvain High Priest , he will give:

High Priest's Sigil NG
Sylvain's Letter NG

3. Head to the Dion Warrior Guild. Find Cardien Master and talk to her.

Cardien will give Cardien's Letter NG

4. Head to Dion's Grocery Store. Find Maria there and talk to her.

Maria will give Maria's 1st Letter NG

5. Head to the southern gate of Dion. Find Lucas Captain there and talk to him.

Lucas will give Lucas's Letter NG

At MasterWork, you can talk to Lucas in the town center.

6. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Maria's 2nd Letter NG

7. Leave Dion Town through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village (Club Card holders can teleport to the village). Find Creta Astrologer behind the village Warehouse and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's 1st Letter NG

8. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Lucilla's Handbag NG

9. Return to Creta Astrologer and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's Painting1 NG

10. Go back to Maria again and talk to her.

Maria will give Creta's Painting2 NG

11. Head to the southern gate of Dion. Find Lucas Captain there and talk to him.

Lucas will give Creta's Painting3 NG

12. Go back to Maria again and talk to her.

13. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Parina in the Temple and talk to her.

Parina will give Parina's Letter NG

14. Teleport from Gludin Village to Wasteland. Fo north, find Casian Elder among the cliffs and talk to him.

Casian will give Lilac Charm NG

15. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give:

Hierarch's Letter NG
Moonflower Charm NG

16. Teleport from Elven Village to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Grail Diagram NG

17. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38

Until you collect 5 Brown Scroll Scrap NG

18. Teleport to Oren, then to the Sea of Spores. Instead of going inside, head northeast. On the river bank, near the waterfall, find and kill the monster:

Singing Flower of Nightmare Quest Monster Lv. 44

From the killed monster you get 2nd Golden Seed NG

19. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Crystal of Purity NG

20.1. Head to the Temple of Dion. Find Sylvain High Priest and talk to him.

Sylvain will give Symbol of Sylvain NG

21.2. Talk to Mirien Magister in the same Temple.

Mirien will give Mirien's 2nd Sigil NG

22. Teleport to Dion, then to Tanor Canyon, and head east toward the river. Before reaching the shore, find and kill the monster:

Singing Flower of Darkness Quest Monster Lv. 44

From the killed monster you will receive 3rd Golden Seed NG

23. Teleport to Giran. Find Pushkin Master Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Pushkin will give Pushkin's List NG

On MasterWork, you can talk to Pushkin in the Central Square of Giran.

24. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Jurek Grand Magister and talk to him.

Jurek will give:

Grand Magister's Sigil NG
Jurek's List NG

25. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34

Until you collect 5 Shaman's Necklace NG

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

You can reach the next location on foot.

26. Teleport to Giran, then to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36
Wyrm Lv. 35

Until you collect:

Pure Mithril Ore NG x 10
Wyrm's Talon NG x 20

27. Head to the Dragon Valley. Near the entrance find and kill monsters:

Shackle Lv. 45

Until you collect 2 Shackle's Scalp NG

28. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Guard Lv. 35

Until you collect 20 Ant Soldier Acid NG

Quest item obtained from all ant monsters inside the Ant Nest.

29. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins Of Agony. Head west towards Fellmere Lake. On the island in the center of the lake, find and kill the monster:

Singing Flower of Phantasm Quest Monster Lv. 44

From the killed monster you will receive 1st Golden Seed NG

30. Teleport to Dion. Find Rukal Bard in the Temple and talk to him.

Rukal will give Score of Elements NG

31. Teleport from Dion to the Plains of Dion. Find and kill monsters:

Monster Eye Destroyer Lv. 26

Until you collect 5 Monster Eye Destroyer's Skin NG

32. Teleport to Giran. Find Pushkin Master Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Pushkin will give Pure Mithril Cup NG

33. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Jurek Grand Magister and talk to him.

Jurek will give Symbol of Jurek NG

34. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mirien's 3rd Sigil NG

35. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Cronos Elder in the main square and talk to him.

Cronos will give:

Crono's Sigil NG
Cronos Letter NG

36. Teleport to Giran. Find Dieter Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Dieter will give Dieter's Key NG

37. Teleport to Dion, exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Creta Astrologer behind the Village Warehouse and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's 2nd Letter NG

38. Return to Dieter Magister and talk to him.

Dieter will give:

Dieter's Letter NG
Dieter's Diary NG

39. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Edroc Armor Merchant in the Weapon Shop and talk to him.

Edroc will give Raut's Letter Envelope NG

40. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins Of Agony. Head deep into the location. Near the northern gate of the ruined village find Earth Snake Wise and talk to her.

Earth Snake will give Serpent Charm NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

41. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ruins Of Agony. Head to Fellmere Lake. Find Water Undine on the northern shore of the lake and talk to her.

Water Undine will give Undine Charm NG

42. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins of Despair. Head deep into the location. In the southwest of the ruined village find Flame Salamander and talk to her.

Flame Salamander will give Salamander Charm NG

43. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Instead of into the cave head to the northwest edge of the Wastelands. Go through the passage at the edge of the location. Find Wind Sylph and talk to her.

Wind Sylph will give Sylph Charm NG

Alternative route: Teleport to the Wastelands via the Club Teleporter. Head north over the hill.

44. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Thalia's 1st Letter NG

45. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give:

Arkenia's Contract NG
Arkenia's Instructions NG

46. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Adonius Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Adonius will give Adonius's list NG

47. Head to the Gludin Village Warehouse building. Find Raut Warehouse Keeper and talk to him.

Raut will give:

Scripture Chapter 1 NG
Strong Liquor NG

48. Head to Gludin Harbor. Find Triff Drunkard and talk to him.

Triff will give Triff's Ring NG

49. Teleport to Giran. Find Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Valkon will give Valkon's Request NG

50. Teleport to Dion. Find Maria in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Maria will give Crystal of Purity NG

51. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Around the tower, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31
Marsh Spider Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Dazzling Drop NG x 20
Spider Ichor NG x 20

52. Teleport to Giran. Find Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Valkon will give Scripture Chapter 2 NG

53. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39
Grandis Lv. 40

Until you collect:

Windsus Mane NG x 10
Scripture Chapter 3 NG

54. Teleport to Dion. Find Poitan Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Poitan will give Poitan's Notes NG

55. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. North, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Casian's List NG

56. Teleport to Giran. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Harpy Lv. 32
Wyrm Lv. 35
Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Harpy's Feather NG x 20
Harpy's Down NG x 20
Wyrm's Wingbone NG x 10
Medusa's Blood NG x 12

Harpy monsters are in the western part of the location, dragons and medusa are in the eastern part.

57. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Fettered Soul Lv. 38

Until you collect 5 Fettered Soul's Ichor NG

58. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Head east along the road to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Ghoul Lv. 32

Until you collect 10 Ghoul's Skin NG

59. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Adonius Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Adonius will give Andariel Scripture Copy NG

60. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Stardust NG

61. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Thalia's 2nd Letter NG

62. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Isael Silvershadow in the main square and talk to her.

Isael will give Isael's Instructions NG

63. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39

Until you collect:

Talin's Spear Blade NG
Talin's Spear Shaft NG
Talin's Ruby NG
Talin's Aquamarine NG
Talin's Amethyst NG
Talin's Peridot NG

64. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Instead of going inside, go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Monstereye Lv. 41
Enchanted Stone Golem Lv. 42
Enchanted Iron Golem Lv. 43
Enchanted Gargoyle Lv. 44
Ghost Fire Quest Monster Lv. 42

Until you collect:

Enchanted Monster Eye Shell NG x 10
Enchanted Golem Powder NG x 10
Enchanted Iron Golem Shard NG x 10
Enchanted Gargoyle's Nail NG x 5
Flame Crystal NG x 5

65. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Isael Silvershadow in the main square and talk to her.

Isael will give:

Isael's Letter NG
Talins Spear NG

66. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins Of Agony. Head deep into the location. Near the northern gate of the ruined village find Earth Snake Wise and talk to her.

Earth Snake will give Tone of Earth NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

67. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ruins Of Agony. Head to Fellmere Lake. Find Water Undine on the northern shore of the lake and talk to her.

Water Undine will give Tone of Water NG

68. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins of Despair. Head deep into the location. In the southwest of the ruined village find Flame Salamander and talk to her.

Flame Salamander will give Tone of Fire NG

69. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Instead of going into the cave head to the northwest edge of the Wastelands. Go through the passage at the edge of the location. Find Wind Sylph and talk to her.

Wind Sylph will give Tone of Wind NG

Alternative route: Teleport to the Wastelands via the Club Teleporter. Head north over the hill.

70. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Go north. Among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Scripture Chapter 4 NG

71. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Grail of Purity NG

72. Head to the foot of the mountains south of Iris Lake. Find and kill the monster:

Unicorn Of Eva Quest Monster Lv. 30

Kill the unicorn by Talins Spear NG

From the dead unicorn get Tears of Unicorn NG

The nearest unicorn is located southeast of the lake.

73. Return to Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Water of Life NG

74. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Camomile Charm NG

75. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Cronos Elder in the main square and talk to him.

Cronos will give Symbol of Cronos NG

76. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mark of Scholar NG

77. In the Temple of Dion find Rukal Bard and talk to him.

Rukal will give Mark of Magus NG

78. Head to the Dion Warrior Guild. Find Cardien Master and talk to her.

Cardien will give Mark of Life NG

79. To change your profession, talk to Jurek Grand Magister in the Giran Mages Guild.
