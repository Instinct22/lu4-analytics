Update: Crypts of Disgrace (32~36)

Locations 25.09.2025

...the tribes that disobeyed orders and were exiled from the orc kingdom could not be buried on the Immortal Plateau. They built a giant tomb to bury there. But deprived of the honour of resting in the land of their ancestors, the dead of the exiled orcs often rose as undead. That happened also due to the grave robbers' invasion. The noble orcs called that place the Crypts of Disgrace.

Read the full story


Basics

The MasterWork team continues to rework locations to diversify your gaming experience.

The location was reworked and occupied by Elite Monsters levels 33-35 and is designed for group levelling.

Monsters have high health, damage and advanced protection against most control skills. Harsh farm conditions compensate by generous drop, spoil and experience.

Elite Monsters

In the 5th MasterWork season, we added a gradation of monsters by difficulty into the game:

Common monster Ordinary monster type.
Elite Monster Have advanced intelligence and skill set, both attacking and strengthening surrounding monsters. They have increased attack and defence stats, resistance to various debuffs, and invulnerability to lethal attacks.
Unique Monster It's a stronger version of an Elite monster. Have a unique name and can be rarely found in locations.
Raid Boss An extremely strong monster, it is immune to a number of control abilities.

Reworked locations in the lands of Schuttgart populated by Elite monsters and designed for groups of players of any composition and quality of equipment. A large amount of experience for killing, valuable drop/spoil and increased experience rates in a group create excellent conditions for gathering random groups to farm in the Crypts of Disgrace.
