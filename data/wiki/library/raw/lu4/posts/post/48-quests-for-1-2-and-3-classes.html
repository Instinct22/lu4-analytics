Quests for 1, 2 and 3 classes

Quests to change class 29.06.2026

Basic

Completing profession quests is now more rewarding! The Masterwork team has significantly increased the rewards to make the time spent on these quests worthwhile. In addition to the standard EXP, SP, and Diamonds, you will also receive the following bonus rewards for each completed profession quest:

Reward Contains
First Class transfer Reward NG Foundation
  • Ivory Coin NG Foundation (35 - 50)
  • Personal Healing Potion NG (35 - 50)
  • Personal Greater Healing Potion NG Foundation (25 - 35)
  • Personal Greater CP Potion NG Foundation (15 - 25)
  • Sweet Fruit Cocktail Physical Buffs NG (2)
  • Fresh Fruit Cocktail Magical Buffs NG (2)
  • Vitality Maintaining Potion NG (1)
  • Vitality Replenishing Potion NG (1)
  • Lucky Vitality Clover for 1-52 Lv. NG (1)
  • Personal Scroll of Escape NG (5 - 10)
  • Personal Blessed Scroll of Resurrection NG (2 - 3)
  • Soulshot: No Grade for Beginners NG (2000 - 2500)
  • Spiritshot: No Grade for Beginners NG (800 - 1000)
  • Soulshot (D-Grade) D (2000 - 2500)
  • Blessed Spiritshot (D-Grade) D (800 - 1000)
Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation
  • Ivory Coin NG Foundation (50 - 75)
  • Personal Greater Healing Potion NG Foundation (35 - 50)
  • Personal Greater CP Potion NG Foundation (25 - 35)
  • Sweet Fruit Cocktail Physical Buffs NG (1)
  • Fresh Fruit Cocktail Magical Buffs NG (1)
  • Vitality Maintaining Potion NG (1)
  • Vitality Replenishing Potion NG (1)
  • Personal Scroll of Escape NG (5 - 10)
  • Personal Blessed Scroll of Resurrection NG (2 - 3)
  • Soulshot (D-Grade) D (2000 - 2500)
  • Blessed Spiritshot (D-Grade) D (800 - 1000)
  • Soulshot (C-Grade) C (2000 - 2500)
  • Blessed Spiritshot (C-Grade) C (800 - 1000)

Pay attention!

Additional rewards are available only on MasterWork.

All reward items are personal. They cannot be traded, dropped, destroyed, or stored in the warehouse.
