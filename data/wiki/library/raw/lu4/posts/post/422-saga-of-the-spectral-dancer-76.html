Saga of the Spectral Dancer 76+

Quests to change class 08.12.2025

Pay attention!

To complete the quest, you’ll need extra quest items from side quests. We recommend completing those side quests beforehand and gathering the required items in advance:

  • Ice Crystal NG
    • The Finest Ingredients - pt. 1 72+
  • Divine Stone of Wisdom NG
    • Alliance with the Ketra Orcs lv. 2 72+ → Quest Magical Power of Water - pt. 1 74+
    • Alliance with Varka Silenos lv. 2 72+ → Quest Magical Power of Fire - pt. 1 74+

The quests needed to obtain the additional items available at character levels 72 and 74, respectively.

In the later stages of the server, additional items are available in MasterWork Game Store.

Quest guide

1. Starting NPC Mordred Dark Knight is located in the Hardin's Academy dungeon. Talk to Mordred to begin the quest.

Mordred will give Guild Report NG

2. Teleport to Goddard, find Elena Spellbook Seller in the Temple and talk to her.

3. Teleport from Goddard to Hot Springs. Head southeast along the road, enter the central stone arch, find Bavarin Information Broker in the red spring, and speak with him.

Give Bavarin the quest item:

Ice Crystal NG

Bavarin will give Ancient Language Dictionary NG

Ice Crystal NG — reward for completing The Finest Ingredients - pt. 1 72+.

4. Return to Elena Spellbook Seller and talk to her.

Elena will give Resonance Amulet - 1 NG

5. Teleport to Hunter's Village, then to the north of Enchanted Valley (Northern Region). Head east along the river, find the Tablet of Vision behind the trees at the end of the river and examine it.

6.1. Teleport to Rune, then to the Valley of Saints. Follow the road north to the western part of the area, descend into the gorge immediately after the first bridge, and find and kill the following monsters:

Guardian of Forbidden Knowledge Quest Monster Lv. 76

Until you get Resonance Amulet - 2 NG

6.2. Inspect Tablet of Vision

7.1. Teleport to Goddard, then to Ketra Orc Outpost. Follow the road east, around Borderland Fortress. Find Tablet of Vision and examine it.

A quest monster will appear:

Hallate's Dancer Lillian Quest Monster Lv. 76

7.2. Kill the monster and get Resonance Amulet - 3 NG

7.3. Examine the Tablet of Vision again.

11. Teleport to Goddard, find Elena Spellbook Seller in the Temple and talk to her.

Give Elena the quest item:

Divine Stone of Wisdom NG

Elena will give Resonance Amulet - 4 NG

Divine Stone of Wisdom NG — reward for completing Magical Power of Water - pt. 1 74+ (Alliance with Ketra Orcs) or Magical Power of Fire - pt. 1 74+ (Alliance with Varka Silenos)

9. Teleport from Goddard to Ketra Orc Outpost. Head west along the road and pass through the wooden gate. At the fork, turn south toward Varka Silenos Barracks. Before reaching Varka Outpost, at the foot of the mountains, find Tablet of Vision and examine it.

10. Return to Elena Spellbook Seller and talk to her.

11.1. Teleport from Goddard to the Wall of Argos. Head northeast to the Shrine of Loyalty. (Club Card holders can teleport to this location). Find and kill monsters:

Grave Ant Lv. 74
Grave Scarab Lv. 73
Scavenger Ant Lv. 74
Scavenger Scarab Lv. 73
Shrine Knight Lv. 75
Shrine Guard Lv. 75

Until you get Halisha's Mark NG x 700

The 701st Halisha's Mark will summon a quest monster:

Archon of Halisha Quest Monster Lv. 76

11.2. Kill the quest monster and get Resonance Amulet - 5 NG

To get the Amulet, you must last-hit the Archon. Summoning the Archon costs 20 Marks of Halisha. If you fail to kill the monster, collect some more Marks and summon it again.

There's an alternative way to complete this stage of the quest that's suitable for a full group:

In the Four Sepulchers instance zone kill Archon of Halisha Lv. 81 and get Resonance Amulet - 5 NG

This method is recommended only for experienced players.

12. Teleport to Goddard, then to the Wall of Argos. Head northwest of the location, find Tablet of Vision and examine it.

13.1. Teleport to Rune, then to Forest of the Dead. Head to the northwest of the location, climb up to the cliff, find Tablet of Vision and examine it.

After inspection, near the Tablet will appear:

Scryde Heartseeker
Death Lord Hallate Quest Monster Lv. 81

13.2. Attack the Death Lord until he disappears, then talk to Scryde.

Scryde will give Resonance Amulet - 6 NG

13.3. Examine the Tablet of Vision again.

14. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy, go down to the dungeon, find Mordred Dark Knight and talk to him.

Mordred will complete the quest and give a reward.

Completing the quest will change the character's profession.

Congratulations, you are now a Spectral Dancer, use your knowledge wisely!
