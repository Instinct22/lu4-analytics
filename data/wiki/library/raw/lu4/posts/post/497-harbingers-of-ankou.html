NPC 20.07.2026

Basics

The Harbingers of Ankou — a new mini-event developed specifically for Lu4 and MasterWork. This event is connected to the Location Keeper Ankou and serves as a story prequel to the appearance of the already familiar mini-boss.

The Harbingers are non-aggressive elite monsters that appear in various locations. They are not the primary target, but by fighting them, you can obtain unique items.

Pay attention!

The mini-boss Locarion Keeper Ankou is not available on Lu4.

Liannel Sendariel

Liannel Sendariel — a researcher of the Sky Library Guild. She will tell you about the event’s story and the rewards you can receive for defeating the Harbingers.

Liannel stopped at a farm between Gludio and Gludin. To find her, teleport to the Ruins of Agony and follow the road south. The farm is on the left, behind the nearest hill.

Where to find?

The Harbingers can be found in many locations ranging from level 20 to 65. The Harbinger’s level depends on the level of the monsters in that area.

Harbingers have several spawn points within each location, and they occasionally teleport between them.

Rewards

For defeating a Harbinger, a random participant in the battle may receive a special item — a relic shard. The type of shard depends on the defeated monster.
