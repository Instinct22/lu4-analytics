3 in 1 Destroyer profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Destroyer:

  • Quest Trial of the Challenger
  • Quest Testimony of Glory
  • Quest Test of the Champion

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Destroyer profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Challenger NG
  • Mark of Glory NG
  • Mark of Champion NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Vokian will give Vokian's Order NG

2. Head to the Giran Weapon Shop. Find Ascalon Veteran near the shop and talk to him.

Ascalon will give Ascalon's 1st Letter NG

3. Teleport to Dion. Find Mason behind the Warehouse and talk to him.

Mason will give Iron Rose Ring NG

4. Head to the Dion Warrior Guild. Find Kash behind the building and talk to him.

5. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Instead going inside the cave, head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monster:

Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Tyrant Talon NG

6. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Bloody Swampland. Head southeast to the waterfall. Find the entrance to the cave and go inside. The entrance to the cave is behind the waterfall near the lake. Find and kill the monster:

Shyslassys Quest Monster Lv. 36

From the killed monster you will receive:

Broken Key NG
Scroll of Shyslassys NG

The key is required to open the chest that appears nearby. On MasterWork there is some Adena inside the chest.

7. Teleport to Dion. Behind the Warriors Guild find Kash and talk to him.

Kash will give Letter of Kash NG

8. Teleport to Giran, then to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monster:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Fang NG

9. Head to the northeastern gate of Giran. Find Martien and talk to him.

10. Teleport to Dion, then to the Plains of Dion. Head south, find the entrance to the cave and go inside. Find and kill the monster:

Gorr Quest Monster Lv. 36

From the killed monster you will receive Watcher's 1st Eye NG

11. Return to Martien and talk to him.

12. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. Find and kill monster:

Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Manashen Shard NG

13. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Head south towards the lake. Near Antharas Fortress find and kill monster:

Bloody Axe Elite Lv. 35

Until you collect 10 Bloody Axe Head NG

14. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Vokian will give:

Vokian's Order NG
Necklace of Authority NG

15. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 1st Order NG

16. Head to the Dion Warehouse. Behind the building find Mason and talk to him.

Mason will give Mason's Letter NG

17.1. Teleport to Dion, then to Bee Hive. Head west to Hive Fortress. Find the cave entrance and go inside. The cave entrance is opposite the eastern wall of the fortress. Find and kill the monster:

Baraham Quest Monster Lv. 36

From the killed monster you will receive Watcher's 2nd Eye NG

17.2. After killing the monster, Raldo Champion will appear nearby. Talk to him.

18. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Manakia about all the types of orcs, he will give:

Manakia's 1st Letter NG
Manakia's 2nd Letter NG

19. Head to the Giran Weapon Shop. Find Ascalon Veteran near the shop and talk to him.

Ascalon will give Ascalon's 2nd Letter NG

20. Head to Giran Magic Shop. Find Groot Dye Merchant and talk to him.

Groot will give White Rose Insignia NG

21. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Head to the top of the northwestern hill. Find Voltar Breka Chief and talk to him.

Voltar will give Gloves of Voltar NG . After the conversation, quest monsters will appear:

Pashika Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37
Vultus Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the orcs and get:

Pashika's Head NG
Vultus' Head NG

Talk to Voltar again. He will give Scepter of Breka NG

22. Exit Giran City through the eastern gate and cross the bridge. Head northeast along the road to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monster:

Harpy Lv. 32
Harpy Matriarch Quest Monster Lv. 34
Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Harpy's Egg NG x 30
Medusa's Venom NG x 30

23. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monster:

Windsus Lv. 39

Until you collect 30 Windsus Bile NG

24. Teleport to Giran. Find Groot Dye Merchant in the Magic Shop and talk to him.

Groot will give Groot's Letter NG

25. Head to the Giran Weapon Shop. Find Ascalon Veteran and talk to him.

Ascalon will give Ascalon's 3rd Letter NG

26. Teleport to Dion, then to Bee Hive. Head west along the coast towards Hive Fortress. At the entrance to the Fortress, find Kepra Enku Chief and talk to him.

Kepra will give Gloves of Kepra NG . After the conversation, 4 quest monsters will appear:

Enku Orc Overlord Quest Monster Lv. 33

Kill orcs and get 4 Enku Overlord Head NG

Talk to Kepra again, he will give Scepter of Enku NG

27. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Kasman Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Kasman about all the types of orcs. He will give:

Kasman's 1st Letter NG
Kasman's 2nd Letter NG
Kasman's 3rd Letter NG

28. Teleport from Gludin Village to Orc Barracks. Head deeper into the location. Find Burai Turek Chief and talk to him.

Burai will give Gloves of Burai NG . After the conversation, quest monsters will appear:

Makum Bugbear Thug Quest Monster Lv. 37

From killed monsters get 2 Makum Bugbear Head NG

Talk to Burai again, he will give Scepter of Turek NG

29. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Langk Lizardmen Dwellings. Head northwest along the coast. Find Harak Leunt Chief on the coast and talk to him.

Harak will give Scepter of Tunath NG

30. Teleport to Gludio. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, then swim across the river. Find Driko Vuku Chief near the ruined house and talk to him.

Driko will give Driko's Contract NG

31. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Head northeast. Find and kill monster:

Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35

Until you collect 30 Stakato Drone Husk NG

32. Return to Driko Vuku Chief and talk to him.

Driko will give Scepter of Vuku NG

33. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 3rd Order NG

34. Teleport to Oren. Find Mouen Captain at the northern gate and talk to him.

Mouen will give Mouen's 1st Order NG

35. Exit Oren through the western gate. Go down the slope. Find and kill monster:

Timak Orc Overlord Lv. 45

Until you collect 20 Timak Orc Head NG

The quest item is obtained from all Timak Orc monsters in Timak Outpost. Club Card holders may teleport to the location.

36. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the western gate and head down the road to the hillside. Find and kill monster:

Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42

Until you collect 20 Tamlin Orc Skull NG

37. Teleport to Giran, then to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monster:

Road Scavenger Lv. 37
Road Collector Quest Monster Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Road Ratman Head NG

38. Teleport to Oren. Find Mouen Captain at the northern gate and talk to him.

Mouen will give Mouen's 2nd Order NG

39. Teleport from Oren to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monster:

Leto Lizardman Lv. 35
Leto Lizardman Archer Lv. 36
Leto Lizardman Soldier Lv. 37
Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39
Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Leto Lizardman Fang NG

40. Return to Mouen Captain and talk to him.

Mouen will give Mouen's Letter NG

41. Teleport to Giran. Find Ascalon Veteran near the Weapon Shop and talk to him.

Ascalon will give Mark of Champion NG

42. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Scepter Box NG

43. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Tanapi's Order NG

44. Teleport from Orc Village to Immortal Plateau, South. Follow the road north, turn east at the fork to the foot of the mountains. Find and kill monster:

Ragna Orc Overlord Lv. 39
Ragna Orc Seer Lv. 39

Until the quest monster appears:

Revenant of Tantos Chief Quest Monster Lv. 39

Kill the monster and get Scepter of Tantos NG

Alternate monster location: The ravine south of the entrance to the Cave Of Trials.

45. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Ritual Box NG

Head to the second floor of the Hall of Kings. Find Kakai Flame Lord and talk to him.

Kakai will give Mark of Glory NG

46. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Filaur Gray Pillar Member in the House of Elders and talk to him.

47.1. At this stage there is a difference between early and late stages of the server.

In the early stages:

Teleport to Mithril Mines. Go deep to the last room.

In the later stages:

Teleport to the Abandoned Coal Mines. Go deep to one of the pits.

There are no differences in the following points.

Find and kill monster:

Succubus Queen Quest Monster Lv. 34

47.2. Until Raldo Champion appears

Talk to Raldo, he will give Mark of Challenger NG

48. To change your profession, talk to Penatus High Prefect in the Giran Orc Guild.
