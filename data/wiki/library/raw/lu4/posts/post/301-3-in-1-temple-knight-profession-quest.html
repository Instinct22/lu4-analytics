3 in 1 Temple Knight profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Temple Knight:

  • Quest Trial of Duty
  • Quest Testimony of Life
  • Quest Test of the Healer

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Temple Knight profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Duty NG (Mark of Duty)
  • Mark of Life NG (Mark of Life)
  • Mark of Healer NG (Mark of Healer)

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Giran. Find Hannavalt Grand Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

2. Head to Giran Temple. Find the Starting NPC Bandellos Priest and talk to him.

Bandellos will give Report of Perrin NG

3. Teleport to Dion. Find Cardien Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to her.

Cardien will give Cardien's Letter NG

4. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Instead of going inside, head northeast along the coast. Find Sir Aron Tanford and talk to him.

Sir Aron will give Old Knight Sword NG

5. Head deep into the Wasteland location. Find and kill monsters:

Skeleton Raider Lv. 30
Skeleton Marauder Lv. 29

Until the quest monster appears:

Spirit of Sir Herod Quest Monster Lv. 35

Kill the ghost with Old Knight Sword NG

From the killed ghost you will receive Knight's Tear NG

Skeleton monsters can be killed with any weapon, the quest ghost monster that appears must be killed with the quest sword.

6. Return to Sir Aron Tanford and talk to him.

7. Teleport to Gludin Village. Exit the village through the north gate. Follow the road west to the north lighthouse of Gludin Harbor. Find Perrin and talk to him.

After the conversation, a quest monster will appear:

Tatoma Quest Monster Lv. 32

Kill the monster and talk to Perrin again.

8. Head south and go around the harbor. Climb up the hill to the southern lighthouse. Find Allana and talk to her.

9. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give:

Hierarch's Letter NG
Moonflower Charm NG

10. Teleport from Elven Village to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Grail Diagram NG

11.1. Teleport to Gludio. Exit the city through the western gate. Head to the houses in the northwest. Behind one of the houses find Gupu Father and talk to him.

In the dialog, select the second line:

"Pretend that you did not hear anything"

11.2. Talk to Gupu Father again. The dialogue must be done twice.

12. Teleport to Gludio. Find Sorius Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sorius will give Order of Sorius NG

13. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks and go deeper into the location. Find Daurin Hammercrush and talk to him.

After the conversation, 3 quest monsters will appear:

Leto Lizardman Leader Quest Monster Lv. 38
Leto Lizardman Agent Quest Monster Lv. 35 x 2

Kill all quest monsters and get 1st Secret Letter NG

Talk to Daurin again.

14.1. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Mysterious Dark Elf near the altar and talk to her.

Mysterious Dark Elf will summon three quest monsters:

Leto Lizardman Assassin Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill all quest monsters and get 2nd Secret Letter NG

14.2. Wait for the Mysterious Dark Elf to appear and talk to her. She will summon three quest monsters:

Leto Lizardman Sniper Quest Monster Lv. 36

Kill all quest monsters and get 3rd Secret Letter NG

14.3. Wait for the Mysterious Dark Elf to appear and talk to her. She will summon three quest monsters:

Leto Lizardman Lord Quest Monster Lv. 40
Leto Lizardman Wizard Quest Monster Lv. 39 x 2

Kill all quest monsters and get 4th Secret Letter NG

14.4. Wait for the Mysterious Dark Elf to appear and talk to her.

15. Head south along the road from the Altar of Rites. Look for the red tents between the Necropolis of Devotion and Cloud Mountain Fortress. Find Kristina Saint near the tents and talk to her.

Kristina will give Kristina's Letter NG

16. Teleport to Gludio. Find Sorius Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

17. Teleport to Dion. Find Sir Kiel Nighthawk in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

18. Exit the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Strain Lv. 31
Ghoul Lv. 32

Until you collect 10 Report Piece NG

10 report pieces will turn into Report Piece NG

19. Return to Sir Kiel Nighthawk and talk to him.

Sir Kiel will give Mirror of Orpic NG

20. Leave the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Hangman Tree Lv. 35

Until Spirit Of Sir Talianus appears

Talk to Spirit of Sir Talianus. He will give Tear of Confession NG

21. Return to Sir Kiel Nighthawk and talk to him.

22. Teleport to Giran. Find Pushkin Master Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Pushkin will give Pushkin's List NG

On MasterWork you can talk to Pushkin in the Central Square of Giran.

23. Head to the Temple of Giran, find Bandellos Priest and talk to him.

Bandellos will give Mark of Healer NG

24. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36
Wyrm Lv. 35

Until you collect:

Pure Mithril Ore NG x 10
Wyrm's Talon NG x 20

25. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Guard Lv. 35

Until you collect 20 Ant Soldier Acid NG

Quest item obtained from all ant monsters inside the Ant Nest.

26. Head to the Giran Blacksmith Shop. Find Pushkin Master Blacksmith and talk to him.

Pushkin will give Pure Mithril Cup NG

27. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Thalia's 1st Letter NG

28. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give:

Arkenia's Contract NG
Arkenia's Instructions NG

29. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Adonius Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Adonius will give Adonius's list NG

30. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Spider Lv. 34

Until you collect 20 Spider Ichor NG

31. Teleport to Giran and exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Harpy Lv. 32

Until you collect 20 Harpy's Down NG

32. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Adonius Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Adonius will give Andariel Scripture Copy NG

33. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Stardust NG

34. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Thalia's 2nd Letter NG

35. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Isael Silvershadow in the main square and talk to her.

Isael will give Isael's Instructions NG

Talk twice, you need to have a dialogue about two quests.

36. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39

Until you collect:

Talin's Spear Blade NG
Talin's Spear Shaft NG
Talin's Ruby NG
Talin's Aquamarine NG
Talin's Amethyst NG
Talin's Peridot NG
Militas Article NG x 20

Quest items are looted from all Leto Lizardman monsters in the Plains of the Lizardmen.

37. Return to Isael Silvershadow and talk to her.

Isael will give:

Tear of Loyalty NG
Isael's Letter NG
Talins Spear NG

Talk twice, you need to have a dialogue about two quests.

38. Teleport to Giran. Find Dustin Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

39. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35

Until you collect 3 quest items:

Athebaldt's Skull NG
Athebaldt's Ribs NG
Athebaldt's Shin NG

Several monsters are located to the east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

40. Return to Dustin Priest and talk to him.

Dustin will give Saints Ashes Urn NG

41. Teleport to Takling Island Village, then to the Obelisk of Victory. Find Sir Collin Windawood and talk to him.

Sir Collin will give Letter of Windawood NG

42. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Head to the southeastern shore of Iris Lake. Find Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Grail of Purity NG

43. Head to the foot of the mountains south of Iris Lake. Find and kill the monster:

Unicorn Of Eva Quest Monster Lv. 30

Kill the unicorn while holding Talins Spear NG

From the killed unicorn get Tears of Unicorn NG

The nearest unicorn is located southeast of the lake.

44. Return to Thalia and talk to her.

Thalia will give Water of Life NG

45. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Camomile Charm NG

46. Teleport to Dion. Find Cardien Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to her.

Cardien will give Mark of Life NG

47. Teleport to Giran. Find Dustin Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Dustin will give Letter of Dustin NG

48. Head to the Giran Warriors Guild. Find Hannavalt Grand Master and talk to him.

Hannavalt will give Mark of Duty NG

49. To change your profession, talk to Hannavalt Grand Master in the Giran Warriors Guild.
