Lu4: Quest Song of Hunter 30+

Useful quests 10.07.2026

Basics

The quest "Song of the Hunter" is a multi-stage repeatable quest with valuable rewards. The Lu4 developers have reworked the quest and its rewards while preserving its overall concept.

The quest includes several different activities, such as:

  • Quests to increase Hunter’s Guild membership rank
  • Adena mini-quest: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Cycle Requests
  • Adena mini-quest: Blood Crystal
  • Purchase of special Hunter’s Guild goods

Increasing your Hunter’s Guild membership rank improves the list of available activities.

Guild Level 1

The Hunters Guild has a long, romantic history, and has always been in the dreams of youths who seek adventure... Whether they choose to hunt criminals or monsters! It's definitely not an easy task to join the Hunters Guild.

Starting NPC Grey Guild Member
Starting location Hunter's Village
Level 30 ~ 65
Type Repeatable
Requirements —
Reward Hunter’s Guild Membership Lv. 1

The starting quest for joining the Hunter’s Guild. Upon completing the introductory quest, the features of a Level 1 Guild Member will become available.

Quest guide

1. Starting NPC Grey Guild Member is located at the entrance to the Central Building of Hunter's Village.

Talk to Gray to start the quest.

2. Grey will point out 5 monsters in different locations and ask you to bring any 3 sets of quest items.

You need to collect the number of quest items specified in the table from any 3 monsters of your choice.

Monster Location Quest item Quantity
Skeleton Raider Lv. 30 Wasteland Skull of a Skeleton Rider NG 20
Basilisk Lv. 28 Wasteland Charm of Basilisk NG 30

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31 Cruma Marshlands Stakato Worker Necklace NG 30

3. Once you have collected 3 full sets of quest items, return to Grey Guild Member and talk to him to take a reward.

Reward - Membership in the Hunters Guild lvl 1.

Membership level 1 benefits:

  • Access to Level 1 Requests
  • Access to Level 1 Goods
  • Access to the Bloody Crystals mini-quest
  • Access to the quest for Level 2 Hunter’s Guild Membership

Request

Requests are a special type of mini-quest available only to members of the Hunter’s Guild.

To accept Requests, talk to Tor Guild Member in the central square of Hunter's Village. Tor will offer a choice of 2 random requests from the general list. You can select only 1 request.

Bring Tor the required number of quest items and receive a reward:

  • Adena NG — The amount depends on the type of Request.
  • Certificate of Hunter NG — An item used to purchase Hunter’s Guild goods.
  • Laurel Leaf Pin NG — An item used to upgrade the Request class.

The level of your Hunter’s Guild membership directly affects the list of available Requests. There are 2 levels of Requests, each with 3 classes.

  • Hunter’s Guild Membership Level 1
    • Request Lv. 1 — Class C
    • Request Lv. 1 — Class B
    • Request Lv. 1 — Class A
  • Hunter’s Guild Membership Level 2
    • Request Lv. 2 — Class C
    • Request Lv. 2 — Class B
    • Request Lv. 2 — Class A
  • Hunter’s Guild Membership Level 3
    • Request Lv. 3 — Class C
    • Request Lv. 3 — Class B
    • Request Lv. 3 — Class A
  • Hunter’s Guild Membership Level 4
    • Request Lv. 4 — Class C
    • Request Lv. 4 — Class B
    • Request Lv. 4 — Class A

The Request class depends on the number of Laurel Leaf Pin NG in your inventory and is selected randomly.

  • Class C — 0-2 Laurel Leaf Pin NG
  • Class B — 3-4 Laurel Leaf Pin NG
  • Class A — 5+ Laurel Leaf Pin NG

Pay attention!

Having 5 Laurel Leaf Pin NG does not guarantee receiving a Class A Request. The class is selected randomly.

Laurel Leaf Pin NG can also be used to reroll the Requests offered by Tor. In this case, Tor will take 1 item and offer 2 new random Requests. If too many items are spent this way, high-class Requests may become unavailable.

Blood Crystal

Blood Crystal — a miniquest for upgrading Crystals with Adena rewards. The upgrade mechanics are similar to the SA upgrade.

To start the miniquest, talk to Cybellin (Cybellin) at the Grocery Store in Hunter's Village. Cybellin will give you:

Blood Crystal: Purity 1 NG
Cybellin's Dagger NG

Find the required Monsters and kill them while holding the Dagger.

Monsters to hunt
Monster Location Monster Location
Leto Lizardman Archer Lv. 36 Plains of the Lizardmen Harit Lizardman Grunt Lv. 51 Forest of Mirrors
Leto Lizardman Soldier Lv. 37 Plains of the Lizardmen Harit Lizardman Archer Lv. 52 Forest of Mirrors
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39 Plains of the Lizardmen Harit Lizardman Warrior Lv. 53 Forest of Mirrors
Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40 Plains of the Lizardmen

When killing a Monster, you need to hold Cybellin's Dagger NG in your hands.

Upon the Monster’s death, the Blood Crystal may either level up or break.

To receive a reward or a new Crystal, return to Cybellin (Cybellin) and talk to her.

The Blood Crystal’s level determines the reward:

  • Blood Crystal: Purity 2 NG — Adena NG x 500
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 3 NG — Adena NG x 1 500
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 4 NG — Adena NG x 9 000
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 5 NG — Adena NG x 30 000
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 6 NG — Adena NG x 60 000
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 7 NG — Adena NG x 100 000
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 8 NG — Adena NG x 250 000
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 9 NG — Adena NG x 550 000
  • Blood Crystal: Purity 10 NG — Adena NG x 1 200 000
