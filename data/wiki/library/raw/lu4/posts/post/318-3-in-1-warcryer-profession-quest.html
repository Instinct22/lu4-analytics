3 in 1 Warcryer profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Warcryer:

  • Quest Trial of the Pilgrim
  • Quest Testimony of Glory
  • Quest Test of the War Spirit

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Warcryer profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Pilgrim NG
  • Mark of Glory NG
  • Mark of Warspirit NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Dion. Find Somak Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

2. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Vokian will give Vokian's Order NG

3. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Fang NG

4. Go north from the teleport point in Death Pass. Find Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give Orim's Contract NG

5. Teleport to Oren. Find Pekiron Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Pekiron will give Pekiron's Totem NG

6. Teleport from Oren to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39

Until you collect:

Tonar's Skull NG
Tonar's Rib Bone NG
Tonar's Spine NG
Tonar's Arm Bone NG
Tonar's Thigh Bone NG

7. Return to Pekiron Seer and talk to him.

Pekiron will give Tonar's Remains NG

8. Teleport from Oren to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater, find and kill monsters:

Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Manashen Shard NG

9. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower find and kill monsters:

Porta Lv. 40
Excuro Lv. 41
Mordeo Lv. 42

Until you collect:

Porta's Eye NG x 10
Excuro's Scale NG x 10
Mordeos Talon NG x 10

10. Teleport from Giran to the entrance of Dragon Valley. Go north to Death Pass, find Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give Braki's Remains NG

11. Teleport to Gludio. Find Racoy Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Racoy will give Racoy's Totem NG

12. Head to the Temple of Gludio. Find Vivyan Priestess and talk to her.

Vivyan will give Vivyan's Letter NG

13. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Find Sarien Grocer in the camp and talk to him.

Sarien will give Insect Diagram Book NG

14. Head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Tyrant Talon NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

15. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave, head to the rooms near the Epic Boss. Find and kill monsters:

Noble Ant Lv. 37
Noble Ant Leader Lv. 38

Until you collect:

Kiruna's Skull NG
Kiruna's Rib Bone NG
Kiruna's Spine NG
Kiruna's Arm Bone NG
Kiruna's Thigh Bone NG

16. Teleport to Gludio. Find Racoy Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Racoy will give Kiruna's Remains NG

17. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Voucher of Trial NG

18. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

19. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

20. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Vokian will give:

Vokian's Order NG
Necklace of Authority NG

21. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 1st Order NG

22. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

You need to conduct two dialogues, choosing two different quests.

22.1. Ask Manakia about all the types of orcs, he will give:

Manakia's 1st Letter NG
Manakia's 2nd Letter NG

22.2 Manakia will give Manakia's Totem NG

23. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Head to the top of the northwestern hill. Find Voltar Breka Chief and talk to him.

Voltar will give Gloves of Voltar NG . After the conversation, quest monsters will appear:

Pashika Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37
Vultus Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the orcs and get:

Pashika's Head NG
Vultus' Head NG

Talk to Voltar again, he will give Scepter of Breka NG

24. Teleport to Giran. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Hermodt's Rib Bone NG
Hermodt's Spine NG
Hermodt's Arm Bone NG
Hermodt's Thigh Bone NG

North of the Necropolis of Martyrdom, find and kill the quest monster:

Stenoa Gorgon Queen Quest Monster Lv. 37

From the killed monster you get Hermodt's Skull NG

25. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Manakia will give Hermodt's Remains NG

26. Teleport to Dion. Find Somak Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Somak will give Vendetta Totem NG

27. Teleport from Dion to Bee Hive. Head west along the coast, towards the Hive Fortress. At the entrance to the Fortress, find Kepra Enku Chief and talk to him.

Kepra will give Gloves of Kepra NG . After the conversation, 4 quest monsters will appear:

Enku Orc Overlord Quest Monster Lv. 33

Kill orcs and get 4 Enku Overlord Head NG

Talk to Kepra again, he will give Scepter of Enku NG

28. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Kasman Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Kasman about all the types of orcs, he will give:

Kasman's 1st Letter NG
Kasman's 2nd Letter NG
Kasman's 3rd Letter NG

29. Teleport from Gludin Village to Langk Lizardmen Dwelling. Head northwest along the coast. Find Harak Leunt Chief on the coast and talk to him.

Harak will give Scepter of Tunath NG

30. Head north along the road to the Forgotten Temple. Go inside, to the fiery part of the temple. In the last room, turn left, find and kill monsters:

Lava Salamander Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Essence of Flame NG

31. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head deeper into the location. Find Burai Turek Chief and talk to him.

Burai will give Gloves of Burai NG . After the conversation, quest monsters will appear:

Makum Bugbear Thug Quest Monster Lv. 37

From killed monsters get 2 Makum Bugbear Head NG

Talk to Burai again, he will give Scepter of Turek NG

32. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Spirit of Flame NG

33. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

34.1. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Gauri Twinklerock near the Forge and talk to her.

Gauri will give Tag of Rumor NG

34.2. Head to the northern gate of the village. Find Dorf Wanderer and talk to him.

Dorf will give Gray Badge NG

35. Teleport to Gludio, exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, then swim across the river. Find Driko Vuku Chief near the ruined house and talk to him.

Driko will give Driko's Contract NG

36. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Head northeast, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35

Until you collect 30 Stakato Drone Husk NG

37. Return to Driko Vuku Chief and talk to him.

Driko will give Scepter of Vuku NG

38. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 3rd Order NG

39. Teleport to Giran. Find Primos Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

39. Teleport to Oren. Exit the city through the western gate. Go down the slope, find and kill monsters:

Timak Orc Overlord Lv. 45

Until you collect 20 Timak Orc Head NG

The quest item is obtained from all Timak Orc monsters in Timak Outpost. Club Card holders have access to teleport to the location.

40. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill. Find and kill monsters:

Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42

Until you collect:

Tamlin Orc Skull NG x 20
Tamlin Orc Head NG x 13

41. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Scepter Box NG

In the Orc Guild find Somak Seer and talk to him, he will give:

Warspirit Totem NG
Braki's Remains NG
Tonar's Remains NG
Hermodt's Remains NG
Kiruna's Remains NG

42. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Petron Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Petron will give Picture of Nahir NG

43. Teleport from Talking Island Village to Elven Ruins. Head inside the dungeon. In the room next to the Raid Boss find and kill monsters:

Nahir Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Hair of Nahir NG

44. Return to Petron Priest and talk to him.

Petron will give Statue of Einhasad NG

45. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Tanapi's Order NG

46. Teleport from Orc Village to Immortal Plateau, South. Follow the road north, turn east at the fork to the foot of the mountains. Find and kill monsters:

Ragna Orc Overlord Lv. 39
Ragna Orc Seer Lv. 39

Until the quest monster appears:

Revenant of Tantos Chief Quest Monster Lv. 39

Kill the monster and get Scepter of Tantos NG

Alternate monster location: The ravine south of the entrance to the Cave Of Trials.

47. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Mark of Warspirit NG

48. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Ritual Box NG

Head to the second floor of the Hall of Kings. Find Kakai Flame Lord and talk to him.

Kakai will give Mark of Glory NG

49. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Andellia near the teleport point and talk to her.

50.1. Teleport from Elven Village to Neutral Zone. Head north to the waterfall. North of the lake find and kill monsters:

Black Willow Quest Monster Lv. 30

Until you get Debris of Willow NG

50.2. On the northern shore of the lake find Uruha and talk to her.

Uruha will give Book of Darkness NG

51. Return to Andellia and talk to her.

52. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Wasteland. Go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Book of Sage NG

53. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Mark of Pilgrim NG

54. To change your profession, talk to Penatus High Prefect in the Giran Orc Guild.
