3 in 1 Warlock profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Warlock:

  • Quest Trial of the Scholar
  • Quest Testimony of Trust
  • Quest Test of the Summoner

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Warlock profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Scholar NG
  • Mark of Trust NG
  • Mark of Summoner NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give:

Letter to Elf NG
Letter to Dark Elf NG

2.1. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mirien's 1st Sigil NG

2.2. Talk to Sylvain High Priest in the same Temple, he will give:

High Priest's Sigil NG
Sylvain's Letter NG

3. Head to Dion's Grocery Store. Find Maria there and talk to her.

Maria will give Maria's 1st Letter NG

4. Head to the southern gate of Dion. Find Lucas Captain there and talk to him.

Lucas will give Lucas's Letter NG

On MasterWork you can talk to Lucas in the city center.

5. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Maria's 2nd Letter NG

6. Leave Dion Town through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village (Club Card holders can teleport to the village). Find Creta Astrologer behind the village Warehouse and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's 1st Letter NG

7. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Lucilla's Handbag NG

8. Return to Creta Astrologer and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's Painting1 NG

9. Go back to Maria again and talk to her.

Maria will give Creta's Painting2 NG

10. Head to the southern gate of Dion. Find Lucas Captain there and talk to him.

Lucas will give Creta's Painting3 NG

11. Go back to Maria again and talk to her.

12. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Galatea High Summoner in the Magic Shop and talk to her.

Galatea will give Galatea's Letter NG

13. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Order of Asterios NG

14. Teleport from Elven Village to Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Dryad Lv. 13
Dryad Elder Lv. 14

Until the quest monster appears:

Actea Of Verdant Wilds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

15. Go deep into the Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Lirein Lv. 18
Lirein Elder Lv. 19

Until the quest monster appears:

Luell Of Zephyr Winds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

16. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Scroll of Elf Trust NG

17. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Letter from Thifiell NG

18. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

Clayton will give Order of Clayton NG

19. Head to Dion's Grocery Store. Find Lara Grocer and talk to her.

Lara will give one random list. Delete the item in your quest inventory and talk to Lara again until you get Lara's 1st List NG

20. This stage of the quest depends on the previously received list.

Must collect 6 Beginner's Arcana NG

Lara's 1st List

20.1.1. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Lv. 35
Leto Lizardman Archer Lv. 36

Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39
Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you collect 30 Leto Lizardman Amulet NG

Giant Fungus Lv. 40

Until you collect 30 Sac of Redspores NG

20.1.3. Teleport to Dion. Find Lara Grocer in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Lara will give 2 Beginner's Arcana NG

To continue the quest you must have 6 Beginner's Arcana NG

If you have collected 6 cards, proceed to the next stage of the quest.

If there are not enough cards, get a new list from Lara.

Lara's 2nd List

Karul Bugbear Lv. 40

Until you collect 30 Karul Bugbear Totem NG

Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

Until you collect 30 Shards of Manashen NG

20.2.3. Teleport to Dion. Find Lara Grocer in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Lara will give 2 Beginner's Arcana NG

To continue the quest you must have 6 Beginner's Arcana NG

If you have collected 6 cards, proceed to the next stage of the quest.

If there are not enough cards, get a new list from Lara.

Breka Orc Lv. 31
Breka Orc Archer Lv. 32
Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34
Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35
Breka Orc Warrior Lv. 33

Until you collect 30 Breka Orc Totem NG

20.3.2. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Fettered Soul Lv. 38

Until you collect 30 Crimson Bloodstone NG

20.3.3. Teleport to Dion. Find Lara Grocer in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Lara will give 2 Beginner's Arcana NG

To continue the quest you must have 6 Beginner's Arcana NG

If you have collected 6 cards, proceed to the next stage of the quest.

If there are not enough cards, get a new list from Lara.

20.4.1. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Instead of going inside the cave, head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Tyrant Lv. 35

Until you collect 30 Talons of Tyrant NG

20.4.2. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39

Until you collect 30 Tusk of Windsus NG

20.4.3. Teleport to Dion. Find Lara Grocer in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Lara will give 2 Beginner's Arcana NG

To continue the quest you must have 6 Beginner's Arcana NG

If there are not enough cards, get a new list from Lara.

Noble Ant Leader Lv. 38

Until you collect 30 Wings of Droneant NG

Until you collect 30 Fangs of Wyrm NG

Lara will give 2 Beginner's Arcana NG

To continue the quest you must have 6 Beginner's Arcana NG

21. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38

Until you collect 5 Brown Scroll Scrap NG

Skip this step if you have already collected the quest items during the previous step.

22. Teleport to Dion. Find Maria in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Maria will give Crystal of Purity NG

23.1. Head to the Temple of Dion. Find Sylvain High Priest and talk to him.

Sylvain will give Symbol of Sylvain NG

23.2. Talk to Mirien Magister in the same Temple.

Mirien will give Mirien's 2nd Sigil NG

24. Teleport from Dion to Bee Hive. Head towards Hive Fortress. Find Camoniell Summoner near the house and talk to her.

After the conversation, a battle of summoned servants will begin. Make sure you have summoned your servant and prepared it for battle.

Attack Racer the Unicorn Quest Monster Lv. 38 with your servant. Your character should not take part in the fight!

After winning, talk to Camoniell, she will give Camoniell's Arcana NG

25. Teleport to Giran. Find Jurek Grand Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Jurek will give:

Grand Magister's Sigil NG
Jurek's List NG

26. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34

Until you collect 5 Shaman's Necklace NG

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

You can reach the next location on foot.

27. Teleport to Giran, then to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Blood NG

10 vials of blood will turn into Basilisk Plasma NG

28. Head to the Dragon Valley. Near the entrance find and kill monsters:

Shackle Lv. 45

Until you collect 2 Shackle's Scalp NG

29. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Soldier Lv. 35

Until you collect 10 Giant Aphid NG

10 aphid will turn into Honey Dew NG

Quest item obtained from all ant monsters inside the Ant Nest

30. Teleport to Dion, then to Plains of Dion. Find and kill monsters:

Monster Eye Destroyer Lv. 26

Until you collect 5 Monster Eye Destroyer's Skin NG

31. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Around the tower find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31

Until you collect 10 Stakatos Fluids NG

10 fluids will turn into Stakato Ichor NG

Quest item dropped by all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

32. Head to the southeast of the Cruma Marshlands. Find Belthus Summoner and talk to him.

After the conversation, a battle of summoned servants will begin. Make sure you have summoned your servant and prepared it for battle.

Attack Shadow Turen Quest Monster Lv. 39 with your servant. Your character should not take part in the fight!

After winning, talk to Belthus. He will give Belthus's Arcana NG

33. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

34. Teleport to Giran. Find Jurek Grand Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Jurek will give Symbol of Jurek NG

35. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mirien's 3rd Sigil NG

36. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Cronos Elder in the main square and talk to him.

Cronos will give:

Crono's Sigil NG
Cronos Letter NG

37. Teleport to Giran. Find Dieter Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Dieter will give Dieter's Key NG

38. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Creta Astrologer behind the Village Warehouse and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's 2nd Letter NG

39. Return to Dieter Magister (Dieter) and talk to him.

Dieter will give:

Dieter's Letter NG
Dieter's Diary NG

40. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Edroc Armor Merchant in the Weapon Shop and talk to him.

Edroc will give Raut's Letter Envelope NG

41. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Raut Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Raut will give:

Scripture Chapter 1 NG
Strong Liquor NG

42. Head to Gludin Harbor. Find Triff Drunkard and talk to him.

Triff will give Triff's Ring NG

43. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Scroll of Dark Elf Trust NG

44. Teleport to Elven Village, then to Elven Fortress. Instead of going inside, head south along the road. Turn right at the fork and head west. Find Celestiel Summoner by the road and talk to him.

After the conversation, a battle of summoned servants will begin. Make sure you have summoned your servant and prepared it for battle.

Attack Unicorn Phantasm Quest Monster Lv. 40 with your Servant. Your character should not take part in the fight!

After winning, talk to Celestiel, he will give Celestiel's Arcana NG

Valkon will give Valkon's Request NG

Hollint will give Letter to Seresin NG

Talk to Seresin Cardinal at the entrance to the Temple of Oren

Seresin will give:

Letter to Dwarf NG
Letter to Orc NG

47. Teleport to Dion. Find Maria in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Maria will give Crystal of Purity NG

48. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Letter to Manakia NG

49. Teleport to Giran. Find Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Valkon will give Scripture Chapter 2 NG

50. Head to the Giran Orc Guild. Find Manakia Seer and talk to him.

51. Teleport from Giran to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39
Grandis Lv. 40

Until you collect:

Parasite of Lota NG x 10
Scripture Chapter 3 NG

52. Return to Manakia Seer and talk to him.

Manakia will give Letter of Manakia NG

53. Teleport to Dion. Find Poitan Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Poitan will give Poitan's Notes NG

54. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Head west, find Basilla Summoner near the house and talk to her.

After the conversation, a battle of summoned servants will begin. Make sure you have summoned your servant and prepared it for battle.

Attack Mimi the Cat Quest Monster Lv. 39 with your Servant. Your character should not take part in the fight!

After winning, talk to Basillia, she will give Basillia's Arcana NG

55. Return to the teleport point to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Head inside the location, go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Casian's List NG

56. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Scroll of Orc Trust NG

57. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Letter to Nikola NG

58. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head southwest. Find Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give Order of Nikola NG

59. Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower find and kill monsters:

Porta Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Heartstone of Porta NG

60. Return to Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

61. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Head east along the road to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Ghoul Lv. 32

Until you collect 10 Ghoul's Skin NG

62. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Scroll of Dwarf Trust NG

63. Teleport to Giran. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect 12 Medusa's Blood NG

64. In the Gorgon Flower Garden find Brynthea Summoner and talk to her.

After the conversation, a battle of summoned servants will begin. Make sure you have summoned your servant and prepared it for battle.

Attack Silhouette Tilfo Quest Monster Lv. 40 with your servant. Your character should not take part in the fight!

After winning, talk to Brynthea, she will give Brynthea's Arcana NG

65. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Fettered Soul Lv. 38

Until you collect 5 Fettered Soul's Ichor NG

66. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Recommendation of Hollint NG

67. Teleport from Oren to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Gargoyle Lv. 44

Until you collect 5 Enchanted Gargoyle's Nail NG

68. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Biotin High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Biotin will give Mark of Trust NG

69. Teleport from Talking Island Village to Singing Waterfall. Head north along the coast, find Almors Summoner near the houses and talk to him.

After the conversation, a battle of summoned servants will begin. Make sure you have summoned your servant and prepared it for battle.

Attack Pako the Cat Quest Monster Lv. 38 with your Servant. Your character should not take part in the fight!

After winning, talk to Almors, he will give Almors's Arcana NG

70. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Galatea High Summoner in the Magic Shop and talk to her.

Galatea will give Mark of Summoner NG

71. Teleport from Gludin Village to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Scripture Chapter 4 NG

72. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Cronos Elder in the main square and talk to him.

Cronos will give Symbol of Cronos NG

73. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mark of Scholar NG

74. To change your profession, talk to Jurek Grand Magister in the Giran Mages Guild.
