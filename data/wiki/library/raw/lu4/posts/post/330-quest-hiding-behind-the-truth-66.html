Pay attention!

Mission rewards for completing these quests can be received once for each game account. Game accounts are common to both servers — after completing the quest on the Fire server, the reward for the mission on the Water server will become unavailable.

Hiding Behind the Truth quest guide

1. The starting NPC Benedict Priest is at the top of the Rune Temple. Talk to him to get the quest Hiding Behind the Truth. The dialogue choice does not affect anything.

2. Talk to NPC Agripel High Priest , who is standing next to Benedict.

3. Teleport to the Forest of the Dead and talk to NPC Mysterious Wizard .

4. In the southwestern part of the forest (the spot with vampires) find Broken Bookshelf and examine it.

5. A monster Triol's Pawn Quest Monster Lv. 65 will appear from the closet, kill it.

6. Inspect Broken Bookshelf again to obtain Contract NG .

7. In the northern part of the forest, find NPC Mysterious Wizard and talk to him.

8. In the southwestern part of the forest (the spot with vampires), find NPC Lidia's Maid and talk to her.

8. In the southeastern part of the forest, near the Cursed Village, find Tombstone and examine it.

9. A Coffin will appear near the Tombstone, examine it to obtain Lidia's Dress NG .

10. In the southwestern part of the forest (the spot with vampires), find NPC Lidia's Maid and talk to her several times.

11. In the northern part of the forest, find NPC Mysterious Wizard and talk to him.

12. Teleport to Rune. At the top of the Temple, find NPC Agripel High Priest and talk to him. At the end of the dialogue, your choice will determine who completes the quest.

The choice of dialogue response does not affect the final reward.

If you chose "Meet Lydia"

13.1. In the western part of the forest, find NPC Lidia's Maid and talk to her to complete the quest.

If you chose "Meet Hardin"

13.2. Teleport to Forest of the Dead and talk to NPC Mysterious Wizard to complete the quest.
