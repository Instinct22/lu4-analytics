Location Keeper Ankou

Locations 06.01.2026

Basics

Location Keeper Ankou is a new mini-event designed specifically for MasterWork. Don't sleep on the spot, otherwise Ankou will come and take your life!

Ankou is a pretty strong monster with many nasty skills and surprises. But the winners will get expensive rewards, such as masterwork spheres, buff slot books and boxes with enchanting items.

Where to find?

The location guardian Ankou appears when a certain number of monsters die in the location. Ankou level depends on the location:

  • Cemetery - Level 55
  • Paver Ruins - Level 58
  • Dragon Valley - Level 58
  • Forbidden Gateway - Level 62
  • Forest Of The Dead - Level 68
  • Blazing Swamp - Level 72
  • Wall of Agros - Level 75
  • Hot Springs - Level 78
  • Imperial Tomb - Level 80
  • Silent Valley - Level 80
  • Hellbound - Level 85

Pay attention!

Now, Ankou patrols the area and does not vanish until brave warriors defeat him in a fair fight.

Skills and Features

Skill Description
Swords storm Lv. 1

Anku attacks enemies around with dark blades, causing enemies to stop attacking and Cancel target.

Active skill. Deal physical damage, interrupt the target and any current cast.
Sinister Touch Lv. 1

You feel the touch of Anku's harbinger of death, which consumes your health and mana every second.

Debuff. Deal magic damage that restores Ankou's health and burns HP and MP for 30 seconds. May be canceled by Cleanse Lv. 1

Ankou Cry Lv. 1

A clouding of the mind caused by Anku's scream. Increases MP Consumption and P./ M. Skills.

Debuff. Increases mana consumption and skill cooldown by 30 seconds. May be canceled by Cleanse Lv. 1

Obscure legacy Lv. 1

All you got is pain. Atk. Spd, Casting Spd and Speed is significantly reduced.

Active skill. Deal magic damage to the target and in an area around the target. The target and other players get a debuff that slows attack, casting and moving speed for 3 seconds.
Ankou’s Curse Lv. 1

Magic skills are blocked, temporarily decreasing speed. Black Shroud is activated, inflicting major damage on the minds and stamina of nearby party members after 10 seconds.

Debuff. Block magic skills and reduce moving speed. After 10 seconds, take away % of current HP and MP. May cancelled by Cleanse Lv. 1

Pay attention!

Players using certain skills near Ankou can cause him to respond by using extra skills to counter players. We leave this part of the content for independent study.
