3 in 1 Bishop profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Bishop:

  • Quest Trial of the Pilgrim
  • Quest Testimony of Trust
  • Quest Test of the Healer

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Bishop profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Pilgrim NG
  • Mark of Trust NG
  • Mark of Healer NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give:

Letter to Elf NG
Letter to Dark Elf NG

2. Teleport to Giran. Find Bandellos Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Bandellos will give Report of Perrin NG

3. Teleport to Gludin Village. Exit the village through the north gate. Follow the road west to the north lighthouse of Gludin Harbor. Find Perrin and talk to him.

After the conversation, a quest monster will appear:

Tatoma Quest Monster Lv. 32

Kill the monster and talk to Perrin again.

4. Head south, go around the harbor. Climb up the hill to the southern lighthouse. Find Allana and talk to her.

5.1. Teleport to Gludio. Exit the city through the western gate. Head to the houses in the northwest. Behind one of the houses find Gupu Father and talk to him.

Select the second dialogue line:

"Pretend that you did not hear anything"

5.2. Talk to Gupu Father again.

6. Teleport to Gludio. Find Sorius Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sorius will give Order of Sorius NG

7. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Voucher of Trial NG

8. Go deep into the Orc Barracks. Find Daurin Hammercrush and talk to him.

After the conversation, 3 quest monsters will appear:

Leto Lizardman Leader Quest Monster Lv. 38
Leto Lizardman Agent Quest Monster Lv. 35 x 2

Kill all quest monsters and get 1st Secret Letter NG

Talk to Daurin again.

9. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Order of Asterios NG

10. Teleport from Elven Village to Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Dryad Lv. 13
Dryad Elder Lv. 14

Until the quest monster appears:

Actea Of Verdant Wilds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

11. Go deep into the Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Lirein Lv. 18
Lirein Elder Lv. 19

Until the quest monster appears:

Luell Of Zephyr Winds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

12. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Scroll of Elf Trust NG

13. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Letter from Thifiell NG

14.1. Teleport from Dark Elf Village to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites, find Mysterious Dark Elf near the altar and talk to her.

Mysterious Dark Elf will summon three quest monsters:

Leto Lizardman Assassin Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill all quest monsters and get 2nd Secret Letter NG

14.2. Wait for the Mysterious Dark Elf to appear and talk to her, she will summon three quest monsters:

Leto Lizardman Sniper Quest Monster Lv. 36

Kill all quest monsters and get 3rd Secret Letter NG

14.3. Wait for the Mysterious Dark Elf to appear and talk to her, she will summon three quest monsters:

Leto Lizardman Lord Quest Monster Lv. 40
Leto Lizardman Wizard Quest Monster Lv. 39 x 2

Kill all quest monsters and get 4th Secret Letter NG

14.4. Wait for the Mysterious Dark Elf to appear and talk to her.

15. Head south along the road from the Altar of Rites. Look for the red tents between the Necropolis of Devotion and Cloud Mountain Fortress. Find Kristina Saint near the tents and talk to her.

Kristina will give Kristina's Letter NG

16. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

Clayton will give Order of Clayton NG

17. Teleport to Gludio. Find Sorius Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

18. Teleport from Gludio to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave, find and kill monsters:

Ant Soldier Lv. 35

Until you collect 10 Giant Aphid NG

10 aphid will become Honey Dew NG

Quest item obtained from all ant monsters inside the Ant Nest

19. Teleport to Giran. Find Bandellos Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Bandellos will give Mark of Healer NG

20. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Blood NG

10 vials of blood will turn into Basilisk Plasma NG

21. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Around the tower, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31

Until you collect 10 Stakatos Fluids NG

10 fluids will turn into Stakato Ichor NG

Quest item dropped by all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

22. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

23. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Scroll of Dark Elf Trust NG

24. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Letter to Seresin NG

Talk to Seresin Cardinal at the entrance to the Temple of Oren

Seresin will give:

Letter to Dwarf NG
Letter to Orc NG

25. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

26. Talk to Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings.

Kakai will give Letter to Manakia NG

27. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

28. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Forgotten Temple. Head inside, to the fire part of the temple. In the last room, turn left, find and kill monsters:

Lava Salamander Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Essence of Flame NG

29. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

30. Teleport from Giran to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39

Until you collect 10 Parasite of Lota NG

31. Return to Manakia Seer and talk to him.

Manakia will give Letter of Manakia NG

32. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Spirit of Flame NG

33. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

34. Talk to Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings.

Kakai will give Scroll of Orc Trust NG

35. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Letter to Nikola NG (Letter to Nikola)

36. Head to the Dwarven Village Blacksmith Shop. Find Gauri Twinklerock and talk to her.

Gauri will give Tag of Rumor NG

Head to the northern gate of the village. Find Dorf Wanderer and talk to him.

Dorf will give Gray Badge NG

37. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head southwest. Find Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give Order of Nikola NG

38. Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower find and kill monsters:

Porta Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Heartstone of Porta NG

39. Return to Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

40. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Scroll of Dwarf Trust NG

41. Teleport to Giran. Find Primos Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

42. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Recommendation of Hollint NG

43. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Biotin High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Biotin will give Mark of Trust NG

Talk to Petron Priest in the Temple.

Petron will give Picture of Nahir NG

44. Teleport from Talking Island Village to Elven Ruins. Head inside the dungeon. In the room next to the Raid Boss find and kill monsters:

Nahir Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Hair of Nahir NG

45. Return to Petron Priest and talk to him.

Petron will give Statue of Einhasad NG

46. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Andellia near the teleport point and talk to her.

47. Teleport from Elven Village to Neutral Zone. Head north to the waterfall. North of the lake find and kill monsters:

Black Willow Quest Monster Lv. 30

Until you get Debris of Willow NG

On the northern shore of the lake find Uruha and talk to her.

Uruha will give Book of Darkness NG

48. Return to Andellia and talk to her.

49. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Wasteland. Go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Book of Sage NG

50. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Mark of Pilgrim NG

51. To change your profession, talk to Maximilian High Priest in the Temple of Giran.
