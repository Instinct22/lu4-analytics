Quest Testimony of Prosperity

Quests to change class 23.06.2026

Dwarves understand the virtue of abundance above all others. True Dwarves study the teachings of Maphr in order to better grasp the concept of plenty!

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Testimony of Prosperity Quest guide

To complete the quest, you will need the following materials:

Animal Skin NG x 100
Crystal (D-Grade) D x 10

To save time and money, prepare them in advance.

1. The starting NPC Parman Warehouse Keeper is located in the Giran Warehouse. Talk to Parman to start the quest.

Parman will give 1st Ring of Testimony NG

2. Teleport to Dion. Head to the east gate. Find Bright Guard near the gate and talk to him.

Bright will give Bright's List NG

3. Exit Dion through the eastern gate. Head east along the road to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Mandragora Sprout Lv. 21
Mandragora Sapling Lv. 23
Mandragora Blossom Lv. 25

Until you collect Mandragora Petal NG x 20

4. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Around the tower, find and kill monsters:

Giant Crimson Ant Lv. 28

Until you collect Crimson Moss NG x 10

5.1. Teleport to Dion. Head to the eastern gate. Find Bright Guard near the gate and talk to him.

Bright will give Mandragora Bouquet NG

5.2. Find Emily near the east gate inside the city and talk to her.

Emily will give Emily's Recipe NG

6. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Wilford Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Pick the line "An Elf more than 1,000 years old" in the dialogue

Wilford will give Crystal Brooch NG

7. Head to Talking Island Village Temple. Find Lilith and talk to her.

Lilith will give Lilith's Elven Wafer NG

8. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Piotur near the house and talk to him.

Piotur will give Blessed Seed NG

9. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Head to the Council of Elders. Talk to the six NPCs in the House of Elders one by one.

9.1. Talk to Lockirin Iron Gate's , he will give:

Collection License NG
Lockirin's 1st Notice NG
Lockirin's 2nd Notice NG
Lockirin's 3rd Notice NG
Lockirin's 4th Notice NG
Lockirin's 5th Notice NG

9.2. Talk to Balanki Silver Scale's

9.3. Talk to Arin Black Anvil's

9.4. Talk to Keef Bronze Key's

9.5. Talk to Spiron Golden Wheel's

9.6. Talk to Filaur Gray Pillar Member

10. Talk to four NPCs in Dwarven Village one by one.

10.1. Head to the Grocery Store. Find Mion Grocer and talk to her.

Mion will give Contribution of Mion NG

10.2. Head to the Warehouse. Find Torocco Carrier behind the building and talk to him.

Torocco will give Procuration of Torocco NG

10.3. Head to the north gate. Find Maryse Redbonnet near the house and talk to her.

Maryse will give Maryse's Request NG (Maryse's Request)

Talk to Maryse again, give her Animal Skin NG x 100

Maryse will give Contribution of Maryse NG

10.4. Head to the Weapon Shop. Find Shari Armor Merchant and talk to her.

Shari will give Contribution of Shari NG

11. Leave Dwarven Village through the southern gate. Follow the road southeast towards Strip Mine. Find Bolter Miner near the road and talk to him.

Bolter will give Receipt of Bolter NG

12. Return to Dwarven Village, then teleport to Western Mining Zone. Follow the road northeast to the mouth of the river. On the island, off the northern shore, find Toma Master and talk to him.

Toma will give Contribution of Toma NG

On Lu4, Master Toma does not teleport and can be found in all of his locations simultaneously.

13. Teleport to Dwarven Village and head to the House of Elders. Talk to the six NPCs in the House of Elders one by one.

13.1. Talk to Balanki Silver Scale's (Balanki)

Balanki will give 2nd Receipt of Contribution NG

13.2. Talk to Arin Black Anvil's

Arin will give 5th Receipt of Contribution NG

13.3. Talk to Keef Bronze Key's

You will have to pay 5000 Adena NG

Keef will give 3rd Receipt of Contribution NG

13.4. Talk to Spiron Golden Wheel's

Spiron will give 1st Receipt of Contribution NG

13.5. Talk to Filaur Gray Pillar Member

Filaur will give 4th Receipt of Contribution NG

13.6. Talk to Lockirin Iron Gate's

Lockirin will give Old Account Book NG

14. Teleport to Giran. Find Parman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and Talk to him.

Parman will give:

2nd Ring of Testimony NG
Parman's Letter NG

15. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head southwest. Find Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give Clay Dough NG

16. Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor, in the hallway behind the Raid Boss, find Box Of Titan and examine it.

After examining the box, you will receive Pattern of Keyhole NG

17. Return to Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give:

Recipe: Titan Key NG
Nikola's List NG

Register the recipe in the crafting book.

18. In the Cruma Marshlands, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31
Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Toad Lord Lv. 32

Until you collect:

Stakato Shell NG x 20
Marsh Spider's Thorn NG x 10
Toad Lord Sac NG x 10

Stakato Shell is dropped from all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

19. Open the crafting book and create Key of Titan NG

Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor, in the hallway behind the Raid Boss, find Box Of Titan and open it with the key.

From the opened box, you will receive Maphr Tablet Fragment NG

20. Teleport to Giran. Find Parman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Parman will complete the quest and give Mark of Prosperity NG
