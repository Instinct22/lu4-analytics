Law Enforcement 76+

Quests to change class 08.12.2025


Law Enforcement
Starting NPC Liane Grand Master
Starting location Aden
Level 76 ~ 85
Necessary for the profession Judicator
Type One-time
Reward
Adena NG x 26 000

Quest guide

1. Starting NPC Liane Grand Master is located in the Kamael Guild in Aden. Talk to Liane to begin the quest.

2. Teleport to Kamael Village, find Kekropus Hierarch in the Elder's House, and talk to him.

3. Exit the Elder's House through the back balcony, head upstairs, find Eindburgh Vice Hierarch and talk to him.

Eindburgh will complete the quest and give a reward.

Completing the quest will change the character's profession.

Congratulations, you are now a Judicator, use your knowledge wisely!

On MasterWork, you can choose the third Kamael subclass, Judicator, as your main class and play it just like any other character. You now have access to the Olympiad, the chance to earn Hero status, and full participation in parties — not merely serving as a buffer window.
You can become an Inquisitor after completing the special quest Seeds of Chaos.
