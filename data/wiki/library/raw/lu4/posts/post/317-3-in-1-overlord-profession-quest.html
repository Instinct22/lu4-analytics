3 in 1 Overlord profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Overlord:

  • Quest Trial of the Pilgrim
  • Quest Testimony of Glory
  • Quest Test of the Lord

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Overlord profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Pilgrim NG
  • Mark of Glory NG
  • Mark of Lord NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Voucher of Trial NG

2. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Ordeal Necklace NG

3. Talk to three NPCs in the Hall of Kings in Orc Village one by one.

3.1. On the second floor of the Hall of Kings find Tantus Neruga Chief and talk to him.

Tantus will give Tantus's Charm NG

3.2. On the stairs of the Hall of Kings find Varkees Atuba Chief and talk to him.

Varkees will give Varkee's Charm NG

3.3. On the other stairs of the Hall of Kings find Hatos Urutu Chief and talk to him.

Hatos will give Hatos's Charm NG

4. Head to the Orc Village Blacksmith Shop. Find Sumari Blacksmith and talk to him.

Sumari will give Sumari's Letter NG

5. Head to the Orc Village Weapon Shop. Find Jakal Weapon Merchant and talk to him.

Jakal will give Neruga Axe Blade NG

You will have to pay 1000 Adena NG

6. Buy 1000 Bone Arrow D from the Grocery Store

Head to the Hall of Kings. Find Tantus Neruga Chief on the second floor and talk to him.

Tantus will give Axe of Ceremony NG

7. Head to the first floor of the Hall of Kings. Find Tanapi Seer and talk to him.

8. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave, find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

9.1. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's Charm NG

9.2. In the Orc Guild find Somak Seer and talk to him.

Somak will give Urutu Blade NG

10. Teleport to Giran, find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Manakia will give Manakia's Orders NG

In the Orc Guild find Vokian Prefect and talk to him.

Vokian will give Vokian's Order NG

11. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Fang NG

12. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater, find and kill monsters:

Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Manashen Shard NG

13. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Tyrant Talon NG

14. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Vokian will give:

Vokian's Order NG
Necklace of Authority NG

15. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 1st Order NG

16. Teleport to Giran, find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Manakia about all the types of orcs, he will give:

Manakia's 1st Letter NG
Manakia's 2nd Letter NG

17. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Head to the top of the northwestern hill. Find Voltar Breka Chief and talk to him.

Voltar will give Gloves of Voltar NG . After the conversation, quest monsters will appear:

Pashika Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37
Vultus Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the orcs and get:

Pashika's Head NG
Vultus' Head NG

Talk to Voltar again, he will give Scepter of Breka NG

18. In the Breka's Stronghold location, find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35
Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34

Until you collect 20 Breka Orc Fang NG

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

19. Teleport to Giran, find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Manakia will give Manakia's Amulet NG

20. Teleport to Oren, find Takuna Duda-Mara Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Takuna will give Takuna's Charm NG

21. Teleport to Dion, then to Bee Hive. Head west along the coast towards Hive Fortress. At the entrance to the Fortress, find Kepra Enku Chief and talk to him.

Kepra will give Gloves of Kepra NG . After the conversation, 4 quest monsters will appear:

Enku Orc Overlord Quest Monster Lv. 33

Kill orcs and get 4 Enku Overlord Head NG

Talk to Kepra again, he will give Scepter of Enku NG

22. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Kasman Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Kasman about all the types of orcs, he will give:

Kasman's 1st Letter NG
Kasman's 2nd Letter NG
Kasman's 3rd Letter NG

23. Teleport from Gludin Village to Orc Barracks. Head deeper into the location. Find Burai Turek Chief and talk to him.

Burai will give Gloves of Burai NG . After the conversation, quest monsters will appear:

Makum Bugbear Thug Quest Monster Lv. 37

From killed monsters get 2 Makum Bugbear Head NG

Talk to Burai again, he will give Scepter of Turek NG

24. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Langk Lizardmen Dwellings. Head northwest along the coast. Find Harak Leunt Chief on the coast and talk to him.

Harak will give Scepter of Tunath NG

25. Head north along the road to the Forgotten Temple. Go inside to the fiery part of the temple. In the last room, turn left, find and kill monsters:

Lava Salamander Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Essence of Flame NG

26. Teleport to Gludio. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, then swim across the river. Find Driko Vuku Chief near the ruined house and talk to him.

Driko will give Driko's Contract NG

27. Teleport from Dion to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35
Marsh Spider Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Stakato Drone Husk NG x 30
Marsh Spider's Feeler NG x 10
Marsh Spider's Feet NG x 10

28. Teleport to Gludio. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, then swim across the river. Find Driko Vuku Chief near the ruined house and talk to him.

Driko will give Scepter of Vuku NG

29. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 3rd Order NG

30. Teleport to Oren. Find Takuna Duda-Mara Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Takuna will give Handiwork Spider Brooch NG

31. Exit Oren through the western gate. Go down the slope. Find and kill monsters:

Timak Orc Overlord Lv. 45

Until you collect:

Timak Orc Skull NG x 10
Timak Orc Head NG x 20

Timak Orc Head is obtained from all Timak Orc monsters in Timak Outpost. Club Card holders have access to teleport to the location.

32. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Monstereye Lv. 41

Until you collect 20 Enchanted Monster Cornea NG

33. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill. Find and kill monsters:

Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42

Until you collect 20 Tamlin Orc Skull NG

34. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

You need to conduct two dialogues and choose two different quests.

34.1. Chianta will give Scepter Box NG

34.2. Chianta will give Monster Eye Woodcarving NG

35. Teleport to Orc Village. Head to the Hall of Kings, find and talk to three NPCs one by one.

35.1. On the stairs of the Hall of Kings find Varkees Atuba Chief and talk to him.

Varkees will give Huge Orc Fang NG

35.2. On the other stairs of the Hall of Kings find Hatos Urutu Chief and talk to him.

Hatos will give Sword Into Skull NG

35.3. On the second floor of the Hall of Kings find Kakai Flame Lord and talk to him.

Kakai will give Bear Fang Necklace NG

36. Head to the first floor of the Hall of Kings. Find Tanapi Seer and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Tanapi's Order NG

37. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave, find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

You need to conduct two dialogues and choose two different quests.

37.1. Martankus will give Spirit of Flame NG

37.2. Martankus will give Martankus's Charm NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

38. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Immortal Plateau, South. Follow the road north, at the fork turn east to the foot of the mountains. Find and kill monsters:

Ragna Orc Overlord Lv. 39
Ragna Orc Seer Lv. 39

Until you get:

Ragna Orc Head NG
Ragna Chief Notice NG

And until the quest monster appears:

Revenant of Tantos Chief Quest Monster Lv. 39

Kill the monster and get Scepter of Tantos NG

Alternate monster location: The ravine south of the entrance to the Cave Of Trials.

39. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave and keep to the right side. Inside the cave, find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Immortal Flame NG

40. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

You need to conduct two dialogues and choose two different quests.

Tanapi will give Ritual Box NG

41. Head to the second floor of the Hall of Kings. Find Kakai Flame Lord (Kakai) and talk to him.

You need to conduct two dialogues and choose two different quests.

41.1. Kakai will give Mark of Glory NG

41.2. Kakai will give Mark of Lord NG

42.1. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Gauri Twinklerock near the Blacksmith Shop and talk to her.

Gauri will give Tag of Rumor NG

42.2. Head to the northern gate of the village. Find Dorf Wanderer and talk to him.

Dorf will give Gray Badge NG

43. Teleport to Giran. Find Primos Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

44. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Petron Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Petron will give Picture of Nahir NG

45. Teleport from Talking Island Village to Elven Ruins. Head inside the dungeon. In the room next to the Raid Boss find and kill monsters:

Nahir Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Hair of Nahir NG

46. Return to Petron Priest and talk to him.

Petron will give Statue of Einhasad NG

47. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Andellia near the teleport point and talk to her.

48.1. Teleport from Elven Village to Neutral Zone. Head north to the waterfall. North of the lake find and kill monsters:

Black Willow Quest Monster Lv. 30

Until you get Debris of Willow NG

48.2. On the northern shore of the lake find Uruha and talk to her.

Uruha will give Book of Darkness NG

49. Return to Andellia and talk to her.

50. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Wasteland. North among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Book of Sage NG

51. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Mark of Pilgrim NG

52. To change your profession talk to Penatus High Prefect in the Giran Orc Guild.
