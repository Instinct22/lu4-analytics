Clan passive skills

Skills 08.07.2026

Total items required: 0

Basics

From the 1st level of the clan, it is possible to learn passive skills. These skills require special items — Proof of Loyalty NG . As the clan leve increases, new skills become available.

Pay attention!

On Lu4, standard passive clan skills work for ALL clan members.

Items for learning

To learn clan skills on Lu4, you need Proof of Loyalty NG . Proof of Loyalty can be obtained in several ways:

  • Reward for completing the first class transfer
  • Reward for completing second class marks
  • Hunting certain high-level monsters
  • Drops from world bosses
  • Purchase from other players
