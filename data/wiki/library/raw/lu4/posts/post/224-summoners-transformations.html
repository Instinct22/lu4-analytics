Summoner's Transformations

Skills 10.12.2025


Basics

In the Gracia Final update, Summoner classes with a third profession gained racial transformation skills in their arsenal. Only Summoners who had reached level 83 could learn these new skills.

On MasterWork races transformations divided by 2 levels, that can be received at level 79 and 83 respectively.

Besides that, on MasterWork have been added are 4 new and unique transformations, which allow changing the archetype. Archetype transformations are learned at level 40 upon obtaining the second profession.

Archetype Transformations

At level 40 and second profession 4 transformations available for Summoners.

The classes of summoners:

  • Warlock
  • Elemental Summoner
  • Phantom Summoner

Transformations 1 level/ 2 level

  • Required level: 40/ 76
  • Duration: 6 hours
  • Cooldown: 2 seconds

The following rules apply to all archetype transformations:

  • Basic stats STR/ DEX/ CON changes, depending on transformation type.
  • Mass Summon Phantom Cubic Lv. 1 and other cubes stays with a character. To summon them again transformation have to be canceled.
  • Party Blessing of Queen Lv. 1 and other unique class buffs can be used while transformed.
  • Servitor will be unsummoned.

When using the level 2 skill, the summoned servant does not disappear.

Races Transformations

Races transformation are unique for each class, they change characters appearance and provides new skills.

Transformations 1 level/ 2 level

  • Required level: 79/ 83
  • Duration: 10/ 15 minutes
  • Cooldown: 30/ 25 minutes

As the character's level increases, the level of transformation skills also increases.

  • Lv. 79 — Skill Lv. 1
  • Lv. 81 — Skill Lv. 2
  • Lv. 82 — Skill Lv. 3
  • Lv. 83 — Skill Lv. 4
  • Lv. 84 — Skill Lv. 5
  • Lv. 85 — Skill Lv. 6

The following rules apply for all races transformations:

  • Basic stats STR/ DEX/ CON/ INT/ WIT/ MEN change depending on the transformation.
  • Mass Summon Phantom Cubic Lv. 1 and other cubes stays with a character. To summon them again transformation have to be canceled.
  • Party Blessing of Queen Lv. 1 and other unique class buffs can be used while transformed.
  • Servitor will be unsummoned.
