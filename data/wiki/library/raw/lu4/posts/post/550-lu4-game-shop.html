Basics

Dear players, we’re excited to introduce the Lu4 Game Store. We’ve gathered a wide range of useful items all in one convenient place. To access the store’s services, speak with Adventurers' Guide Lu4 Manager in any city.

Master Coin NG Foundation — the currency you receive for donations in your Personal Account

  • 10 Master Coins = €1 (Euro). The Master Coin rate is pegged to the euro and may vary against other currencies, depending on the exchange rate of these currencies against the euro
  • Coins are transferred between players and can be listed for purchase or sale in a personal store. This allows you to use the Game Store without investing real money
  • Coins can be selected as a currency for transactions through personal shops and manufactures along with Adena

The project has a loyalty system — bonuses for topping up your balance and cumulative discounts in the Personal Account store.

Magical Support

Lu4 Manager supports new players and provides magical support.

A free character and summon buff is available to players up to level 30 from Adventurers' Guide Lu4 Manager .

Use a pre-made set of buffs or choose from the list provided.

PA holders have access to magical support at any level.

Teleport

Characters up to level 30 have access to free teleports to cities and villages.

Premium Account holders receive free teleportation to cities and villages from the Lu4 Manager, while travel to hunting zones from Gatekeepers in cities costs 50% of the standard teleport price.

Sub to Main

Sometimes, you enjoy a subclass so much that you want to make it your main. On MasterWork, you have this opportunity!

Lu4 Manager's offers a service for changing a subclass to a main.
A special item is required to perform the change — Proof of Magic Transfer Sub to Main NG

The certificate is sold only in your Personal Account on the website, price — Master Coin NG Foundation x 250. After purchasing the Certificate, contact NPC Adventurers' Guide Lu4 Manager to change your class.

Special conditions:

  • Main class and subclass must have 2nd profession
  • If you change your class from Gladiator level 60 to Shilen Knight subclass level 45, your main class will be Shilen Knight level 45. Gladiator level 60 will become your subclass
  • After changing your class, your character's race will change to match the new class
  • Change is available only once a week
