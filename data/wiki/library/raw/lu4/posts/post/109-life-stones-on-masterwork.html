Life Stones on Masterwork

Custom mechanics 09.03.2026


Basics

Life Stones — one of the ways to improve your gear and get some extra stats and (or) skills. Besides that, installed Life Stone protects you from dropping your gear, after being killed by other players.

Specially for MasterWork augmentation system was rebuilt for player's convenience. In a basic Life Stones are divided by level, but on MasterWork we abandoned this division. There is now a single type of Stones, that can be used for augmentation at any level.

Best way to farm Life Stones — instances and bosses of any level. As the reward for killing bosses, and, with a low chance, monsters you will receive Chest with Life Stone NG , that contains all types of Life Stones:

  • Life Stone NG
  • Mid-Grade Life Stone NG
  • High-Grade Life Stone NG
  • Top-Grade Life Stone NG
  • Superior-Grade Life Stone NG Foundation
  • Accessory Life Stone - Necklace NG
  • Accessory Life Stone - Earring NG
  • Accessory Life Stone - Ring NG

You can find the full list of monsters for obtaining Life Stones on the Wikipedia page Chest with Life Stone NG .

Combat skills from Life Stones work according to High Five mechanics, however, they have restrictions on Power and Level, depending on the stage of the server. Life Stone Skills divided by level — 56, 60, 64, 68, 70, 72, 74, 76, 79, 83.

After changing the stage, skills automatically increase in level. Passive and active buffs always have Level 10. This way you will always have the skill at the highest available level.

Insertion and Extraction

You no longer need to find a blacksmith to insert the Life Stone — using the new game client allows you to augment equipment directly from your inventory.

  • Press Augment button in inventory window
  • Select required Life Stone
  • Select an item. The number and type of gems required for augmentation will be indicated at the bottom of the window
  • Insert the Stone. If you are not satisfied with the result — you can immediately insert the next one. Additionally, you will need adena to automatically extract an unsuccessful Stone

To remove Life Stone without installing a new one, ask a blacksmith at any city.

Life Stone with skills

A new type of Life Stones has been added on MasterWork — Superior-Grade Life Stone NG Foundation

When inserting this Life Stone, the weapon is guaranteed (100%) to receive 2 parameters:

  • Bonus to stats or basic characteristics
  • Passive, active or chance item skill

Insertion and removal cost:

  • Insert — x8 off the regular cost for a specific weapon
  • Withdrawal — x8 of the normal cost for a specific weapon

Superior-Grade Life Stone NG Foundation can be exchanged by Pushkin Master Blacksmith and by other blacksmiths.

Jewelry skills

Two similar skills in a same type of jewelry don't stack — the character will only be affected by the effect one of them. For example: two rings for protection from Paralysis will not give double protection, only one, of two identical effects, same as the Ant Queen's ring.

Two similar skills in different type of jewelry stack — both effects will apply to the character. For example: an earring with protection against Paralysis + 7% and a ring with protection against Paralysis + 5% will give a total of +12% protection.

Two different skills in one type of jewelry stacks — both effects will apply to the character. For example: an earring with protection from Paralysis + 7% and an earring with protection from Shock attacks + 7% will give a total of +7% to protection from Paralysis and +7% to protection from Shock.

Pay attention!

Skill bonuses from Life Stones stack with the effects of rare and epic jewelry.

For example: rare Tateossian Necklace S Foundation with Necklace Augment - Paralyze Resistance Lv. 1 in total will give +20% protection against Paralysis.
