Forge of the Gods: stages improvement

Locations 29.04.2025

The volcanic region in the northeastern part of Goddard is known as the gods' cauldron. It's said that rivers of lava flow there! I don't think it's a place you'd want to spend your vacation!

Basics

Back in 2018, the MasterWork team created, and now successfully using a phase-based system for the Forge of the Gods. To open new stages of the location, players need to perform certain actions.

Mechanics

The main action required to upgrade to the next stage is killing the raid boss:

Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80

Before stage 1, Amber can be seen at the Forge of the Gods entrance.

Upgrading the stage

To upgrade to stage 1, you must kill the raid boss Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 .
Spawn Spot: Entrance to the Forge of Gods.

To upgrade to stage 2, you must kill the raid boss Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 3 times.
Spawn Spot: near the spiral, next to the NPC Klein Valakas Watchman

To upgrade to stage 3, you must kill the raid boss Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 3 times.
Spawn Spot: not far from the descent to the lower level of the Forge of Gods.

Downgrading the stage

Location level can't be decreased.

Stages description

The initial stage

  • The entrance to the Forge of Gods is closed
  • Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 стоит перед входом в локацию

1st stage

  • The first halls with stakato are available, up to the room with a spiral
  • The pass to rooms further than the spiral is closed
  • Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 standing in a room with a spiral
  • Klein Valakas Watchman not available

2nd stage

  • All rooms on the top level of the location are available
  • The lower level is closed
  • Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 stands in front of the pass to the lower level
  • Klein Valakas Watchman not available

3rd stage

  • All rooms on the upper and lower levels of the location are available
  • Ember Raid Boss Lv. 80 standing in its usual place
  • Klein Valakas Watchman available to receive a Valakas quest
