Basics

To add variety to gameplay and expand your character’s development options, the Lu4 team has revamped outdated effects from Soul Crystals (SA). The questionable effects Critical Vampire and Rsk. Evasion have been replaced with popular and useful effects such as Health, Focus, and more.

Attention!

For detailed information on the new SA effects, read the article New SA Effects
