Beleth on MasterWork

NPC 13.11.2025

The most formidable mage and malevolent demon ever to walk the earth. Empowered by Hellbound, he has ascended to a level of power so unfathomable that even the gods themselves dare not comprehend it. Recently, Beleth has begun delving into forbidden experiments — melding Chaos with the essence of humanity. But something has shifted. Hellbound trembles under the weight of his actions, and the balance of power teeters on the brink. None can predict his next move.

Basics

Beleth is a majestic Epic Boss introduced to the game with the Hellbound update. Beleth's abode is located on the Hellbound. Players who defeat the Boss will receive an Epic Jewelry reward — Beleth's Ring.

In the basic game, Beleth's Throne Room is on the top level of the Steel Citadel. To enter the Throne Room, players must complete all levels of the Steel Citadel.

The Steel Citadel location is unavailable on MasterWork. The entrance to Beleth's Throne Room has been moved to the Sand Swept Dunes location and is marked on the map as Mystical Device.

The battle with Beleth is not available to MidCompany members.

How to reach Beleth

To access Hellbound, the player must complete a special quest — Path to hellbound.

The entrance to Beleth's Throne Room is in the Sand Swept Dunes location.

Mystical Device Lv. 82 — a mystical device for transporting to the Throne Room. At random time between 21:00 and 21:15, wormholes become active, signalling the opening of the Throne Room.

Beleth Entrance Stone NG — an item for moving through the Wormhole, which must be purchased from one of the NPCs:

  • Budenka Harbor Caravaner
  • Butler MW Premium Store
  • Mystical Device Lv. 82

To purchase, you need Ancient Tome of the Demon NG , which is obtained from all monsters on Hellbound, or some Master Coin NG Foundation and Adena NG

Beleth will appear 5 minutes after the first player enters the Throne Room.

The fight with Beleth has four stages. Depending on the players' actions in Stage 1, Stage 2 can be skipped, or the entire fight can be reset.

Beleth's Throne Room is a NoPK zone. This means that a player can kill another player without affecting their reputation.

Stage 1

Leona Blackbird Fire Dragon Bride Lv. 82 will appear in the center of the hall. At the same time, three illusions of Beleth will appear at the foot of the throne.

Attack one of Beleth's incarnations. The stage will end when one of the copies reach 50% HP. Each of the illusions triggers one of three scenarios:

  1. Stage 2 Activation
  2. Stage 3 Activation — Best Option
  3. End of the Battle — Worst Option

Each illusion follows its own scenario. Only the illusion with the worst scenario can be identified, and Leona will target it.

If you assisted Leona and killed the illusion with the worst scenario — the Throne Room will close, and all players inside the room will be teleported to the Continent.

To successfully complete the raid, you must re-enter the Throne Room.
