Masterwork 23.06.2026


Basics

The Personal Account balance is valid for all project servers. For filling the Personal Account balance, you receive Master Coin NG Foundation .

10 Master Coins = €1 (Euro). The Master Coin rate is pegged to the euro and may vary against other currencies, depending on the exchange rate of these currencies against the euro.

To top up your Personal Account balance:

  • Log in to your Personal Account on the project website
  • Select Balance section in the left menu
  • Specify the amount of Master Coin you want to receive
  • Select a payment system and currency. There is a convenient currency calculator right of the "Pay" button
  • Click the Pay button. You will be redirected to the selected payment system
  • After payment, Master Coin will be credited to your balance within a few minutes to a day. The waiting time depends on the speed of your bank
  • If Master Coin is not received within 24 hours, please contact the Support Service, and they will help you
  • The number of coins on the Balance is shown in the upper right corner of the main page of the Personal Account. After top up, click the Refresh button that will appear when you point the Balance block

Pay attention!

Some payment systems may ask you to submit additional data for verification by the KYC policy.

E-Global does not collect or store this data!

IF MASTER COIN IS NOT CREDITED WITHIN 24 HOURS, PLEASE CONTACT SUPPORT SERVICE

Master Coin in game

Master Coin NG Foundation — game currency used for purchases in the Game Store from NPC Butler MW Premium Store . In addition, coins can be selected as a currency for transactions through private store and manufacture along with Adena.

Coins can be transferred between players and purchased or sold in a private store. That allows you to use the benefits of the Game Shop without investing real money.

To transfer Master Coin NG Foundation from your Personal Account balance to the game:

  • Log in to your Personal Account on the project website
  • Select the desired server
  • Open the Master Coin transfer window at the top of the screen
  • Specify the number of coins
  • Select the game account and character you want to receive coins for
  • Enter PIN. If you do not know the account PIN code, go to Settings - Security and restore the code by email
  • Click the "Transfer" button. Done! The coins will be sent to the character's inventory.

Market balance

Pay attention!

The Market balance and the Personal Account balance are different accounts. You can't pay for purchases on the Market from your Personal Account balance. To make transactions on the Market, you must top up your Market account.

For detailed information on topping up your Market account, read the article Ingame Market.

Bonus code

Active E-Global players often encounter bonus codes in the game and on the forum. Usually, this is a reward for event participation, a compliment bonus for unscheduled maintenance work, or a prize for finding an Easter egg. How can you turn a bonus code into a reward? It's very simple!

  • Log in to your Personal Account on the project website
  • Select the desired server in the upper left part of the screen. The bonus code may work for all servers or only for a specific one. Be careful!
  • At the top of the screen, click on the "Bonus Code" button
  • Enter or paste the copied bonus code into the special field
  • Select the character you want to receive bonuses for
  • Click the "Activate" button
  • Done! The reward for the bonus code has been sent to your character
