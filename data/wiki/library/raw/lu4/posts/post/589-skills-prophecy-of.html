Basics

    • Revelation of Water Lv. 2
  • Shillien Elder

    • Revelation of Wind Lv. 2
  • Warcryer
    • Motive of Victory Lv. 2
    • Chant of Victory Lv. 2
    • Chant of Magnus Lv. 1
  • Overlord
    • Legacy of Pa'agrio Lv. 2 (Legacy of Pa'agrio)
