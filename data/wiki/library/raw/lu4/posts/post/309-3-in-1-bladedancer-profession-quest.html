3 in 1 Bladedancer profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Bladedancer:

  • Quest Trial of the Challenger
  • Quest Testimony of Fate
  • Quest Test of the Duelist

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Bladedancer profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Challenger NG
  • Mark of Fate NG
  • Mark of Duelist NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Oren. Find Kaien Duelist in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Kaien will give:

Order Gludio NG
Order Dion NG
Order Giran NG
Order Oren NG
Order Aden NG

2. Teleport to Giran. Find Kaira Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Kaira will give Kaira's Letter NG

3. Head to the Temple of Giran. Go around the building, find Metheus Brother and talk to him.

Metheus will give Metheus's Funeral Jar NG

4. Teleport to Dion. Behind the Warriors Guild find Kash and talk to him.

5. Exit the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Dead Seeker Lv. 34
Hangman Tree Lv. 35

Until you get:

Dead Seeker Fang NG x 10
Kasandra's Remains NG

6. Teleport to Giran. Find Metheus Brother behind the Temple building and talk to him.

Metheus will give Herbalism Textbook NG

Talk to him again, the dialogue must be carried out twice.

7. Teleport to Dion. Find Ixia Master in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Ixia will give Ixia's List NG

8. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Puncher Lv. 34
Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you get:

Puncher's Shard NG x 10
Tyrant's Blood NG x 10

You can reach the next location on foot.

9. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Head to the rooms near the Epic Boss. Find and kill monsters:

Noble Ant Leader Lv. 38

Until you get 10 Noble Ant's Feeler NG

10. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Bloody Swampland. Head southeast to the waterfall. Find the entrance to the cave and go inside. The entrance to the cave is behind the waterfall near the lake. Find and kill the monster:

Shyslassys Quest Monster Lv. 36

You will receive from the killed monster:

Broken Key NG
Scroll of Shyslassys NG

The key is required to open the chest that appears nearby, on MasterWork there is some Adena inside the chest.

11. Teleport to Dion. Behind the Warriors Guild find Kash and talk to him.

Kash will give Letter of Kash NG

12. Teleport from Dion to Cruma Tower. Head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35

Until you get:

Marsh Spider's Fluids NG x 10
Nightshade Root NG x 10
Drone's Chitin NG x 10

Items drops from all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

13. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Follow the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Dead Seeker Lv. 34

Until you get 10 Dead Seeker Dung NG

14. Teleport to Giran. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you get 10 Medusa's Ichor NG

15. Teleport to Dion. Find Ixia Master in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Ixia will give Belladonna NG

16. Teleport to Giran. Find Martien at the northeastern exit and talk to him.

17. Head behind the Giran Temple building. Find Metheus Brother and talk to him.

Metheus will give Alder's Skull NG

18.1. Head to the Dark Elf Guild of Giran. Find Kaira Magister and talk to her.

Kaira will give Alders Skull NG

18.2. After talking to Kaira, the ghost Alders Spirit will appear. Talk to him.

19. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Roa Magister and talk to her.

Roa will give Alder's Receipt NG

20. Teleport to Dion, then to the Plains of Dion. Head south, find the entrance to the cave and go inside. Find and kill the monster:

Gorr Quest Monster Lv. 36

You will receive from the killed monster Watcher's 1st Eye NG

21. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Norman will give Revelations Manuscript NG

22. Teleport to Giran. Find Kaira Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Kaira will give Kaira's Recommendation NG

23. Head to the northeastern gate of Giran. Find Martien and talk to him.

24.1. Teleport to Dion, then to Bee Hive. Head west to Hive Fortress. Find the cave entrance and go inside. The cave entrance is opposite the eastern wall of the fortress. Find and kill the monster:

Baraham Quest Monster Lv. 36

You will receive from the killed monster Watcher's 2nd Eye NG

24.2. After killing the monster, Raldo Champion will appear nearby. Talk to him.

25. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Filaur Gray Pillar Member in the House of Elders and talk to him.

26.1. At this stage there is a difference between early and late stages of the server.

In the early stages:

Teleport to Mithril Mines. Go deep into the location to the last room.

In the later stages:

Teleport to the Abandoned Coal Mines. Go deep into the location to one of the pits.

There are no differences in the following points.

Find and kill monsters:

Succubus Queen Quest Monster Lv. 34

Until Raldo Champion appears

26.2. Talk to Raldo, he will give Mark of Challenger NG

27. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give:

Palus Charm NG
Thifiell's Letter NG

28.1. Teleport from Dark Elf Village to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Arkenia's Note NG

28.2. In the vicinity of the Altar of Rites find two NPCs and talk to them:

Bloody Pixy , she will give Pixy Garnet NG
Blight Treant , he will give Blight Treant Seed NG

The Bloody Pixy and the Blight Treant also dwell in other places in the Dark Elf lands.

29. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35

Until you get:

Breka Overlord Skull NG x 10
Overlord Necklace NG x 10

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

30. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Fettered Soul Lv. 38
Grandis Lv. 40

Until you get:

Fettered Soul's Chain NG x 10
Grandis' Skull NG x 10

31. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill. Find and kill monsters:

Karul Bugbear Lv. 40
Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42

Until you get:

Karul Bugbear Skull NG x 10
Tamlin Orc's Ring NG x 10
Tamlin Orc's Arrow NG x 10

The next location can be reached on foot through a tunnel in the mountain west of Hunters' Village.

32. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you get:

Leto Overlord Skull NG x 10
Chief's Amulet NG x 10

33. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Monstereye Lv. 41

Until you get 10 Enchanted Monster Eye Meat NG

34. Teleport to Oren. Find Kaien Duelist in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Kaien will give Final Order NG

35. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Grandis Lv. 40

Until you get 3 Grandis' Skin NG

36. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the north-eastern gate and head south along the road. Before reaching the Fortress, find and kill monsters:

Lakin Lv. 44

Until you get 3 Lakin's Mace NG

37. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Go inside, on the first floor of the tower find and kill monsters:

Excuro Lv. 41
Krator Lv. 44

Until you get:

Excuro's Skin NG x 3
Krator's Shard NG x 3

38. Teleport to Oren, exit the city through the western gate. Go down the slope. Find and kill monsters:

Timak Orc Overlord Lv. 45

Until you get 3 Timak Orc's Belt NG

Club Card holders have access to a teleport to the Timak Outpost.

39. Teleport to Oren. Find Kaien Duelist in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Kaien will give Mark of Duelist NG

40. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Bloody Swampland. Find and kill the monster:

Black Willow Lurker Quest Monster Lv. 39

You will receive from the killed monster Black Willow Leaf NG

41.1. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find two NPCs around the Altar and talk to them:

Bloody Pixy , she will give Red Pixy Dust NG
Blight Treant , he will give Blight Treant Sap NG

The Bloody Pixy and the Blight Treant also dwell in other places in the Dark Elf lands.

41.2. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Arkenia's Letter NG

42. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Mark of Fate NG

43. To change your profession, talk to Angus Grand Master in the Dark Elf Guild of Giran.
