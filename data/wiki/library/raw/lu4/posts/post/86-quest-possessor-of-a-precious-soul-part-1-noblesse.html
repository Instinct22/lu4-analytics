Quest Possessor of a Precious Soul, part 1 (noblesse)

Useful quests 07.10.2025


Basic information

The nobility is an important and necessary status to play on a high levels. Noblesse can participate in the Olympiad, gatekeepers open lists of convenient teleports to popular locations, in addition, the character receives new skills:

Skill Description
Noblesse Blessing Lv. 1 Maintains target's buff/de-buff condition even following death and resurrection.
The Blessing of Noblesse and the Amulet of Luck disappear, however.
Summon CP Potion Lv. 1 Summons 20 CP Potions and 20 Greater CP Potions.
Fortune of Noblesse Lv. 1 Allows a party member to avoid negative after-death effects after being killed by a boss or raid monster. Consumes 4 A-Grade Lucky Amulets.
Harmony of Noblesse Lv. 1 Advances to the frontline of the battlefield., increases P. Def. by 3000, Speed by 25%, resistance to buff canceling attacks by 80%, resistance to bow attacks by 10, and resistance to crossbow attacks by 6. Decreases CP by 20%. Can only be used on the battlefield when CP is above 50%. Maximum fighting spirit and HP are limited.
Symphony of Noblesse Lv. 1 Advances to the frontline of the battlefield., increases M. Def. by 4500, Speed by 25%, resistance to buff canceling attacks by 80%, resistance to bow attacks by 10, and resistance to crossbow attacks by 6. Decreases CP by 20%. Can only be used on the battlefield when CP is above 50%. Maximum fighting spirit and HP are limited.
Build Advanced Headquarters Lv. 1 Constructs a high quality encampment that possesses double HP of a standard encampment and assist clan member recovery and battle additions during castle sieges.
Strider Siege Assault Lv. 1 Uses 15005 Power to attack a castle's gate. Can be used while mounted on a Strider.
Wyvern Aegis Lv. 1 P. Def. +7000, M. Def. by 6000 and resistance to buff-canceling attacks by 100%. Can be used while mounted on a Wyvern.

To get the Noblesse status you need to complete a chain of quests Possessor of a Precious Soul, that are available for characters in a subclass status having level 50 and above.

Pay attention!
On MasterWork servers for the Grand Olympiad status of Noblesse IS NOT REQUIRED

For your convenience we have prepared the guide of all parts of the quest Possessor of a Precious Soul in pictures.

Possessor of a Precious Soul, part 1

Start NPC Talien
Start location Aden
Level 50 ~ 75
Type One-time
Reward
Exp NG x 263 043
Virgil's Letter NG
Opens access to the quest Possessor of a Precious Soul, part 2

1. Start NPC Talien is located in Aden near the stairs opposite the entrance to the Temple. Talking to him, we get a quest Possessor of a Precious Soul, part 1

2. Teleport to Giran, run to Gabrielle , who is located in the south part of the central square. Talk to her.

3. Teleport from Giran to the Dragon Valley, we find at the entrance to the valley Gilmore Antharas Watchman , and talk to him.

4. Teleport to Dion, from there to Beehive and run to the Hive Fortress. Against the eastern wall of the fortress in the wall of the gorge we find the entrance to the cave. Enter, kill the monster Baraham Quest Monster Lv. 36 and get Legend of Seventeen NG

5. Return to Aden to Talien , talk to him.

6. Teleport to Hein, run to Grocere Store and talk to Kantabilon Melody Maestro

7. Teleport to Giran, from there to the Antharas' Lair. Turning our backs to the entrance into the lair and run along the left side past the fortress. After descending into the main canyon we find and kill

Maluk Succubus Lv. 59
Maluk Succubus Turen Lv. 60

Kill monsters untill you'll get 10 Malruk Succubus Claw NG . Chance of drop is very low!

7.2 In the High Five update, the Dragon Valley location was changed and the quest monsters were moved. We teleport to Giran or Hunter's Village, from there to Hardin's Academy. We run straight towards the raid boss Tiger King Karuta Raid Boss Lv. 45 . Near the place where the boss appears we find and kill

Maluk Succubus Lv. 59
Maluk Succubus Turen Lv. 60

Kill monsters untill you'll get 10 Malruk Succubus Claw NG . Chance of drop is very low!

8. Return to Hein to Kantabilon Melody Maestro , we talk to him twice, we get Echo Crystal - Forgotten Song NG

9. Teleport to Aden, run to Talien , talk to him.

10. Teleport to the Hunter's Village, run to the Warrior Guild, talk to Stedmiel Master and get Faded Poetry Book NG

11. Return to Aden to Talien , talk to him.

12. We teleport to Rune, run to the Temple, teleport to the second floor. In the left part of the Temple we find a corridor leading to the balcony. We go up, we find the dwarf Virgil , talk to him.

13. There also talk to the orc Ogmar

14. Go down to the ground floor, run to the Mage Guild, find Rahorakti Grand Seer talk to him.

15. Teleport from Rune to Swamp of Screams, find and kill monsters Splinter Stakato Drone Lv. 69 , untill you'll get 5 Crimson Moss NG . Chance of drop is very low!

16. Return to Rune. go to Rahorakti Grand Seer , talk to him and get Rahorakti's Miraculous Medicine NG

17. Go up to the balcony, find the dwarf Kassandra talk to her.

18. There talk to Virgil

19. Teleport to Goddard, find an elf at the eastern gate Caradine and talk to her.

20. In Goddard Blacksmith find Noel Head Blacksmith and talk to him. He asks you to bring 5 Lunargent NG and 1 Hellfire Oil NG .

You can get these items by quest Supplier of Reagents or buy from other players.

Find the right items and talk to Noel Head Blacksmith

21. Return to Caradine , talk to her and get Virgil's Letter NG . Quest completed.
