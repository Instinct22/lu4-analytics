Quest Exploration of Giants Cave, Part 1

Useful quests 14.01.2025


Basics

Giant's Cave quest consists of two parts and is a popular way to obtain equipment recipes.

Quest and rewards, same as the location itself, are different for Interlude and High Five stages.

On MasterWork access to the second part of the quest does not require Ancient Language Dictionary: Intermediate Level NG .

Interlude

Quest guide Exploration of Giants Cave, Part 1

Starting NPC Sobling Head Researcher
Level 51 ~ 63
Type Repeatable
Reward (random)

1. Talk to Sobling Head Researcher at the entrance to the Giant's Cave and accept the task.

2. Go to the upper level of the Giant's Cave and kill the monsters:

Yintzu Lv. 56
Paliote Lv. 57
Hamrut Lv. 58
Kranrot Lv. 59

Killed monsters will drop Ancient Parchment NG . Open them by a double click, to receive one of the items:
