Quest Trial of the Guildsman

Quests to change class 23.06.2026

In order to become a full-fledged member of Dwarven society, a Dwarven apprentice must first prove himself worthy by creating 7 journeyman rings.

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Trial of the Guildsman Quest guide

To complete the quest, you need the following materials:

Steel NG x 7
Varnish NG x 70
Crystal (D-Grade) D x 70

To save time and money, prepare them in advance.

1. The starting NPC Valkon Warehouse Keeper is located in the Giran Warehouse. Talk to Valkon to start the quest.

Will need to pay 2000 Adena NG

Valkon will give Valkon's Recommendation NG

2. Teleport to Dion. Exit the town through the eastern gate. Head east along the road to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Mandragora Sprout Lv. 20
Mandragora Sprout Lv. 21
Mandragora Sapling Lv. 23
Mandragora Blossom Lv. 25

Until you get Mandragora Berry NG

You can get the quest item from killed monsters without visiting Talking Island Village. Ignore the quest marker and the mark on the minimap.

3. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Altran Blacksmith in the Blacksmith's Shop and talk to him.

Altran will give:

Altran's Instructions NG
Altran's 1st Recommendation NG
Altran's 2nd Recommendation NG
Recipe: Journeyman Ring NG

Find and use Recipe: Journeyman Ring NG in your inventory. This recipe is now registered in your crafting book and allows you to create Journeyman Ring NG . You can open the book using the Dwarven Craft Lv. 1 skill.

4. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give:

Norman's Instructions NG
Norman's Receipt NG

5. Teleport to Gludio. Find Pinter Blacksmith in the Forge and talk to him.

Pinter will give Pinter's Instructions NG

Artisan dwarves will additionally receive Recipe: Amber Bead NG . Register the recipe.

6. Teleport from Gludio to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Captain Lv. 31
Ant Lv. 30
Ant Overseer Lv. 32

Until you collect Amber Bead NG x 70

Artisan dwarves can receive an additional quest item from killed monsters — Amber Lump NG . Using the previously obtained recipe, you can turn 1 Piece of Amber into 5 Amber Beads.

Scavenger dwarves can use the skill Spoil Lv. 4 to obtain additional Amber Beads.

7. Return to Pinter Blacksmith and talk to him.

Pinter will give Journeyman Deco Beads NG x 7

8. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Duning Blacksmith in the Blacksmith's shop and talk to him.

Duning will give Duning's Instructions NG

9. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Lv. 31
Breka Orc Archer Lv. 32
Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34
Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35
Breka Orc Warrior Lv. 33

Until you collect Duning's Key NG x 30

10. Return to Duning Blacksmith and talk to him.

11. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give Norman's List NG

12. Teleport to Dion and exit the city through the eastern gate. Follow the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Strain Lv. 31
Ghoul Lv. 32
Dead Seeker Lv. 34
Granite Golem Lv. 33

Until you collect:

Gray Bone Powder NG x 70
Red Pigment NG x 70
Granite Whetstone NG x 70

13. Teleport to Dion, then to the Plains of Dion. Head east towards the Monster Race Track. Find and kill monsters:

Silenos Lv. 30

Until you collect Braided Yarn NG x 70

14. Teleport to Gludin Village, find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give Journeyman Gem NG x 7

Open the crafting book and create Journeyman Ring NG x 7

15. Teleport to Giran. Find Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

In a dialogue, always choose the first line:

"Every Dwarf must work tirelessly (I learned the mind attitude and virtues that one should possess as a faithful union member)."

If you choose another line, you will receive twice less experience, SP and Adena for completing the quest.

Valkon will complete the quest and give you Mark of Guildsman NG
