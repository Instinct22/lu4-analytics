Quest Audience with the Land Dragon (Pass to Antharas) 50+

Useful quests 14.04.2025

Audience with the Land Dragon quest guide

Starting NPC Gabrielle
Level 50 ~ 85
Type Repeatable
Reward

Portal Stone NG

Item needed to activate the teleport crystal that teleports to Antharas' Lair.

1. Starting NPC Gabrielle located in Giran.

Talk to Gabrielle to begin the quest.

Gabrielle will give a Feather of Gabrielle NG .

2. Teleport to the Forest of Mirror and hunt:

Harit Lizardman Shaman Lv. 54
Harit Lizardman Matriarch Lv. 55

Kill monsters until the quest monster appears:

Harit Lizardman Zealot Quest Monster Lv. 55

From a killed quest monster you will receive the following:

Totem of Land Dragon NG

3. Teleport to the Forsaken Plains and kill monsters:

Marsh Stalker Lv. 54
Marsh Drake Lv. 55

From killed monsters, you will receive:

Marsh Stalker Horn NG
Marsh Drake Talons NG

4. In the Forsaken Plains kill:

Hamrut Lv. 58
Kranrot Lv. 59

From killed monsters, you will receive:

Hamrut Leg NG
Kranrot Skin NG

During the Interlude stage monsters are located in the Giants Cave.

5. Teleport to the Forbidden Gateway and kill:

Blood Queen Lv. 60

Kill monsters until the quest monster appears:

Ghost of Offering Quest Monster Lv. 20

From a killed quest monster you will receive the following:

Remains of Sacrifice NG

6. Talk to Kendra Master at the Aden Warriors Guild.

Kendra will give Mark of Watchman NG .

7. Talk to Orven High Priest in the Aden Temple.

Orven will give Mark of Watchman NG .

8. Talk to Chakiris Prefect at the Orc Guild in Hunters Village.

Chakiris will give Mark of Watchman NG .

9. Talk to Kaiena Magister on the 4th floor of the Ivory Tower.

Kaiena will give Mark of Watchman NG .

10. Return to Gabrielle and talk to her.

11. Teleport to Windy Hill.

Head north, find and kill the quest monster:

Abyssal Jewel 1 Quest Monster Lv. 60

From killed quest monsters you will receive:

1st Fragment of Abyss Jewel NG

The guards will appear after killing a quest monster:

Jewel Guardian Mara Quest Monster Lv. 53

From the killed guard you will receive:

Mara Fang NG

12. Talk to Moke Warehouse Chief in the Warehouse in Gludin Village.

Moke will give a Mark of Watchman NG .

13. Teleport to the Sea of Spores.

Don't go inside. Head south from the entrance, find and kill the quest monster:

Abyssal Jewel 2 Quest Monster Lv. 60

From killed quest monsters you will receive:

2nd Fragment of Abyss Jewel NG

The guards will appear after killing a quest monster:

Jewel Guardian Musfel Quest Monster Lv. 53

From the killed guard you will receive:

Musfel Fang NG

14. Talk to Helton Blacksmith at Oren's Blacksmiths Shop.

Helton will give Mark of Watchman NG .

15. Return to Gabrielle and talk to her.

Gabriel will give Herald of Slayer NG .

16. Talk to Gilmore Antharas Watchman at the entrance to Dragon Valley.

17. In the Dragon Valley find and kill the following monsters:

Cave Maiden Lv. 59
Cave Keeper Lv. 58

Kill monsters until the quest monster appears:

Abyssal Jewel 3 Quest Monster Lv. 60

From a killed quest monster you will receive the following:

3rd Fragment of Abyss Jewel NG

There is no need to kill the guards.

At the Interlude stage, monsters are located in the depths of the location, not far from the entrance to Antharas' Lair.

In the later stages, the monsters are located to the north from the entrance to the Dragon Valley.

18. Talk to Theodric Antharas Watchman at the entrance to the Antharas' Lair.

Theodric will give a Portal Stone NG .

A Portal Stone NG can be purchased from Butler MW Premium Store .
