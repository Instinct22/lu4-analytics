Game Event: King of the Hill

Locations 11.12.2024


Basics

One of the most important but undervalued resources is reputation points (Fame). As a rule, accumulating fame is a boring procedure associated with afk in a siege zone. Familiar, but lame.

MasterWork team has developed a special event that allows you to get reputation points while exping or farming — "King of the Hill".

Some popular locations are labeled as "Hill". Your task is to gather and keep more clan members in the area, than any other clan does.

  • Only members of a clan that is "at war" status can participate
  • To capture, you must have more alive members of your clan than any other, but at least 7

When event conditions are met, an advertisement [ClanName] has captured [location]. All hail the King!" appears, and the icon of the winning clan appears on the area flag.

When the "king of the hill" is overthrown or players leave the location, there will be another announcement "Current there is no King of the [location]. Gather your forces to claim it!"

As a reward, all members of the invader clan receive fame points, the amount depends on time and location.

  • Every alive clan member who is in a location receives a special buff indicating that the player is a defender of location and allows him to gain reputation points
  • Buff duration and location status check interval - 5 minutes
  • Reputation Points are awarded once every 5 minutes and the number is increased when you have a Premium Account
