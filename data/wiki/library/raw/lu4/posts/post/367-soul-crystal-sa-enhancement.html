Soul Crystal (SA) Enhancement

Items 29.06.2026

Basics

Soul Crystals, better known as SA (Special Abilities), gives the weapon additional properties.

On Lu4, Soul Crystals can be inserted into D, C, or B-grade weapons to obtain one of three effects. Dual weapons (swords, daggers, blunts) receive a specific SA effect from enchantment up to level 4 and above.

Soul Crystals cannot be inserted into weapons without a grade (NoGrade).

You can insert a Soul Crystal into a weapon by contacting a Blacksmith in any city.

You can remove the Soul Crystal from your weapon at the town Blacksmith.

Pay attention!

When the SA effect is removed, the Soul Crystal does not return.

How to upgrade the Soul Crystal?

To obtain a Soul Crystal of the required level, you need to infuse it with monster souls. To do this, purchase one of the level 0 crystals from the Blacksmith in any city.

In the Lu4 project, Soul Crystal leveling was reworked. To level up your Soul Crystal, purchase one of the level 0 crystals.

Blacksmiths will offer you an empty Soul Crystal in one of three colors: blue, green, or red. You can only purchase one crystal at a time. To purchase additional crystals, speak with the blacksmith again, but be careful — you only need one crystal in your inventory to upgrade a crystal.

To upgrade your Soul Crystal, find the required monster (see the table below) and kill it. You don't need to use the Crystal on the monster.

The chance of absorbing a soul isn't 100%, so for each crystal level, you'll have to kill anywhere from 1 to several dozen monsters.

Pay attention!

On Lu4 you don't need to use the Soul Crystal, just have it in your inventory.

To successfully absorb a soul, you must have only 1 soul crystal in your inventory!

If there are multiple crystals, you'll see a failure message due to crystal resonance. Make sure you've removed all unnecessary crystals from your inventory before starting leveling.

The SA leveling quest is activated for your character upon logging into the game. You don't need to accept the quest from an NPC. If the quest was cancelled manually, you must log back into the game to activate it.
