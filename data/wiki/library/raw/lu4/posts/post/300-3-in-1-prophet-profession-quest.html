3 in 1 Prophet profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Prophet:

  • Quest Trial of the Pilgrim
  • Quest Testimony of Trust
  • Quest Test of the Reformer

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Prophet profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Pilgrim NG
  • Mark of Trust NG
  • Mark of Reformer NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Oren, find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give:

Letter to Elf NG
Letter to Dark Elf NG

2. Teleport to Giran. Find Pupina Priestess in the Temple and talk to her.

Pupina will give Book of Reform NG

3. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Head east along the road to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Nameless Revenant Quest Monster Lv. 38

Until you collect 7 Ripped Diary NG

After collecting 7 diary fragments, a quest monster will appear:

Aruraune Quest Monster Lv. 40

Kill the monster and get Huge Nail NG

4. Return to Pupina Priestess and talk to her.

Pupina will give Letter of Introduction NG

5. Teleport to Dion, then to Plains of Dion. Near the teleport point find Sla Preacher and talk to her.

Sla will give Sla's Letter NG

6. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Voucher of Trial NG

7. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

8. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave, keep to the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

9. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Forgotten Temple. Head inside, to the fire part of the temple. In the last room, turn left, find and kill monsters:

Lava Salamander Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Essence of Flame NG

10. Return to Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Spirit of Flame NG

11. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

12. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Gauri Twinklerock near the Blacksmith Shop and talk to her.

Gauri will give Tag of Rumor NG

Head to the northern gate of the village. Find Dorf Wanderer and talk to him.

Dorf will give Gray Badge NG

13. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Order of Asterios NG

14. Teleport from Elven Village to Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Dryad Lv. 13
Dryad Elder Lv. 14

Until the quest monster appears:

Actea Of Verdant Wilds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

15. Go deep into the Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Lirein Lv. 18
Lirein Elder Lv. 19

Until the quest monster appears:

Luell Of Zephyr Winds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

16. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Scroll of Elf Trust NG

17. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Letter from Thifiell NG

18. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Bloody Swampland. Head east, at the foot of the mountains find Katari and talk to her.

After the conversation, two NPCs will appear:

Ol Mahum Pilgrim — Quest NPC
Ol Mahum Inspector Quest Monster Lv. 38 — Quest monster

Kill the inspector monster, then talk to the pilgrim, he will give Ol Mahum's Money NG

Talk to Katari again, after the conversation the quest monster will appear:

Ol Mahum Betrayer Quest Monster Lv. 36

Kill the betrayer monster and get Letter of Betrayer NG

If necessary, use Dryad Root Lv. 9 to kill the traitor.

Talk to Katari again, she will give Katari's Letter NG

19. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

Clayton will give Order of Clayton NG

20. Teleport from Dion to Plains of Dion. Near the teleportation point find Sla Preacher and talk to her.

Sla will give:

Greetings NG
Letter of Greetings NG
Letter of Greetings NG

21. Teleport to Giran. Find Primos Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

22. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Blood NG

10 vials of blood will turn into Basilisk Plasma NG

23. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave, find and kill monsters:

Ant Soldier Lv. 35

Until you collect 10 Giant Aphid NG

10 aphid will turn into Honey Dew NG

Quest item obtained from all ant monsters inside the Ant Nest

24. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Around the tower find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31

Until you collect 10 Stakatos Fluids NG

10 fluids will turn into Stakato Ichor NG

Quest item dropped by all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

25. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

26. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Scroll of Dark Elf Trust NG

27. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Immortal Plateau, South. Go along the road to the northwest, at the fork turn to the northeast. On the hill on the right find Kakan and talk to her.

After the conversation, two NPCs will appear:

Ol Mahum Pilgrim — Quest NPC
Crimson Werewolf Quest Monster Lv. 36 — Quest Monster

Kill the werewolf monster, then talk to Kakan. She will give Kakan's Letter NG

28. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Exit the village through the north-eastern gate. Cross the bridge and follow the road to the east. Near the village, find Nyakuri and talk to her.

After the conversation, two NPCs will appear:

Ol Mahum Pilgrim — Quest NPC
Krudel Lizardman Quest Monster Lv. 36 — Quest monster

Kill the lizard monster, then talk to Nyakuri. She will give Nyakuri's Letter NG

29. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Petron Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Petron will give Picture of Nahir NG

30. Teleport from Talking Island Village to Singing Waterfall. Inside the cave find Ramus and talk to him.

Ramus will give Undead List NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

31. Teleport to Talking Island Village, then to Elven Ruins. Head inside the dungeon. Find and kill monsters:

Misery Skeleton Lv. 14
Skeleton Archer Lv. 13
Skeleton Marksman Lv. 14
Skeleton Lord Lv. 15
Silent Horror Lv. 16

Until you collect:

Bone Fragment NG
Bone Fragment NG
Bone Fragment NG
Bone Fragment NG
Bone Fragment NG

Each monster drops only one unique quest item.

32. Head to the room next to the Raid Boss in the Elven Ruins. Find and kill the monsters:

Nahir Quest Monster Lv. 35

Until you get Hair of Nahir NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

33. Teleport to Talking Island Village, then to the Singing Waterfall. Inside the cave find Ramus and talk to him.

Ramus will give Ramus's Letter NG

34. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Petron Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Petron will give Statue of Einhasad NG

35. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Andellia near the teleport point and talk to her.

36. Teleport from Elven Village to Neutral Zone. Head north to the waterfall, north of the lake find and kill monsters:

Black Willow Quest Monster Lv. 30

Until you get Debris of Willow NG

On the northern shore of the lake find Uruha and talk to her.

Uruha will give Book of Darkness NG

37. Return to Andellia and talk to her.

38. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Wasteland. Go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Book of Sage NG

39. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Santiago Hermit near the house and talk to him.

Santiago will give Mark of Pilgrim NG

40. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Letter to Seresin NG

Talk to Seresin Cardinal at the entrance to the Temple of Oren

Seresin will give:

Letter to Dwarf NG
Letter to Orc NG

41. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Letter to Manakia NG

42. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

43. Teleport from Giran to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39

Until you collect 10 Parasite of Lota NG

44. Return to Manakia Seer and talk to him.

Manakia will give Letter of Manakia NG

45. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Scroll of Orc Trust NG

46. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Letter to Nikola NG

47. Teleport to Dion, then to the Plains of Dion. Near the teleport point find Sla Preacher and talk to her.

Sla will give Mark of Reformer NG

48. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of inside, head southwest. Find Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give Order of Nikola NG

49. Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower find and kill monsters:

Porta Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Heartstone of Porta NG

50. Return to Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

51. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Scroll of Dwarf Trust NG

52. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Recommendation of Hollint NG

53. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Biotin High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Biotin will give Mark of Trust NG

54. To change your profession, talk to Maximilian High Priest in the Temple of Giran.
