Update: Hellbound. Anomic Foundry (78~85)

Locations 25.04.2025

The desert climate led to a serious problem: what would the natives now eat? The answer was obvious: fish. But Beleth did not trust anyone — fishermen could reach the mainland and inform the Ivory Tower mages about him. Therefore, Beleth created his own "fishermen". To the south of the Steel Citadel, he established a factory known among the natives as the "Anomic Foundry," where he created a large number of monsters, resembling sharks but with two arms and two legs, so they could serve a dual purpose: as soldiers, slave overseers, and more, strengthening his hold over the island.

Basic information

In the western part of the Hellbound is the Anomic Foundry, where in the primeval flame of lava springs is melted metal for the Steel Citadel. Hundreds of slaves working on mining and a whole army of guards protects them. At times a dangerous undead appears from the disturbed bowels to get rid of uninvited guests.

Anomic Foundry — an extremely dangerous place, only a few brave men dare to fight with local inhabitants in search of a mysterious weapon.

This season, the MasterWork team has developed the Anomic Foundry location, now it works as it was designed by Korean developers. In addition, the location added a new qyest and updated drop from monsters.

The location is hard, suitable for the farming by full group. In some places you can place a small group or solo, to kill passing undead.

Pay attenion!

For comfortable exping use skills Banish Undead Lv. 1 and Turn Undead Lv. 1 , they must be ehcnanted to +17...+21.

This requirement is due to the high level of monsters in the location.

Location mechanics

There are melting furnaces in the area around which the Foundry Laborers stand. From each impact of the worker the chance of undead Lesser Evil is triggered. If Foundry Laborer deals damage — it will work faster and faster Undead appearances increase. If Foundry Laborer is killed — the speed of undead appearance will significantly decrease.

When the player deals damage to Foundry Laborer, all nearby Brigadiers and Evil rush to the aggressor. Be careful!

The monsters of Lesser Evil run through the entire area, moving from the furnace to the furnace. As each furnace passes, there is a chance that the monster will turn into the Greater Evil. Reaching the end of the location undead disappear.
