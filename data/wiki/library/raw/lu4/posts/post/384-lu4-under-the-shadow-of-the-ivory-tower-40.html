Quest guide

Pay attention!

To start the quest, a second profession is required.

The quest is only available for Mystic and Orc Shaman classes.

Quest items will not be given to characters whose level exceeds the required level by 9 or more.

1. The starting NPC Cema Magic Trader is located in the Hardin's Academy dungeon. Talk to Cema to start the quest.

2. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Instead going inside, go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Monstereye Lv. 41
Enchanted Stone Golem Lv. 42
Enchanted Iron Golem Lv. 43
Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

To get Nebulite Orb NG

3. Once you have enough, return to Cema Magic Trader and talk to her to exchange the quest items for adena or various goods.

Cema will give Adena NG x 50 for each Nebulite Orb NG

Mini-games

Nebulite Orb NG can be additionally obtained for winning games.

Rock - Paper - Scissors

Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. At the foot of the Tower find Marsha Collector and talk to her.

Play Rock - Paper - Scissors, for each victory Marsha will give you double the reward.

Bet: Nebulite Orb NG x 10.

Loss: -10 spheres
Draw: Refund
Win: +10 spheres

Game Rules:

  • Rock beats Scissors
  • Scissors beats Paper
  • Paper beats Rock

Coin Toss

Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. In the south find Trumpin Collector and talk to him.

Play Coin Toss, a winning streak will increase your prize. Maximum 5 wins in a row.

Starting bid: Nebulite Orb NG x 10.

Loss: -10 spheres
Consecutive wins:
1 Win: +10 spheres
2 Wins: +25 spheres
3 Wins: +60 spheres
4 Wins: +130 spheres
5 Wins: +270 spheres

Rules of the game:

  • You need to guess which side of the Trumpin coin will land

Lottery

Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Without entering, go down into the crater and find Marsha Collector at the foot of the Tower, and talk to her.

Play the Lottery! Receive a guaranteed random prize.

Cost: Nebulite Orb NG x 150.
