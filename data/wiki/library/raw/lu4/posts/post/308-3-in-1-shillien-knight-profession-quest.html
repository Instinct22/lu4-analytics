3 in 1 Shillien Knight profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Shillien Knight:

  • Quest Trial of Duty
  • Quest Testimony of Fate
  • Quest Test of Witchcraft

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Shillien Knight profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Duty NG
  • Mark of Fate NG
  • Mark of Witchcraft NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Giran. Find Hannavalt Grand Master (Hannavalt) in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

2. Head to the Dark Elf Guild of Giran. Find Kaira Magister and talk to her.

Kaira will give Kaira's Letter NG

3. Head to the Temple of Giran. Go around the building, find Metheus Brother and talk to him.

Metheus will give Metheus's Funeral Jar NG

4. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to Dragon Valley. Go north to Death Pass. Find Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give Orim's Diagram NG

5. Teleport to Giran. Find Alexandria Armor Merchant in the Luxury Goods Shop and talk to her.

Alexandria will give Alexandria's Book NG

6. Head to the Dark Elf Guild of Giran. Find Kaira Magister and talk to her.

Kaira will give Aklantoth 2nd Gem NG

7. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Iker Magister and talk to him.

Iker will give Iker's List NG

8. Teleport to Oren, then to the Sea of Spores. Find and kill monsters:

Dire Wyrm Lv. 42

Until you get 20 Dire Wyrm Fang NG

9. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Lv. 35
Leto Lizardman Archer Lv. 36
Leto Lizardman Soldier Lv. 37
Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39
Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you get 20 Leto Lizardman Charm NG

10. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Stone Golem Lv. 42

Until you get 20 Enchanted Stone Golem Heartstone NG

11. Teleport to Giran. Find Iker Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Iker will give Aklantoth 1st Gem NG

12. Teleport to Dion. Find Lara Grocer in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Lara will give Lara's Memo NG

13. Exit the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east of the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Nameless Revenant Quest Monster Lv. 38
Hangman Tree Lv. 35

Until you get:

Aklantoth 3rd Gem NG
Kasandra's Remains NG

14. Teleport to Giran. Find Metheus Brother behind the Temple building and talk to him.

Metheus will give Herbalism Textbook NG

Talk to him again, the dialogue must be carried out twice.

15. Teleport to Dion. Find Ixia Master in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Ixia will give Ixia's List NG

16. Teleport from Dion to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Marsh Stakato Soldier Lv. 33

Until you get:

Marsh Spider's Fluids NG x 10
Nightshade Root NG x 10

Items drops from all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

17.1. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Nestle Accessory Merchant in the Grocery Store and talk to him.

Nestle will give Nestle's Memo NG

17.2. Talk to Leopold across from the entrance to the Grocery Store.

Leopold will give Leopold's Journal NG

18. Teleport from Gludin Village to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Instead of going inside, head northeast along the coast. Find Sir Aron Tanford and talk to him.

Sir Aron will give Old Knight Sword NG

19. Head deep into the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Skeleton Raider Lv. 30
Skeleton Marauder Lv. 29

Until the quest monster appears:

Spirit of Sir Herod Quest Monster Lv. 35

Kill the ghost with Old Knight Sword NG

Kill the ghost by Old Knight Sword NG

Skeleton monsters can be killed with any weapon, the quest ghost monster that appears must be killed with the quest sword.

20. In the depths of the Wastelands, find and kill monsters:

Skeletal Mercenary Quest Monster Lv. 39
Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you get:

Aklantoth 4th Gem NG
Aklantoth 5th Gem NG
Aklantoth 6th Gem NG
Tyrant's Blood NG x 10

21. Return to Sir Aron Tanford and talk to him.

22. Teleport to Giran, then to the entrance to the Valley of Dragons. Go north to the Death Pass. Find Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give 1st Brimstone NG

After the conversation, a quest monster will appear nearby:

Drevanul Prince Zeruel Quest Monster Lv. 42

Attack the monster and it will disappear immediately.

Talk to Orim again, Orim will give:

Orim's Instructions NG
Orim's 1st Letter NG
Orim's 2nd Letter NG

23. Teleport to Giran, exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you get 10 Medusa's Ichor NG

24. Teleport to Dion. Find Sir Kiel Nighthawk in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

25. Leave the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Dead Seeker Lv. 34
Strain Lv. 31
Ghoul Lv. 32

Until you get:

Dead Seeker Dung NG x 10
Report Piece NG x 10

10 report pieces will turn into Report Piece NG

26. Return to Sir Kiel Nighthawk and talk to him.

Sir Kiel will give Mirror of Orpic NG

27. Leave the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Hangman Tree Lv. 35

Until Spirit Of Sir Talianus appears

Talk to the Spirit of Sir Talianus, he will give Tear of Confession NG

28. Return to Sir Kiel Nighthawk (Sir Kiel Nighthawk) and talk to him.

29. Head to the Dark Elf Guild of Dion. Find Ixia Master and talk to her.

Ixia will give Belladonna NG

30. Teleport to Giran. Find Metheus Brother behind the Temple building and talk to him.

Metheus will give Alder's Skull NG

31.1. Head to the Dark Elf Guild of Giran. Find Kaira Magister and talk to her.

Kaira will give Alders Skull NG

31.2. After talking to Kaira, the ghost Alders Spirit will appear. Talk to him.

32. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Roa Magister and talk to her.

Roa will give Alder's Receipt NG

33. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Sir Klaus Vasper at the entrance to the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sir Klaus will give Sir Vasper's Letter NG

34. Head to the Gludin Village Warehouse building. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper and talk to him.

Norman will give Revelations Manuscript NG

35. Teleport to Giran. Find Kaira Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Kaira will give Kaira's Recommendation NG

36. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Iker Magister and talk to him.

Iker will give:

Iker's Amulet NG
Soultrap Crystal NG

37. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give:

Palus Charm NG
Thifiell's Letter NG

38.1. Teleport from Dark Elf Village to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Arkenia's Note NG

38.2. In the vicinity of the Altar of Rites find two NPCs and talk to them:

Bloody Pixy , she will give Pixy Garnet NG
Blight Treant , he will give Blight Treant Seed NG

The Bloody Pixy and Blight Treant also inhabit other areas in the Dark Elf lands.

39. Teleport to Oren. Find Vadin Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Vadin will give Vadin's Crucifix NG

40. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Isael Silvershadow in the main square and talk to her.

41. Exit Hunter's Village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill. Find and kill monsters:

Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42
Karul Bugbear Lv. 40

Until you get:

Tamlin Orc Amulet NG x 20
Karul Bugbear Skull NG x 10

The next location can be reached on foot through a tunnel in the mountain west of Hunters' Village.

42. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you get:

Leto Overlord Skull NG x 10
Militas Article NG x 20

The Items are dropped from all Leto Lizardman monsters in the Plains of the Lizardmen.

43. Teleport to Oren. Find Vadin Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Vadin will give Vadin's Sanctions NG

44. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Isael Silvershadow in the main square and talk to her.

Isael will give Tear of Loyalty NG

45. Teleport to Giran. Find Dustin Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

46. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35

Until you get:

Athebaldt's Skull NG
Athebaldt's Ribs NG
Athebaldt's Shin NG
Breka Overlord Skull NG x 10

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

47. Return to Dustin Priest and talk to him.

Dustin will give Saints Ashes Urn NG

48. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Grandis Lv. 40

Until you get 10 Grandis' Skull NG

49. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Sir Klaus Vasper at the entrance to the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sir Klaus will give Sword of Binding NG

50. Teleport to Takling Island Village, then to the Obelisk of Victory. Find Sir Collin Windawood and talk to him.

Sir Collin will give Letter of Windawood NG

51. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Bloody Swampland. Find and kill the monster:

Black Willow Lurker Quest Monster Lv. 39

From the killed monster you get Black Willow Leaf NG

52.1. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to Altar of Rites. Find two NPCs in the vicinity of the Altar and talk to them:

Bloody Pixy , she will give Red Pixy Dust NG
Blight Treant , he will give Blight Treant Sap NG

The Bloody Pixy and Blight Treant also inhabit other areas in the Dark Elf lands.

52.2. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Arkenia's Letter NG

53. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Mark of Fate NG

54. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village (Club Card holders can teleport to the village). Find Evert Fisher at the village pier and talk to him.

Evert will give 2nd Brimstone NG

After the conversation, a quest monster will appear nearby:

Drevanul Prince Zeruel Quest Monster Lv. 42

Kill a demon by Sword of Binding NG

After the demon dies you will receive:

Purgatory Key NG
Zeruel Bind Crystal NG

55. Teleport to Giran. Find Dustin Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Dustin will give Letter of Dustin NG

56. Head to the Giran Warriors Guild. Find Hannavalt Grand Master and talk to him.

Hannavalt will give Mark of Duty NG

57. Teleport from Giran to the entrance of Dragon Valley. Go north to Death Pass. Find Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give Mark of Witchcraft NG

58. To change your profession, talk to Angus Grand Master in the Dark Elf Guild of Giran.
