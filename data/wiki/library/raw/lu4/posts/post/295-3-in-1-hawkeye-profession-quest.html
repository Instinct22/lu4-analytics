3 in 1 Hawkeye profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Hawkeye:

  • Trial of the Seeker
  • Testimony of Trust
  • Test of Sagittarius

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Hawkeye profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Seeker NG
  • Mark of Trust NG
  • Mark of Sagittarius NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Bernard Guild President in the central building and talk to him.

Bernard will give Bernard's Introduction NG

2. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give:

Letter to Elf NG
Letter to Dark Elf NG

3. Teleport to Giran. Find Dufner Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Dufner will give Dufners Letter NG

4. Teleport to Dion. Find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give Terry's 1st Order NG

5. Exit the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Neer Ghoul Berserker Lv. 29

Until you get Mysterious Spirit Ore NG

6. Return to Terry Master and talk to him.

Terry will give Terry's 2nd Order NG

7. Teleport from Dion to Fortress of Resistance. Find and kill monsters:

Ol Mahum Captain Lv. 30

Until you get Ol Mahum Spirit Ore NG

8. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village (Club Card holders can teleport to the village). Find Hamil Sagittarius near the mill south of the village and talk to him.

Hamil will give Hamil's 1st Letter NG

9. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Instead of going inside, head northeast along the coast. Find Sir Aron Tanford and talk to him.

10. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Find and kill monsters:

Turek Orc Warlord Lv. 30

Until you get Turek Spirit Ore NG

11. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Order of Asterios NG

12. Teleport from Elven Village to Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Dryad Lv. 13
Dryad Elder Lv. 14

Until the quest monster appears:

Actea Of Verdant Wilds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

13. Go deep into the Elven Forest. Find and kill monsters:

Lirein Lv. 18
Lirein Elder Lv. 19

Until the quest monster appears:

Luell Of Zephyr Winds Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the monster and get Seed of Verdure NG

14. Teleport to Elven Village. Find Asterios Hierarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Asterios will give Scroll of Elf Trust NG

15. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Letter from Thifiell NG

16. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

Clayton will give Order of Clayton NG

17. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Captain Lv. 31

Until you collect:

Ant Spirit Ore NG
Hunter's 1st Rune NG x 10
Giant Aphid NG x 10

10 aphid will turn in a Honey Dew NG

Aphids and Runes are obtained from all ant monsters inside the Ant Nest.

18. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Hamil Sagittarius near the mill south of the village and talk to him.

Hamil will give Hamil's 2nd Letter NG

19. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

20. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34
Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35

Until you collect:

Hunter's 2nd Rune NG x 10
Talisman of Snake NG x 1

You can reach the next location on foot.

21. Teleport to Giran. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge and follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Turak Bugbear Warrior Lv. 33

Until you get Turak Bugbear Spirit Ore NG

22. Teleport to Giran, then to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Guardian Basilisk Blood NG

10 vials of blood will turn into Basilisk Plasma NG

23. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

24. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Outside the tower, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31

Until you collect 10 Stakatos Fluids NG

10 fluids will turn into Stakato Ichor NG

Quest item dropped by all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

25. Teleport to Dion. Find Clayton Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

26. Head to Dion's Warrior Guild. Find Terry Master and talk to him.

Terry will give Terry's Box NG

26. Leave Dion Town through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Hamil Sagittarius near the mill south of the village and talk to him.

Hamil will give Hamil's 3rd Letter NG

27. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Scroll of Dark Elf Trust NG

28. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Viktor Weapon Merchant in the Weapon Shop and talk to him.

Select the second line in the dialogue "Altebar Karryos"

Viktor will give Viktor's Request NG

29. Teleport to Giran. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden. Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect 10 Medusa Scales NG

30. Return to Viktor Weapon Merchant and talk to him.

Viktor will give:

Shilen's Spirit Ore NG
Analysis Request NG

31. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Letter to Seresin NG

Talk to Seresin Cardinal at the entrance to the Temple of Oren

Seresin will give:

Letter to Dwarf NG
Letter to Orc NG

32. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Find Marina Magister on the second floor and talk to her.

Marina will give Marina's Letter NG

33. Teleport to the third floor of the Ivory Tower. Find Gauen Magister and talk to him.

34. Go down into the crater under the Ivory Tower. Find and kill monsters:

Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

Until you get Manashen's Horn NG

35. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Brunon Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Brunon will give Experiment Tools NG

36. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Letter to Manakia NG

37. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

38. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Road Scavenger Lv. 37

Until you get Mithril Clip NG

39. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39

Until you collect 10 Parasite of Lota NG

40. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Manakia will give Letter of Manakia NG

41. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Find Marina Magister on the second floor and talk to her.

Marina will give Analysis Result NG

42. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of Kings and talk to him.

Kakai will give Scroll of Orc Trust NG

43. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Letter to Nikola NG

44. Teleport to Dion. Find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give List of Host NG

45. Teleport from Dion to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head southwest. Find Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give Order of Nikola NG

46. Head northeast to the Cruma Marshlands. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35
Marsh Stakato Soldier Lv. 33
Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31

Until you collect:

Reinforced Bowstring NG
Stakato Chitin NG
Abyss Spirit Ore NG

47. Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower find and kill monsters:

Porta Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Heartstone of Porta NG

48. Return to Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

49. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG

Several monsters are located to the east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

50. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Warrior Captain Lv. 36

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG

51. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Scroll of Dwarf Trust NG

52. Teleport to Oren. Find Hollint High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Hollint will give Recommendation of Hollint NG

53. Teleport from Oren to Ivory Tower. Find Gauen Magister on the third floor and talk to him.

Gauen will give Crescent Moon Bow NG

54. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Hamil Sagittarius near the mill south of the village and talk to him.

We recommend to pre-purchase:

Wooden Arrow NG
Soulshot: No Grade NG

55. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Lv. 35
Leto Lizardman Archer Lv. 36
Leto Lizardman Soldier Lv. 37
Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39
Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG , and until the quest monster appears:

Serpent Demon Kadesh Quest Monster Lv. 41

Kill a demon with a Crescent Moon Bow NG equipped and without using any skills.

From the killed demon get Talisman of Kadesh NG

56. Teleport to Dion. Find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give Terry's Report NG

57. Leave Dion Town through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Hamil Sagittarius near the mill south of the village and talk to him.

Hamil will give Mark of Sagittarius NG

58. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Biotin High Priest in the Temple and talk to him.

Biotin will give Mark of Trust NG

59. Teleport to Giran, find Dufner Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Dufner will give Mark of Seeker NG

60. To change your profession, talk to Hannavalt Grand Master in the Giran Warriors Guild.
