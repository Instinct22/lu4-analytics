Battle Transformations

Skills 14.04.2025


Basics

Transformations are a group of skills that replace the character's abilities and allow you to change your archetype and role in the group.

General transformations are available to all classes. Transformation changes the character's appearance, adds unique skills and can be helpful not only in battle.

Among other transformations available to all classes, there is a unique Cursed Weapon in the game, which, when picked up, transforms into a Dark Warrior. Cursed Weapon transformations cannot be learned. Cursed Weapons include the Demonic Sword Zariche and the Blood Sword Akamanah.

To learn transformations you must complete the quest More Than Meets The Eye and bring Transformation Sealbook to Avant-Garde Transformation Wizard in Ivory Tower.

On MasterWork, you do not need to complete a quest to learn transformations, and Avant-Garde is in the temple of the Arcan city. After learning, the skill is available forever.

There are different ways to obtain Transformation Sealbooks. Defeat monsters or Raid Bosses, purchase from NPCs or as quest rewards. All Sealbooks can be traded with other players or sold through a personal store.

In the original game, there were one-time Transformation Scrolls that allowed you to use the skill transformations only once.

Changes on MasterWork also affected the transformation system. To learn transformations, you do not need to complete a quest. Ways to obtain transformations have changed, and some transformations have become unavailable.

Summoner transformations are described in detail in the article Summoners Transformations.

Class transformations are described in detail in the article Class-based Transformations.

The following rules apply to all general transformations:

  • All basic characteristics, such as STR/ DEX/ CON/ INT/ WIT/ MEN, change depending on the transformation
  • All active skills are replaced with transformation skills
  • Summoned pets/servants do not disappear
  • Duration: 30 minutes
  • Cooldown: 4 hours

Cursed Weapon

For all cursed transformations the following rules apply:

  • Basic characteristics remain unchanged
  • All class skills remain available, Cursed Weapon adds Transformation Skills
  • Cursed Weapon transformations replace other transformations
  • The owner of the Cursed Weapon cannot be a member of the group
  • It is impossible to get positive skills from other players
  • The location of the Cursed Weapon is displayed on the game world map
  • The character's karma changes to -999 999
  • The character's name is replaced with the name of the Cursed Weapon
  • Cursed Weapons cannot be unequipped/ dropped
  • Cursed Weapon disappears/drops on death
