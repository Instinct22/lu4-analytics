Isle of Prayer and Chromatic Highlands (74~83)

Locations 23.12.2025

An island north of Innadril where Fafurion's beloved Witch Parme lives. It was once a small and unprepossessing island, but Fafurion transformed it into a majestic and beautiful island fit for a queen.

Basics

Isle of Prayer and Chromatic Highlands was significantly rebuild to increase their exping and attribute farming attractiveness:

  • Number of monsters increased
  • EXP and SP gained amount increased
  • New spots added at both zones
  • Added new path to Chromatic Highland
  • Added new passive quest to obtain S-grade gear
  • Added new club teleport spot to Chromatic Highland:
    • Chromatics Highlands - I (original teleport)
    • Chromatics Highlands - II (new teleport)

Location mechanics

Defencive mechanic at Isle of Prayer "no more the three people" do not allow players to farm in big groups.

In basic game, if your party have more than 3 players, and you kill monsters, you will be attacked by very strong location guards — Witch Warder Lv. 79 . On MasterWork realms safe number of party members increased to 5 players.

At Chromatic Highlands that mechanics doesn't work.

MasterWork team improved dragon spawn mechanics at Chromatic Highlands for better farming and exping at the location.

At whole Chromatic Highlands territory Dark Water Dragon Lv. 82 lives. On their own dragons are not very interesting, but their guards, monsters Shade Lv. 82 , drops Earth Stone NG . After they die Fafurion Kindred Lv. 82 spawns same as 4 monsters Water Dragon Detractor Lv. 82 .

Fafurion Kindred keeps no loot, but while he's alive, dragons will spawn next to him, that allows efficient exping for full party. Besides that dragon drops Wind Stone NG .

Fafurion Kindred has 2 traits:

  • Weakened Life Lv. 1 — deadly poison, that gradually decreases health level
  • Disease Lv. 9 — completely blocks healing skills

Dragons will attack Kindred, you have to protect him by healing with Spirit of the Lake NG , that drops from Water Dragon Detractor Lv. 82 . At any moment Fafurion Kindred disappears, and you have to find a new victim.

In the original game Fafurion Kindred Lv. 82 appears at spawn Dark Water Dragon Lv. 82 , not at death location, that makes farming less comfortable.

Pay attention!

On MasterWork this mechanics changed — Fafurion Kindred spawns at Dark Water Dragon death point, if he was killed inside his area. Otherwise, will appear at original spawn, same as before. Although his living time increased.

Water Dragon Detractor Lv. 82 stats and acquired experience increased.

New quest

At Isle of Prayer and Chromatic Highlands was released new passive quest. You don't need to accept it to start completing.

All monsters on the island drops Blue Seed of Evil NG and Black Seed of Evil NG . It doesn't pick up automatically, and stay highlighted on the ground to make it possible to distinguish from common loot.

  • Blue Seed of Evil NG
    • Island Guardian Lv. 78
    • White Sand Mirage Lv. 79
    • Muddy Coral Lv. 80
    • Kleopora Lv. 80
    • Seychelles Lv. 81
    • Naiad Lv. 81
    • Sonneratia Lv. 81
    • Castalia Lv. 81
    • Chrysocolla Lv. 81
    • Pythia Lv. 81
  • Black Seed of Evil NG
    • Water Dragon Detractor Lv. 82
    • Water Dragon Detractor Lv. 82

Pay attention!

Blue Seed of Evil NG and Black Seed of Evil NG can't be traded! Collect it only by one character.

Our support will not transfer items between characters.

Bring collected items to Parnassus flying castle, located in the middle of the island. Path to the castle begins in the northern part of the lake. On Parnassus you will meet Liriel Wavecrest , which will ask you to clear the island of the Seeds of Evil and offer a reward for them:

  • Water Dragon Chest Parts NG , which contains random recipes for helmets, gloves, boots and shields of S-grade
    • Blue Seed of Evil NG x 850
    • Black Seed of Evil NG x 200
    • Adena NG x 1 000 000
  • Water Dragon Chest Armor NG , contains random recipes of S-grade top, bottom and full-body armor
    • Blue Seed of Evil NG x 2 500
    • Black Seed of Evil NG x 500
    • Adena NG x 2 000 000
