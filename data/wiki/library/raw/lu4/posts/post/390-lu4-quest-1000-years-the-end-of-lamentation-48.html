Lu4: Quest 1000 years, the End of Lamentation 48+

Useful quests 17.02.2026


Basics

Quest "1000 Years, the End of Lamentation" — one of the ways to obtain Weapons, Armor, Jewelry, C-grade Enchant Scrolls, various resources, and Adena. The Lu4 team has reworked the quest and rewards while preserving the overall concept of the quest.

The quest consists of hunting monsters and obtaining special items — Items of Dead Heroes. These items can be exchanged for a random reward from an NPC.

Lu4 introduces important changes to the quest familiar to many players. Features of the quest on Lu4:

  • Changed chances of obtaining a Rare Reward
    • Increased chance for 50 items
    • Maximum chance limited to 70%
  • Significantly expanded reward list
    • Various types of Mid and Top C-grade Weapons
    • Top C-grade Armor
    • C-grade Jewelry
    • C-grade Enchant Scrolls
    • Various materials
    • Adena

3. Return to Gilmore Antharas Watchman and talk to him to get your reward.

Gilmore will give a random reward:

  • Adena:
    • Adena NG x 75 for each Articles of Dead Heroes NG
  • Or one of the Rare Rewards:
    • Old Key NG
    • Old Hilt NG
    • Totem Necklace NG
    • Einhasad Crucifix NG

Pay attention!

To receive a Rare Reward, you must have at least 50 Articles of Dead Heroes NG !

The number of quest items increases the chance of receiving a Rare Reward, but the chance cannot exceed 70%.
