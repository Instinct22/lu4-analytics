Quest Alliance with Ketra Orcs 72+

Useful quests 03.09.2025

Basics

Completing the "Alliance with Ketra Orcs" quest allows you to obtain high-level resources, parts and recipes for S-grade equipment.

The quest is divided into five stages. As your alliance level rises, you unlock more valuable rewards and gain access to additional features in the Ketra Orc Village.

To increase the alliance level to 4 and 5, you have to complete additional quests and kill raid bosses:

  • Level 4 — Prove Your Courage, killing boss Varka's Hero Shadith Raid Boss Lv. 80
  • Level 5 — Slay The Enemy Commander, killing boss Varka's Commander Mos Raid Boss Lv. 80

To open access to the quest Magical Power of Water - pt. 1 and get Divine Stone of Wisdom NG , you must have Mark of Ketra's Alliance - Level 2 NG .

Alliance With Ketra Orcs
Starting NPC Wahkan Ketra's Messenger
Starting location Ketra Orc Outpost
Level 72 ~ 85
Type Repeatable
Rewards Materials, S-grade equipment parts and recipes

Level 1

If you or anyone in your party attacks any Ketra Orcs in the Ketra Orc Outpost territory while on a quest, your alliance level will instantly decrease by 1.

1. Talk to Wahkan Ketra's Messenger at the entrance to Ketra Orc Outpost.

2. Head to Varka Silenos Outpost to hunt.

Collect 100 Varka's Badge - Soldier NG by killing monsters:

Varka Silenos Recruit Lv. 77
Varka Silenos Footman Lv. 77
Varka Silenos Scout Lv. 78
Varka Silenos Hunter Lv. 78
Varka Silenos Shaman Lv. 78

3. Return to Ketra Orc Outpost and talk to Wahkan Ketra's Messenger to receive:

Mark of Ketra's Alliance - Level 1 NG

Reward:

  • Ketra Orcs are now non-aggressive towards your character
