3 in 1 Spellhowler profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Spellhowler:

  • Quest Trial of the Scholar
  • Quest Testimony of Fate
  • Quest Test of Magus

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Spellhowler profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Scholar NG
  • Mark of Fate NG
  • Mark of Magus NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

1. Teleport to Giran. Find Kaira Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Kaira will give Kaira's Letter NG

2. Head to the Temple of Giran. Go around the building, find Metheus Brother and talk to him.

Metheus will give Metheus's Funeral Jar NG

3. Teleport to Dion. Find Rukal Bard in the Temple and talk to him.

Rukal will give Rukal's Letter NG

4. Talk to two NPCs in the Temple of Dion in sequence.

4.1. Talk to Mirien Magister , she will give Mirien's 1st Sigil NG

4.2. Talk to Sylvain High Priest , he will give:

High Priest's Sigil NG
Sylvain's Letter NG

5. Head to Dion's Grocery Store. Find Maria there and talk to her.

Maria will give Maria's 1st Letter NG

6. Head to the southern gate of Dion. Find Lucas Captain there and talk to him.

Lucas will give Lucas's Letter NG

On MasterWork, you can talk to Lucas in the city center.

7. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Maria's 2nd Letter NG

8. Leave Dion Town through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village (Club Card holders can teleport to the village). Find Creta Astrologer behind the Warehouse and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's 1st Letter NG

9. Return to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Lucilla's Handbag NG

10. Return to Creta Astrologer and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's Painting1 NG

11. Go back to Maria and talk to her.

Maria will give Creta's Painting2 NG

12. Head to the southern gate of Dion. Find Lucas Captain there and talk to him.

Lucas will give Creta's Painting3 NG

13. Go back to Maria and talk to her.

14. Exit the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road to the east to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Hangman Tree Lv. 35

Until you get Kasandra's Remains NG

15. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Parina in the Temple and talk to her.

Parina will give Parina's Letter NG

16. Teleport from Gludin Village to Wasteland. Go north, find Casian Elder among the cliffs and talk to him.

Casian will give Lilac Charm NG

17. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins Of Agony. Head west towards Fellmere Lake. On the island in the center of the lake, find and kill the monster:

Singing Flower of Phantasm Quest Monster Lv. 44

From the killed monster, you will receive 1st Golden Seed NG

18. Teleport to Dion, then to Tanor Canyon, and head east toward the river. Before reaching the shore, find and kill the monster:

Singing Flower of Darkness Quest Monster Lv. 44

From the killed monster, you will receive 3rd Golden Seed NG

19. Teleport to Giran. Find Metheus Brother behind the Temple and talk to him.

Metheus will give Herbalism Textbook NG

Talk to him again, the dialogue must be carried out twice.

20. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38

Until you collect 5 Brown Scroll Scrap NG

21. Teleport to Oren, then to the Sea of Spores. Instead of going inside, head northeast. On the river bank, near the waterfall, find and kill the monster:

Singing Flower of Nightmare Quest Monster Lv. 44

From the killed monster, you will receive 2nd Golden Seed NG

22. Teleport to Dion. Find Ixia Master in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Ixia will give Ixia's List NG

23. Head to Dion's Grocery Store. Find Maria there and talk to her.

Maria will give Crystal of Purity NG

24.1. Head to the Temple of Dion. Find Sylvain High Priest and talk to him.

Sylvain will give Symbol of Sylvain NG

24.2. Talk to Mirien Magister in the same Temple.

Mirien will give Mirien's 2nd Sigil NG

25. Talk to Rukal Bard in the Temple of Dion.

Rukal will give Score of Elements NG

26. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins Of Agony. Head deep into the location. Near the northern gate of the ruined village find Earth Snake Wise and talk to her.

Earth Snake will give Serpent Charm NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

27. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ruins Of Agony. Head to Fellmere Lake, find Water Undine on the northern shore of the lake and talk to her.

Water Undine will give Undine Charm NG

28. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins of Despair. Head deep into the location. In the southwest of the ruined village find Flame Salamander and talk to her.

Flame Salamander will give Salamander Charm NG

29. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you collect 10 Tyrant's Blood NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

30. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Head to the northwest edge of the Wastelands. Go through the passage at the edge of the location, find Wind Sylph and talk to her.

Wind Sylph will give Sylph Charm NG

Alternative route: Teleport to the Wastelands via the Club Teleporter. Instead of going inside, head north over the hill.

31. Teleport to Giran. Find Jurek Grand Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Jurek will give:

Grand Magister's Sigil NG
Jurek's List NG

32. Exit Giran through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Harpy Lv. 32
Wyrm Lv. 35
Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Harpy's Feather NG x 20
Wyrm's Wingbone NG x 10
Medusa's Ichor NG x 10

Harpy monsters are in the western part of the location, dragon monsters and medusa in the eastern part.

33. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Head northeast, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31

Until you collect:

Marsh Spider's Fluids NG x 10
Dazzling Drop NG x 20
Nightshade Root NG x 10

Quest items drop from all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

34. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Follow the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Dead Seeker Lv. 34

Until you collect 10 Dead Seeker Dung NG

35. Teleport to Dion, then to Plains of Dion. Find and kill monsters:

Monster Eye Destroyer Lv. 26

Until you collect 5 Monster Eye Destroyer's Skin NG

36. Teleport to Dion. Find Ixia Master in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Ixia will give Belladonna NG

37. Teleport to Giran. Find Metheus Brother behind the Temple building and talk to him.

Metheus will give Alder's Skull NG

38.1. Head to the Dark Elf Guild of Giran. Find Kaira Magister and talk to her.

Kaira will give Alders Skull NG

38.2. After talking to Kaira, the ghost Alders Spirit will appear nearby. Talk to him.

39. Head to the Giran Mages Guild. Find Roa Magister and talk to her.

Roa will give Alder's Receipt NG

40. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give Revelations Manuscript NG

41. Teleport to Giran. Find Kaira Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Kaira will give Kaira's Recommendation NG

42. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give:

Palus Charm NG
Thifiell's Letter NG

43.1. Teleport from Dark Elf Village to Spider Nest, Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Arkenia's Note NG

43.2. In the vicinity of the Altar of Rites find two NPCs and talk to them:

Bloody Pixy , she will give Pixy Garnet NG
Blight Treant , he will give Blight Treant Seed NG

The Bloody Pixy and Blight Treant also inhabit other places in the Dark Elf lands.

44. Teleport to Giran, then to Dragon Valley. Go deep into the location. Find and kill monsters:

Shackle Lv. 45

Until you collect 2 Shackle's Scalp NG

45. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35
Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Breka Overlord Skull NG x 10
Shaman's Necklace NG x 5

Several monsters are located east of the teleport point, others are on the northwestern hill.

46. Teleport to Giran. Find Jurek Grand Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Jurek will give Symbol of Jurek NG

47. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mirien's 3rd Sigil NG

48. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Cronos Elder in the main square and talk to him.

Cronos will give:

Crono's Sigil NG
Cronos Letter NG

49. Exit Hunter's Village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill, find and kill monsters:

Karul Bugbear Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Karul Bugbear Skull NG

50. Teleport to Giran. Find Dieter Magister in the Mages Guild and talk to him.

Dieter will give Dieter's Key NG

51. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the southern gate and head south along the road to Floran Village. Find Creta Astrologer behind the Warehouse and talk to her.

Creta will give Creta's 2nd Letter NG

52. Return to Dieter Magister and talk to him.

Dieter will give:

Dieter's Letter NG
Dieter's Diary NG

53. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Edroc Armor Merchant in the Weapon Shop and talk to him.

Edroc will give Raut's Letter Envelope NG

54. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Raut Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Raut will give:

Scripture Chapter 1 NG
Strong Liquor NG

55. Head to Gludin Harbor. Find Triff Drunkard and talk to him.

Triff will give Triff's Ring NG

56. Teleport to Giran. Find Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Valkon will give Valkon's Request NG

57. Teleport to Dion. Find Maria in the Grocery Store and talk to her.

Maria will give Crystal of Purity NG

58. Return to Valkon Warehouse Keeper and talk to him.

Valkon will give Scripture Chapter 2 NG

59. Teleport from Giran to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Windsus Lv. 39
Grandis Lv. 40

Until you collect:

Windsus Mane NG x 10
Grandis' Skull NG x 10
Scripture Chapter 3 NG

60. Teleport to Dion. Find Poitan Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Poitan will give Poitan's Notes NG

61. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Casian's List NG

62. Teleport to Dion, exit the city through the eastern gate. Follow the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Ghoul Lv. 32

Until you collect 10 Ghoul's Skin NG

63. Teleport to Giran, exit the city through the eastern gate, cross the bridge. Follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden. Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect 12 Medusa's Blood NG

64. Teleport to Giran, then to Hardin's Academy. Find and kill monsters:

Fettered Soul Lv. 38

Until you collect 5 Fettered Soul's Ichor NG

65. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40

Until you collect 10 Leto Overlord Skull NG

66. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Instead going inside, go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Monstereye Lv. 41
Enchanted Stone Golem Lv. 42
Enchanted Iron Golem Lv. 43
Enchanted Gargoyle Lv. 44
Ghost Fire Quest Monster Lv. 42

Until you collect:

Enchanted Monster Eye Shell NG x 10
Enchanted Golem Powder NG x 10
Enchanted Iron Golem Shard NG x 10
Enchanted Gargoyle's Nail NG x 5
Flame Crystal NG x 5

67. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins Of Agony. Head deep into the location. Near the northern gate of the ruined village find Earth Snake Wise and talk to her.

Earth Snake will give Tone of Earth NG (Tone of Earth)

You can reach the next location on foot.

68. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ruins Of Agony. Head to Fellmere Lake. Find Water Undine on the northern shore of the lake and talk to her.

Water Undine will give Tone of Water NG

69. Teleport to Gludio, then to the Ruins of Despair. Head deep into the location. In the southwest of the ruined village find Flame Salamander and talk to her.

Flame Salamander will give Tone of Fire NG

70. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Instead of going into the cave, head to the northwest edge of the Wastelands. Go through the passage at the edge of the location, find Wind Sylph and talk to her.

Wind Sylph will give Tone of Wind NG

Alternative route: Teleport to the Wastelands via the Club Teleporter and head north over the hill.

71. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Go north, among the cliffs find Casian Elder and talk to him.

Casian will give Scripture Chapter 4 NG

72. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Bloody Swampland. Find and kill the monster:

Black Willow Lurker Quest Monster Lv. 39

From the killed monster, you will receive Black Willow Leaf NG

73.1. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find two NPCs around the Altar and talk to them:

Bloody Pixy , she will give Red Pixy Dust NG
Blight Treant , he will give Blight Treant Sap NG

The Bloody Pixy and Blight Treant also inhabit other places in the Dark Elf lands.

73.2. Head to the Altar of Rites. Find Arkenia Witch and talk to her.

Arkenia will give Arkenia's Letter NG

74. Teleport to Dark Elf Village. Find Thifiell Tetrarch in the Temple and talk to him.

Thifiell will give Mark of Fate NG

75. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Cronos Elder in the main square and talk to him.

Cronos will give Symbol of Cronos NG

76. Teleport to Dion. Find Mirien Magister in the Temple and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mark of Scholar NG

77. Find Rukal Bard in the Temple of Dion and talk to him.

Rukal will give Mark of Magus NG

78. To change your profession, talk to Angus Grand Master in the Dark Elf Guild of Giran.
