3 in 1 Warsmith profession quest

Quests to change class 27.10.2025

Basics

A character's second profession is available at level 40 and requires completing the chain of 3 quests specific to the profession. Quests necessary to obtain a profession Warsmith:

  • Quest Trial of the Guildsman
  • Quest Testimony of Prosperity
  • Quest Test of the Maestro

On MasterWork, all quests are available from the character's level 35.

This article is a Guide/Manual for completing 3 quests for the Warsmith profession together. After completing the quests, you will receive the items needed to change the profession. The full list of rewards for completing quests:

  • Second Class transfer Reward NG Foundation x 3
  • Exp NG x 2 250 000
  • SP NG x 225 000
  • Dimensional Diamond NG x 45
  • Mark of Guildsman NG
  • Mark of Prosperity NG
  • Mark of Maestro NG

Information about all quests for all professions is available in the article Quests for 1, 2 and 3 professions

Quest guide

To complete the quest, you need the following resources:

Steel NG x 7
Varnish NG x 70
Animal Skin NG x 100
Crystal (D-Grade) D x 80

To save time and money, prepare resources in advance.

1. Teleport to Giran. Find Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

You must pay 2000 Adena NG

Valkon will give Valkon's Recommendation NG

2. In the Giran Warehouse find Parman Warehouse Keeper and talk to him.

Parman will give 1st Ring of Testimony NG

3. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Lockirin Iron Gate's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Select the line "Test of the Maestro" in the dialogue

4. In the House of Elders of Dwarven Village find Filaur Gray Pillar Member and talk to him.

Filaur will give Architecture of Cruma NG

5. Teleport to Dion, head to the east gate. Find Bright Guard near the gate and talk to him.

Bright will give Bright's List NG

6. Exit the city of Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road east to the Execution Grounds (Club Card holders can teleport to the location). Find and kill monsters:

Mandragora Sprout Lv. 21
Mandragora Sapling Lv. 23
Mandragora Blossom Lv. 25

Until you get:

Mandragora Berry NG
Mandragora Petal NG x 20

7. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Find Lorain Researcher at the entrance to the tower and talk to her.

Lorain will give Ingredients of Antidote NG

8. In the Cruma Marshlands find and kill monsters:

Giant Mist Leech Lv. 25
Stinger Wasp Lv. 30
Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Giant Crimson Ant Lv. 28

Until you get:

Leech Blood NG x 10
Stinger Wasp Needle NG x 10
Marsh Spider's Web NG x 10
Crimson Moss NG x 10

9. Return to Lorain Researcher and talk to her.

Lorain will give Report of Cruma NG

10.1. Teleport to Dion. Head to the east gate. Find Bright Guard near the gate and talk to him.

Bright will give Mandragora Bouquet NG

10.2. Find Emily near the east gate inside the city and talk to her.

Emily will give Emily's Recipe NG

11. Teleport to Talking Island Village. Find Altran Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Altran will give:

Altran's Instructions NG
Altran's 1st Recommendation NG
Altran's 2nd Recommendation NG
Recipe: Journeyman Ring NG

Find and use Recipe: Journeyman Ring NG in your inventory. This recipe is now registered in your crafting book and allows you to create Journeyman Ring NG . You can open the book using the skill Dwarven Craft Lv. 1 .

12. Head to the Talking Island Village Warehouse. Find Wilford Warehouse Keeper and talk to him.

Select the line "An Elf more than 1,000 years old" in the dialogue

Wilford will give Crystal Brooch NG

13. Head to Talking Island Village Temple. Find Lilith and talk to her.

Lilith will give Lilith's Elven Wafer NG

14. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Head east, find Piotur near the house and talk to him.

Piotur will give Blessed Seed NG

15. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Filaur Gray Pillar Member in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Filaur will give Recommendation of Filaur NG

16. In the House of Elders of the Dwarven Village find Balanki Silver Scale's and talk to him.

17. Talk to the six NPCs in the House of Elders one by one.

17.1. Talk to Lockirin Iron Gate's , he will give:

Collection License NG
Lockirin's 1st Notice NG
Lockirin's 2nd Notice NG
Lockirin's 3rd Notice NG
Lockirin's 4th Notice NG
Lockirin's 5th Notice NG

17.2. Talk to Balanki Silver Scale's

17.3. Talk to Arin Black Anvil's

17.4. Talk to Keef Bronze Key's

17.5. Talk to Spiron Golden Wheel's

17.6. Talk to Filaur Gray Pillar Member

18. Talk to five NPCs in Dwarven Village one by one.

18.1. Head to the Grocery Store. Find Mion Grocer and talk to her.

Mion will give Contribution of Mion NG

18.2. Head to the Warehouse. Find Torocco Carrier behind the building and talk to him.

Torocco will give Procuration of Torocco NG

18.3. Head to the house near the northeastern exit of the village. Find Croto Chief and talk to him.

Croto will give Paint of Kamuru NG

18.4. Head to the north gate. Find Maryse Redbonnet near the house and talk to her.

Maryse will give Maryse's Request NG

Talk to Maryse again, give her 100 Animal Skin NG

Maryse will give Contribution of Maryse NG

18.5. Head to the Weapon Shop. Find Shari Armor Merchant and talk to her.

Shari will give Contribution of Shari NG

19.Leave Dwarven Village through the southern gate. Follow the road southeast towards Strip Mine. Find Bolter Miner near the road and talk to him.

Bolter will give Receipt of Bolter NG

20. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave, turn left on the bridge. Go further along the tunnel. Find the prison cell with Corpse Of Kamur and go inside.

Go through the fake wall behind Kamur.

21. Proceed further along the tunnel. Find and kill the quest monster:

Evil Eye Lord Quest Monster Lv. 37

From the killed monster you will receive Necklace of Kamuru NG

22. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Croto Chief at the house next to the northeast gate and talk to him.

Croto will give Letter of Solder Detachment NG

23. Head to the Dwarven Village House of Elders. Find Balanki Silver Scale's and talk to him.

Balanki will give Recommendation of Balanki NG

24. In the House of Elders of the Dwarven Village, find Arin Black Anvil's and talk to him.

Arin will give Paint of Teleport Device NG

25. Teleport from Dwarven Village to Western Mining Zone. Follow the road north-east to the mouth of the river. On the island near the northern shore, find Toma Master and talk to him.

In the dialogue, first select "Testimony of Prosperity"

Toma will give Contribution of Toma NG

Talk to Toma again, choosing the first line of dialogue.

Toma will give Broken Teleport Device NG and teleport you to the southeastern part of the Western Mining Zone.

On MasterWork, Master Toma is only found in this location.

26. After teleporting to the southeast of the Western Mining Zone, you will be attacked by three monsters:

King Bugbear Lv. 32

Kill the monsters attacking you.

27. Return to Toma Master and talk to him.

Toma will give 5 Teleport Device NG

28. Teleport to Dwarven Village. Find Arin Black Anvil's in the House of Elders and talk to him.

Arin will give Recommendation of Arin NG

29. In the Council of House of Dwarven Village, find Lockirin Iron Gate's and talk to him.

Lockirin will give Mark of Maestro NG

30. Talk to the six NPCs in the House of Elders one by one.

30.1. Talk to Balanki Silver Scale's

Balanki will give 2nd Receipt of Contribution NG

30.2. Talk to Arin Black Anvil's

Arin will give 5th Receipt of Contribution NG

30.3. Talk to Keef Bronze Key's

You must pay 5000 Adena NG

Keef will give 3rd Receipt of Contribution NG

30.4. Talk to Spiron Golden Wheel's

Spiron will give 1st Receipt of Contribution NG

30.5. Talk to Filaur Gray Pillar Member

Filaur will give 4th Receipt of Contribution NG

30.6. Talk to Lockirin Iron Gate's

Lockirin will give Old Account Book NG

31. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give:

Norman's Instructions NG
Norman's Receipt NG

32. Teleport to Gludio. Find Pinter Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Pinter will give:

Pinter's Instructions NG
Recipe: Amber Bead NG

Register the resulting recipe in the crafting book.

33. Teleport from Gludio to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave, find and kill monsters:

Ant Captain Lv. 31
Ant Lv. 30
Ant Overseer Lv. 32

Until you get 70 Amber Bead NG

Dwarves of the Artisan profession can receive an additional quest item from killed monsters — Amber Lump NG . Using the previously obtained recipe, you can turn 1 Piece of Amber into 5 Amber Beads.

34. Return to Pinter Blacksmith and talk to him.

Pinter will give 7 Journeyman Deco Beads NG

35. Teleport to Giran. Find Parman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse building and talk to him.

Parman will give:

2nd Ring of Testimony NG
Parman's Letter NG

36. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Find Duning Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and Talk to him.

Duning will give Duning's Instructions NG

37. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Lv. 31
Breka Orc Archer Lv. 32
Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34
Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35
Breka Orc Warrior Lv. 33

Until you get 30 Duning's Key NG

38. Return to Duning Blacksmith and talk to him.

39. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give Norman's List NG

40. Teleport to Dion, exit the city through the eastern gate. Follow the road to the east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Strain Lv. 31
Ghoul Lv. 32
Dead Seeker Lv. 34
Granite Golem Lv. 33

Until you get:

Gray Bone Powder NG x 70
Red Pigment NG x 70
Granite Whetstone NG x 70

41. Teleport to Dion, then to the Plains of Dion. Head east towards the Monster Race Track. Find and kill monsters:

Silenos Lv. 30

Until you get 70 Braided Yarn NG

42. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head southwest. Find Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give Clay Dough NG

43. Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower, in the hallway behind the Raid Boss, find the Box Of Titan and examine it.

After examining the box you will receive Pattern of Keyhole NG

44. Return to Nikola Maestro and talk to him.

Nikola will give:

Recipe: Titan Key NG (Recipe: Titan Key)
Nikola's List NG (Nikola's List)

Register the resulting recipe in the crafting book.

45. In the Cruma Marshlands, find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Worker Lv. 31
Marsh Spider Lv. 34
Toad Lord Lv. 32

Until you get:

Stakato Shell NG x 20
Marsh Spider's Thorn NG x 10
Toad Lord Sac NG x 10

Quest items drop from all Marsh Stakato monsters in the Cruma Marshlands.

46. Open the crafting book and create Key of Titan NG

Head inside the Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower, in the hallway behind the Raid Boss, find Box Of Titan and open it with the key.

From the opened box you will receive Maphr Tablet Fragment NG

47. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Norman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Norman will give 7 Journeyman Gem NG

Open the crafting book and create 7 Journeyman Ring NG

48. Teleport to Giran. Find Parman Warehouse Keeper in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Parman will give Mark of Prosperity NG

49. Talk to Valkon Warehouse Keeper in the Giran Warehouse.

In a dialogue, always choose the first line:

"I learned the mind attitude and virtues that one should possess as a faithful union member."

If you choose another line, you will receive twice less experience, SP and adena for completing the quest.

Valkon will give Mark of Guildsman NG

50. To change profession talk to Kusto Head Blacksmith in Giran Blacksmith Shop.
