Clan Active Skills

Skills 06.07.2026

Basics

The Lu4 team added active clan skills to the game to diversify and increase the effectiveness of clan activity. New clan skills are available to clan Leaders level 2 or higher.

  • Only the Leader can use the skills
  • Active clan skills are automatically learned by the Clan Leader
  • The effect of the skill only works around the Clan Leader
  • Skills only apply to clan members
  • Skill effects don't occupy buff slots

Required items

When the clan levels up, new clan skills are automatically learned by the Clan Leader.

New skills consume Proof of Loyalty NG . Proof of Loyalty can be obtained in several ways:

  • Reward for completing the first class transfer
  • Reward for completing second class marks
  • Hunting certain high-level monsters
  • Drops from world bosses
  • Purchase from other players

Clan Rush

Clan Rush Lv. 1

Nearby clan members' Speed +40.

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members within range.

  • Required clan level — 2
  • Effect area — 1500
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 3

Clan Recall

Clan Recall Lv. 1

Instantly teleports nearby clan members to the nearest village.

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members within range.

  • Required clan level — 2
  • Effect area — 1500
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 3

Clan Summon

Clan Summon Lv. 1

Summons clan members to Clan Leader.

Consumption for clan members: 1 Summoning Crystal for each member personally.

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members.

  • Required clan level — 3
  • Effect area — Applies to all clan members who are not in combat mode and have confirmed teleport
  • Using time — 5 seconds
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 5
  • Teleportation cost — Summoning Crystal NG x 1, used by all summoned players

Clan Purity

Clan Purity Lv. 1

Removes all debuffs from nearby clan members.

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members within range.

  • Required clan level — 4
  • Effect area — 1500
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 5

Clan Celestial Aegis

Clan Celestial Aegis Lv. 1

Applies to nearby clan members:
PvP and PvE Damage -80%
PvP and PvE Defence +80%
Immunity to Stun, Slow, Hold, Paralyze, Turn to Stone (Medusa).

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members within range.

  • Required clan level — 5
  • Effect area — 1500
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 5

Clan Restore

Clan Restore Lv. 1

Restores 100% HP and CP, 25% MP and additionally 500 MP to nearby clan members.

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members within range.

  • Required clan level — 6
  • Effect area — 1500
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 5

Clan Noblesse

Clan Noblesse Lv. 1

Applies 'Noblesse Blessing' to nearby clan members.
'Noblesse Blessing' preserves target's buffs and debuffs after death.

Can be used only by Clan Leader, but affects all clan members within range.

  • Required clan level — 6
  • Effect area — 1500
  • Re-use time — 30 minutes
  • Cost — Proof of Loyalty NG x 5
