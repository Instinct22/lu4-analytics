Clan system

Game mechanics 06.07.2026

Basics

The clan system is the foundation of social interaction between players. Clans allow players to unite to achieve goals that are unattainable for individual players — capturing castles and killing epic bosses.

Creating a clan

To create a clan, a character must be level 10 or higher. Speak to the High Priest in a temple or the Grand Master in any guild. Later, these NPCs will help you level up the clan and learn clan skills.

A clan name can include up to 16 letters or numbers, spaces and special characters are not allowed.

Pay attention!

Squad and Academy are not available on Lu4.

Clan Reputation system is not available.

Clan Crest

A unique logo (Crest) can be installed by clan members of level 3 and above. Installing/changing a clan emblem is available to the clan leader and other members who have the appropriate privilege.

To install the emblem, open the clan window (Alt+N) and click the “Management” button. In the opened window that appears, select the “Clan emblem” tab, then click the “Add Clan Crest” button and specify the path to the emblem file. The file must be an image in the .png or .bmp format, 16 by 12 pixels in size.

If the image is larger than 16 by 12 pixels, it will be automatically resized to the correct dimensions, but the result may not be satisfactory.

Transferring Clan Leader Rights

To transfer clan leadership rights to another member, the current clan leader must visit a special NPC and submit a request for the transfer. The rights will be transferred after the server's technical restart.

The transfer can be canceled at any time before the restart.

Pay attention!

On Lu4 servers, clan leadership can be transferred without a restart for Master Coin NG Foundation . To do this, select the option “Delegate Clan Leader [50 MC]”.

Leaving a Clan

A player can leave a clan at any time. However, there are several penalties associated with this:

  • A player who leaves a clan voluntarily receives a 4-hour restriction on joining another clan.
  • If a player is expelled by the clan leader, the player receives a 4-hour penalty on joining another clan. Additionally, the clan receives a 4-hour penalty on recruiting new members.
  • The clan leader cannot leave the clan.

Clan Disbandment

To disband a clan, the Leader must speak to the High Priest in the temple or the Grand Master in any guild.

The Leader can disband the clan without the consent of other clan members.

A clan cannot be disbanded if it is participating in a clan war.

Disbandment takes seven days, during which the request can be canceled.

Clan Passive Skills

Clan passive skills on Lu4 have been reworked. Required clan levels and items have been changed.

Upon reaching clan level 1 and above, it's possible to learn passive clan skills. To learn skills, you need Proof of Loyalty NG . As the clan leve increases, new skills become available.

For a complete list of passive skills for each clan level, see Clan’s Passive Skills.

Active skills

The Lu4 team added active clan skills to the game to diversify and increase the effectiveness of clan activity. New clan skills are available to clan Leaders level 2 or higher.

  • Only the Leader can use the skills
  • Active clan skills are automatically learned by the Clan Leader
  • The effect of the skill only works around the Clan Leader
  • Skills only apply to clan members
  • Skill effects don't occupy buff slots

When the clan levels up, new clan skills are automatically learned by the Clan Leader.

New skills consume Proof of Loyalty NG . Proof of Loyalty can be obtained in several ways:

  • Reward for completing the first class transfer
  • Reward for completing second class marks
  • Hunting certain high-level monsters
  • Drops from world bosses
  • Purchase from other players

For a complete list of active skills for each clan level, see Active clan skills.
