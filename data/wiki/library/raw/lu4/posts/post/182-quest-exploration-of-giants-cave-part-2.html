Quest Exploration of Giants Cave, Part 2

Useful quests 14.01.2025


Basics

Giant's Cave quest consists of two parts and is a popular way to obtain equipment recipes.

Quest and rewards, same as the location itself, are different for Interlude and High Five stages.

On MasterWork access to the second part of the quest does not require Ancient Language Dictionary: Intermediate Level NG .

Interlude

Quest guide Exploration of Giants Cave, Part 2

Starting NPC Sobling Head Researcher
Level 57 ~ 69
Type Repeatable
Reward (random)
Recipe: Sealed Armor of Nightmare(60%) NG
Recipe: Sealed Leather Armor of Nightmare(60%) NG
Recipe: Sealed Majestic Plate Armor(60%) NG
Recipe: Sealed Majestic Leather Armor(60%) NG

In basic game to get access to Exploration of Giants Cave, Part 2 you will need Ancient Language Dictionary: Intermediate Level NG , received by completing the quest Exploration of Giants Cave, Part 1.

1. Talk to Sobling Head Researcher at the Giant's Cave entrance and get quest.

2. Follow to Giant's Cave lower part and hunt:

Lesser Giant Soldier Lv. 62
Lesser Giant Scout Lv. 63
Lesser Giant Mage Lv. 64
Lesser Giant Elder Lv. 65

Dead monsters will drop Ancient Titan Book NG . Open it by double-click, to receive one of random items:

  • Giants Science & Technology
  • Titan Culture
  • Giant's Love Letter NG
  • Giants' Doodle NG

3. Collect full set of scrolls and trade it with Sobling Head Researcher on a random A-grade armor recipe.
