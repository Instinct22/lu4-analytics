Path of the Human Knight 18+

Quests to change class 13.07.2026

To become a Knight you must endure the legendary quest known as Path to a Human Knight.

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Path of the Human Knight Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Sir Klaus Vasper is located near the Gludin Warriors Guild. Talk to Sir Klaus Vasper to start the quest.

2. Head to the western gate of Gludin. Find Bezique Captain and talk to him.

3. Head to Gludin Temple. Find Levian High Priestess and talk to her.

4. Teleport from Gludin to Windmill Hill. Find and kill monsters:

Langk Lizardman Lv. 15
Arachnid Tracker Lv. 19
Venomous Spider Lv. 18

Until you collect:

Lizardman's Totem NG x 20
Venomous Spider's Leg NG x 20

5. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Bezique Captain at the western gate and talk to him.

Bezique will give Coin of Lords NG

6. Head to Gludin Temple. Find Levian High Priestess and talk to her.

Levian will give Coin of Lords NG

7. Teleport to Gludio. Find Bathis Captain at the northern gate and talk to him.

8. Leave Gludio through the eastern gate and follow the road north. Find and kill monsters:

Bugbear Raider Lv. 17

Until you collect Bugbear Necklace NG x 10

9. Return to Bathis Captain and talk to him.

Bathis will give Coin of Lords NG

10. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Sir Klaus Vasper near the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sir Klaus Vasper will complete the quest and give you Sword of Ritual NG

11. To change your profession, talk to Ramos Grand Master in the Gludin Warriors Guild.

The completed quest opens access to professions: Paladin and Dark Avenger
