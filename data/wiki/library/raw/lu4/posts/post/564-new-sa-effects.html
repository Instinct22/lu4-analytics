New SA effects

Items 22.04.2026

Basics

The Lu4 team has added the ability to insert a Soul Crystal into D-grade and C-grade weapons.

Dual weapons of D and C grades, similar to high-grade dual weapons, now gain effects when modified to +4. This makes early-season gameplay more varied and dynamic.

Pay attention!

For detailed information on the updated SA effects, read the article Updated SA effects
