Level 66 ~ 85
Type Repeatable
Reward

Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Moth gro-Bagol Hammersmith is in the camp near the entrance to the Wall of Argos. Talk to the orc and hunt monsters nearby.

2. Go to the Wall of Argos, find and kill monsters:

Canyon Antelope Slave Lv. 69
Canyon Bandersnatch Slave Lv. 71
Eye of Restrainer Lv. 71
Buffalo Slave Lv. 72
Eye of Guide Lv. 72
Gaze of Nightmare Lv. 72
Eye of Watchman Lv. 73
Homunculus Lv. 73
Eye of Pilgrim Lv. 74
Eye of Watchman Lv. 73
Grendel Slave Lv. 74
Elder Homunculus Lv. 75

Collect 500 Glimmering Eye of Argos NG .

3. Return to Moth gro-Bagol Hammersmith . Talk to him and get Argos memory fragments NG .

4. Find NPC Vernad Sky Library in any city, talk to him and get Londo's diary NG and Arkenia's Grimoire 61-75 Lv. A .

5. Return to Moth gro-Bagol Hammersmith , talk to him and gain access to exchange Glimmering Eye of Argos NG for recipes and parts of A-grade weapons and armor.
