Useful quests 17.02.2026


Fantasy Wine
Starting NPC Harlan Guard
Starting location Dion
Level 20 ~ 40
Type Repeatable
Reward
60 Year Old Wine NG
Exp NG x 15 000
SP NG x 1 500
Adena NG x 3 500

Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Harlan Guard is located at the northwestern wall of Dion. Talk to Harlan to start the quest.

2. Go hunting and kill monsters to collect:

Leaf of Eucalyptus NG x 80
Stone of Chill NG x 100

Dion

Exit the city through the northern gate and head north. Find and kill monsters:

Enku Orc Champion Lv. 21
Enku Orc Shaman Lv. 20

Partisan's Hideaway

Teleport from Dion to Partisan's Hideaway. Find and kill monsters:

Ol Mahum Sergeant Lv. 29
Ol Mahum Captain Lv. 30

3. Return to Harlan Guard and talk to him.

Harlan will complete the quest and give you 60 Year Old Wine NG .
