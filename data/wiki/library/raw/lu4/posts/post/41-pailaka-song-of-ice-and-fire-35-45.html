Pailaka — Song of Ice and Fire (35-45)

Useful quests 29.10.2025


"...Have you heard of Pailaka? Inspectors start finding them after the discovery of Kamaloka.

These places emerge under the influence of chaos. They are where powerful magic experiments take place, where many lives are lost, or where Kamaloka energy is unleashed. They don’t become Kamaloka but slip out of reality instead. And yet, they still exist...."

Inspector Adler

Basics

Pailaka is a one-time quest with a strictly limited level. To complete it, players must pass an instance. The reward is Experience and SP points, useful items, and Vitality points.

Pay attention!

In the basic game Pailaka — A Song of Ice and Fire is available to characters of level 36 ~ 42

On MasterWork this quest is available to players level 35 ~ 45

Quest guide

Talk to NPC Inspector Adler Pailaka - Song of Ice and Fire (Level 35-45) near the temple in Gludin to start the quest.

He will move the character to an instance — copy of the Forgotten Temple. You have 60 minutes to complete the instance.

"...The Spirits of Fire and Water once existed in perfect balance — but now, the elements have begun to merge. The source of this disturbance remains unknown. What is clear, however, is that some Spirits, infused with the unstable power of both elements, have transformed into dangerous monsters. To beat them was forged an exceptional weapon, and you will have the chance to wield it.

To restore peace within the Temple, the Spirits of the Mixed Elements must be eliminated..."

Talk to NPC Inspector Sinai near the Temple entrance

Get a Sprite's Sword D and Temple Book of Secrets NG from Inspector Sinai. The book lists monsters that need to be killed..

Forgotten Temple Map

— Instance entry
1 — Hillas Spirit of Water Lv. 36
2 — Papion Spirit of Water Lv. 36
3 — Kinsus Spirit of Fire Lv. 39
4 — Gargos Spirit of Fire Lv. 39
5 — Adiantum Lv. 42

Useful information

In different parts of the Temple you will find Crystal Water Bottle and Burning Brazier . Break these vessels to get useful items:

  • Water Attribute Enhancer NG — Increases Water resistance
  • Fire Attribute Enhancer NG — Increases Fire resistance
  • Quick Healing Potion NG — Instantly restores HP
  • Pailaka Instant Shield NG — Potion that gives invulnerability

"...In a cave of water, where even left and right are unbalanced, you will find that reckless Water Spirit, Hillas, waiting for you..."

Go to the water part of the temple. The fourth room on the right is inhabited by Hillas Spirit of Water Lv. 36

Kill the Spirit using the quest weapon. After the monster dies, you will receive the item Essence of Water NG

"So, you possess the Essence of Water? That changes everything. They say that if the sword’s power alone isn’t enough to protect the Temple, the Temple itself can strengthen it. And it’s true."

Return to the centre of the room and talk to the Temple Inspector.

The Inspector will enhance your weapon to Enhanced Sprite's Sword D . Equipping swords adds the skill Shalin Heal Lv. 1 , which restores 540 health when used.

"Take up the sword again and seek out other enemies. The book will be helpful!"

After reading the book, you learn that the next enemy is Papion Spirit of Water Lv. 36 , located on the bridge where the Water and Fire temples meet.

Kill the Spirit using the quest weapon to get the next clue.

"You have defeated the Water Sprite! However, the Fire Spirit is even fiercer and stronger. To face it, you must have the Essence of Water."

The book suggests finding the Kinsus Spirit of Fire Lv. 39 and taking the Essence of Fire. Go to the fire part of the temple. The monster we need is in the third room on the right.

Kill the Spirit using the quest weapon. After the monster dies, you will receive the Essence of Fire NG

"Tell me, has your weapon already absorbed the Essence of Water? Ah, then it's simple! We have never before tried to combine the Essences of Water and Fire, but now it should be possible. Now, give me your sword and the Essence of Fire"

Return to the central part of the room and talk to Temple Inspector.

The explorer will enhance your weapon to Sword of Ice and Fire D . Equipping the sword adds the skill Shalin Strike Lv. 1 , which deals physical damage when used.

"Ah! The sword is finished! I can feel its power! Cant' you feel it calling to the power of the Sprite?"

The book tells you where to find the new target. Gargos Spirit of Fire Lv. 39 lives on the right side of the next room.

Kill the Spirit using the quest weapon to get the next clue.

"Only those truly without fear may enter the very heart of chaos, where fire and water collide. There the Spirite Adiantum wields both the power of fire and water"

The final boss Adiantum Lv. 42 is in the room with the bridge between the fire and water parts of the Temple.

Return to the room with the bridge and dive underwater. On the left side of the room, there will be a path to a secret room.

After Adiantum dies, Inspector Adler Pailaka - Song of Ice and Fire (Level 35-45) will appear. Talk to him to get your reward.

Thanks to you, we are rapidly restoring stability. Of course, that means that the dimensional portal that connects your world to ours is also quickly closing.

Reward in the basic game

Exp NG x 1 860 000
SP NG x 480 000
Vitality Point NG x 10 000
Pailaka Earring D
Pailaka Ring D
Scroll of Escape NG

Pay attention!

The maximum Vitality is 20,000 points. That means that for the quest, you will receive half of the total Vitality.

For more information about Vitality levels, read the article Vitality System

Rewards on MasterWork

In the sixth season of MasterWork, the reward for completing the quest was moved to the Mission system.

Pay attention!

Experience gained from missions does not affect the Fatigue System.
