Merciless Punishment Level: 10 ~ 16
The exiled traitor Varangka schemes to overthrow the kingdom of the Orcs! Reveal his wicked plot and destroy him! Dejakar Oroka!
Start NPC
Hatos Urutu Chief
Location
Rewards
Butcher's Sword NG Lesser Healing Potion NG (100 pcs.) Soulshot: No Grade for Beginners NG (5 000 pcs.) Spiritshot: No Grade for Beginners NG (2 500 pcs.)

1: Stop Varangka's Conspiracy!

Varangka, Shaman of Darkness, is scheming to destroy the kingdom of the Orcs. Urutu Chief Hatos has learned of his plot, and has ordered the elimination of all of Varangka's followers. Kill them all! Centurion Parugon will tell you where to find them.

2: Catch Varangka's Messenger!

Varangka's messengers and escorts are advancing from the far eastern Immortal Plateau to the Raider's Crossroads! Kill them all!

This can't be the end! Search harder! Monsters to Hunt - Varangka's messengers.
