Lu4: Quest Adept of Taste 20+

Useful quests 17.02.2026


Adept of Taste
Starting NPC Jonas
Starting location Dion
Reward 20 ~ 40
Type Repeatable
Reward
Jonas's Sauce Recipe NG
Exp NG x 53 000
SP NG x 5 300
Adena NG x 10 000

Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Jonas is located in Dion's Warehouse. Talk to Jonas to start the quest.

2. Head to the Grocery Store. Find Sonia Accessory Merchant and talk to her.

Sonia will give Sonia's Botany Book NG

3. Head to the Temple. Find Jacob Guard at the western wall before the hill and talk to him.

Jacob will give Jacob's Insect Book NG

4. Head to the Temple. Talk to the two NPCs in the Temple one by one.

4.1. Talk to Glyvka Priestess .

Glyvka will give Glyvka's Botany Book NG

4.2. Talk to Rollant Magister .

Rollant will give Rollant's Creature Book NG

5. Leave the city through the southern gate. Head to Floran Village. In the village find Pano Grocer and talk to him.

Pano will give Pano's Contract NG

6. Teleport to Dion. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Find and kill monsters:

Hobgoblin Lv. 21
Monster Eye Searcher Lv. 22
Monster Eye Gazer Lv. 25

Until you collect:

Hobgoblin Amulet NG x 30
Body of Monster Eye NG x 30

7. Head east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Mandragora Sprout Lv. 20
Mandragora Sprout Lv. 21
Mandragora Blossom Lv. 25
Mandragora Sapling Lv. 23

Until you collect Red Mandragora Root NG x 40

8. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Marshlands. Find and kill monsters:

Gray Ant Lv. 26
Giant Crimson Ant Lv. 28
Stinger Wasp Lv. 30

Until you collect:

Green Marsh Moss NG x 20
Nectar NG x 20

Stinger Wasp can be dangerous. If you have trouble killing it, teleport to Dion, then to Plains of Dion. Find and kill monsters:

Bloody Bee Lv. 23 until you collect Nectar NG x 20

9. Head to the Grocery Store. Find Sonia Accessory Merchant and talk to her.

Sonia will give Red Mandragora Sap NG

10. Head to the Temple. Find Jacob Guard at the western wall before the hill and talk to him.

Select the line "Ask him to make the honey." in the dialogue.

Jacob will give Honey NG

11. Head to the Temple and talk to the two NPCs there, one after the other.

11.1. Talk to Glyvka Priestess .

In the dialogue, select the line "Ask Priestess Glyvka to prepare the moss that was brought."

Glyvka will give you Green Moss Bundle NG .

11.2. Talk to Rollant Magister .

Rollant will give you Monster Eye Meat NG .

12. Leave the city through the southern gate. Head to Floran Village. In the village, find Pano Grocer and talk to him.

Pano will give you Dionian Potato NG .

13. Teleport to Dion. Find Jonas in the Warehouse and talk to him.

Jonas will give Jonas's 2nd Steak Dish NG

14. Head to the Temple. Find Mirien Magister and talk to her.

Mirien will give Mirien's Review NG

15. Return to Jonas and talk to him.

Jonas will complete the quest and give you Jonas's Sauce Recipe NG .
