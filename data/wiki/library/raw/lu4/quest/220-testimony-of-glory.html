Quest Testimony of Glory

Quests to change class 23.06.2026

An eternal winter is coming! To survive the imminent chaos you must be able to unite the power of all the Orc tribes. Are you ready for such a task?

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Testimony of Glory Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Vokian Prefect is located in the Orc Guild of Giran. Talk to Vokian to start the quest.

Vokian will give Vokian's Order NG

2. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to the Dragon Valley. Go west to the Death Pass. Find and kill monsters:

Guardian Basilisk Lv. 36

Until you collect Guardian Basilisk Fang NG x 10

3. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Manashen Gargoyle Lv. 40

Until you collect Manashen Shard NG x 10

4. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Head to the center of the Wastelands. Find and kill monsters:

Tyrant Lv. 35
Tyrant Kingpin Lv. 36

Until you collect Tyrant Talon NG x 10

5. Teleport to Giran. Find Vokian Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Vokian will give:

Vokian's Order NG
Necklace of Authority NG

6. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 1st Order NG

7. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Manakia about all the types of orcs and he will give you:

Manakia's 1st Letter NG
Manakia's 2nd Letter NG

8. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Head to the top of the northwestern hill. Find Voltar Breka Chief and talk to him.

Voltar will give Gloves of Voltar NG . After the conversation will appear the quest monsters:

Pashika Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37
Vultus Son Of Voltar Quest Monster Lv. 37

Kill the orcs and get:

Pashika's Head NG
Vultus' Head NG

Talk to Voltar again. He will give you Scepter of Breka NG

9. Teleport to Dion, then to Bee Hive. Head west along the coast, towards Hive Fortress. At the entrance to the Fortress, find Kepra Enku Chief and talk to him.

Kepra will give you Gloves of Kepra NG . After the conversation will appear 4 quest monsters:

Enku Orc Overlord Quest Monster Lv. 33

Kill orcs and get Enku Overlord Head NG x 4

Talk to Kepra again. He will give you Scepter of Enku NG

10. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Kasman Prefect in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Ask Kasman about all the types of orcs. He will tell you:

Kasman's 1st Letter NG
Kasman's 2nd Letter NG
Kasman's 3rd Letter NG

11. Teleport from Gludin Village to Orc Barracks. Head deeper into the location. Find Burai Turek Chief and talk to him.

Burai will give Gloves of Burai NG . After the conversation will appear the quest monsters :

Makum Bugbear Thug Quest Monster Lv. 37

From killed monsters get Makum Bugbear Head NG x 2

Talk to Burai again. He will give you Scepter of Turek NG

12. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Langk Lizardmen Dwellings. Head northwest along the coast. Find Harak Leunt Chief on the coast and talk to him.

Harak will give Scepter of Tunath NG

13. Teleport to Gludio. Exit the city through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge, then swim across the river. Find Driko Vuku Chief near the ruined house and talk to him.

Driko will give Driko's Contract NG

14. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35

Until you collect Stakato Drone Husk NG x 30

15. Return to Driko Vuku Chief and talk to him.

Driko will give Scepter of Vuku NG

16. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's 3rd Order NG

17. Teleport to Oren. Exit the city through the western gate and go down the slope. Find and kill monsters:

Timak Orc Overlord Lv. 45

Until you collect Timak Orc Head NG x 20

The quest items drop from all Timak Orcs in the Outpost.

18. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill. Find and kill monsters:

Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42

Until you collect Tamlin Orc Skull NG x 20

19. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Scepter Box NG

20. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of King and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Tanapi's Order NG

21. Teleport from Orc Village to Immortal Plateau South. Follow the road north and turn east at the fork to the foot of the mountains. Find and kill monsters:

Ragna Orc Overlord Lv. 39
Ragna Orc Seer Lv. 39

Until the quest monster appears:

Revenant of Tantos Chief Quest Monster Lv. 39

From the killed monster, you will receive Scepter of Tantos NG

Alternate monster location: The ravine south of the entrance to the Cave Of Trials.

22. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Tanapi Seer on the first floor of the Hall of King and talk to him.

Tanapi will give Ritual Box NG

23. Head to the second floor of the Hall of the King. Find Kakai Flame Lord and talk to him.

Kakai will complete the quest and give you Mark of Glory NG
