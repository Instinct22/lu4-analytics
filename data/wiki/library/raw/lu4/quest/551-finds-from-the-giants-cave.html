Main Quests (Lu4) Finds from the Giant’s Cave
Lu4
Finds from the Giant’s Cave Level: 65 ~ 75
According to Sobling, traces of ancient giant workshops still remain in the lower halls of the Giant’s Cave. Among the creatures dwelling there, sealed papyri can sometimes be found containing fragments of production records: armor schematics, weapon forging notes, and remnants of ruined prototypes.
