Keen Claws Level: 3 ~ 9
Trader Payne has developed a unique arrow that uses a wolf claw as its arrowhead! It is most effective in siege situations. Payne is paying rewards to whoever brings him wolf claws to fill the huge order of Kiel Nighthawk.
Start NPC
Payne Armor Merchant
Location
Rewards
Exp NG (1 500 pcs.) SP NG (150 pcs.) Adena NG (300 pcs.) Lesser Healing Potion NG (5 pcs.) Magic Haste Potion NG

1: Collect Claws

Trader Payne ask collect and bring him 30 Wolf Claws. Hunt Ashen Wolves and Goblins.

Wolf Claw NG (30 pcs.)

2: Return to Trader Payne

You've collected 30 wolf claws. Take them to Trader Payne so that he can make the arrows.
