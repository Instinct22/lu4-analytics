Quest Test of the Lord

Quests to change class 23.06.2026

Overlords are born, not made. to become an Overlord who also holds the position of Chief, the candidate must pass the Test of the Lord. The test to determine if you are worthy to become an Overlord has begun!

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Test of the Lord Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Kakai Flame Lord is located on the second floor of the Hall of the King in the Orc Village. Talk to Kakai to start the quest.

Kakai will give you Ordeal Necklace NG

2. Talk to three NPCs in the Hall of King in Orc Village, one by one.

2.1. On the second floor of the Hall of King, find Tantus Neruga Chief and talk to him.

Tantus will give Tantus's Charm NG

2.2. On the stairs of the Hall of King, find Varkees Atuba Chief and talk to him.

Varkees will give Varkee's Charm NG

2.3. On the other stairs of the Hall of King, find Hatos Urutu Chief and talk to him.

Hatos will give Hatos's Charm NG

3. Head to the Orc Village Blacksmith's Shop. Find Sumari Blacksmith and talk to him.

Sumari will give Sumari's Letter NG

4. Head to the Orc Village Weapon Shop. Find Jakal Weapon Merchant and talk to him.

Jakal will give Neruga Axe Blade NG

You must pay Adena NG x 1000

5. Buy 1000 Bone Arrow D in a Grocery Store

Head to the Hall of Kings. Find Tantus Neruga Chief on the second floor and talk to him.

Tantus will give Axe of Ceremony NG

6.1. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Chianta's Charm NG

6.2. Also, in the Orc Guild, find Somak Seer and talk to him.

Somak will give Urutu Blade NG

7. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Manakia will give Manakia's Orders NG

8. Teleport from Giran to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35
Breka Orc Shaman Lv. 34

Until you collect Breka Orc Fang NG x 20

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, and others are on the northwestern hill.

9. Return to Manakia Seer and talk to him.

Manakia will give Manakia's Amulet NG

10. Teleport to Oren. Find Takuna Duda-Mara Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Takuna will give Takuna's Charm NG

11. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Spider Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Marsh Spider's Feeler NG x 10
Marsh Spider's Feet NG x 10

12. Return to Takuna Duda-Mara Chief and talk to him.

Takuna will give Handiwork Spider Brooch NG

13. Exit Oren through the western gate. Go down the slope. Find and kill monsters:

Timak Orc Overlord Lv. 45

Until you collect Timak Orc Skull NG x 10

The quest item drops from all Timak Orcs in Timak Outpost.

14. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Instead of going inside, go down into the crater. Find and kill monsters:

Enchanted Monstereye Lv. 41

Until you collect Enchanted Monster Cornea NG x 20

15. Teleport to Dion. Find Chianta Gandi Chief in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Chianta will give Monster Eye Woodcarving NG

16. Teleport to Orc Village. Head to the Hall of the King. Find and talk to three NPCs one by one.

16.1. On the stairs of the Hall of Kings. Find Varkees Atuba Chief and talk to him.

Varkees will give Huge Orc Fang NG

16.2. On the other stairs of the Hall of Kings. Find Hatos Urutu Chief and talk to him.

Hatos will give Sword Into Skull NG

16.3. On the second floor of the Hall of Kings, find Kakai Flame Lord and talk to him.

Kakai will give Bear Fang Necklace NG

17. Teleport from Orc Village to Cave Of Trials. Head inside the cave. Keep to the right side at all times. Inside the cave, find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Martankus's Charm NG

You can reach the next location on foot.

18. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Immortal Plateau, South. Follow the road north, and at the fork, turn east to the foot of the mountains. Find and kill monsters:

Ragna Orc Overlord Lv. 39
Ragna Orc Seer Lv. 39

Until you receive:

Ragna Orc Head NG
Ragna Chief Notice NG

Alternate monster location: The ravine south of the entrance to the Cave Of Trials.

19. Return to Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will give Immortal Flame NG

20. Teleport to Orc Village. Find Kakai Flame Lord on the second floor of the Hall of King and talk to him.

Kakai will complete the quest and give you Mark of Lord NG
