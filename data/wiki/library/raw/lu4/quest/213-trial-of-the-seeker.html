Quest Trial of the Seeker

Quests to change class 23.06.2026

The peace of the kingdom is threatened by mutant evil spirits. The Seeker Guild wants to conduct secret reconnaissance to find out what they're up to. It's a tough, dangerous job. Are you certain that you're prepared to follow the Path of the Seeker?

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Trial of the Seeker Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Dufner Master is located in the Giran Warriors Guild. Talk to Dufner to start the quest.

Dufner will give you Dufners Letter NG

2. Teleport to Dion, find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give you Terry's 1st Order NG

3. Exit the Dion through the eastern gate. Head along the road east to the Execution Grounds. Find and kill monsters:

Neer Ghoul Berserker Lv. 29

Until you get Mysterious Spirit Ore NG

4. Return to Terry Master and talk to him.

Terry will give you Terry's 2nd Order NG

5. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to Orc Barracks. Find and kill monsters:

Turek Orc Warlord Lv. 30

Until you get Turek Spirit Ore NG

6. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Captain Lv. 31

Until you get Ant Spirit Ore NG

7. Teleport to Giran, exit the city through the eastern gate, cross the bridge, and follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden. Find and kill monsters:

Turak Bugbear Warrior Lv. 33

Until you get Turak Bugbear Spirit Ore NG

8. Teleport to Dion, then to Fortress of Resistance. Find and kill monsters:

Ol Mahum Captain Lv. 30

Until you get Ol Mahum Spirit Ore NG

9. Teleport to Dion, find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give you Terry's Box NG

10. Teleport to Hunter's Village, find Viktor Weapon Merchant in the Weapon Shop and talk to him.

Select the second line in the dialogue "Altebar Karryos"

Viktor will issue Viktor's Request NG

11. Teleport to Giran, exit the city through the eastern gate and cross the bridge. Follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden. Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect Medusa Scales NG x 10

12. Return to Viktor Weapon Merchant and talk to him.

Viktor will give you:

Shilen's Spirit Ore NG
Analysis Request NG

13. Teleport to Oren, then to the Ivory Tower. Find Marina Magister on the second floor and talk to her.

Marina will give the Marina's Letter NG

14. Teleport to Dwarven Village, find Brunon Blacksmith in the Blacksmith's shop and talk to him.

Brunon will give you the Experiment Tools NG

15. Return to Marina Magister and talk to her.

Marina will give you the Analysis Result NG

16. Teleport to Dion, find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give you the List of Host NG

17. Teleport from Dion to Cruma Tower. Instead of going inside, head northeast. Find and kill monsters:

Marsh Stakato Drone Lv. 35

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG

18. Teleport to Oren, then to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Warrior Lv. 38

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG

19. Teleport to Giran, then to Breka's Stronghold. Find and kill monsters:

Breka Orc Overlord Lv. 35

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG

Several monsters are east of the teleport point, and others are on the northwestern hill.

20. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Find and kill monsters:

Ant Warrior Captain Lv. 36

Until you get Abyss Spirit Ore NG

21. Teleport to Dion, find Terry Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Terry will give you Terry's Report NG

22. Teleport to Giran, find Dufner Master in the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Dufner will complete the quest and give you a Mark of Seeker NG
