Quest Test of the War Spirit

Quests to change class 23.06.2026

One of the highest posts a Shaman can hope to achieve is Warcryer. The Warcryer calls on the spirits of his ancestors who died in battle. To become a Warcryer you must retrieve the ashes of a great ancestor!

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Test of the War Spirit Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Somak Seer is located in the Orc Guild of Dion. Talk to Somak to start the quest.

2. Teleport to Gludio. Find Racoy Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Racoy will give Racoy's Totem NG

3. Head to the Temple of Gludio. Find Vivyan Priestess and talk to her.

Vivyan will give Vivyan's Letter NG

4. Teleport to Gludin Village, then to the southern entrance to the Wasteland. Find Sarien Grocer in the camp and talk to him.

Sarien will give Insect Diagram Book NG

5. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ant Nest. Go inside the cave. Head to the rooms near the Epic Boss. Find and kill monsters:

Noble Ant Lv. 37
Noble Ant Leader Lv. 38

Until you collect:

Kiruna's Skull NG
Kiruna's Rib Bone NG
Kiruna's Spine NG
Kiruna's Arm Bone NG
Kiruna's Thigh Bone NG

6. Teleport to Gludio. Find Racoy Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Racoy will give Kiruna's Remains NG

7. Teleport to Giran. Find Manakia Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Manakia will give Manakia's Totem NG

8. Exit Giran through the eastern gate. Cross the bridge and follow the road northeast to the Gorgon Flower Garden. Find and kill monsters:

Medusa Lv. 34

Until you collect:

Hermodt's Rib Bone NG
Hermodt's Spine NG
Hermodt's Arm Bone NG
Hermodt's Thigh Bone NG

North of the Necropolis of Martyrdom, find and kill the quest monster:

Stenoa Gorgon Queen Quest Monster Lv. 37

From the killed monster you get Hermodt's Skull NG

9. Return to Manakia Seer and talk to him.

Manakia will give Hermodt's Remains NG

10. Teleport from Giran to the entrance to Dragon Valley. Go north to Death Pass. Find Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give Orim's Contract NG

11. Teleport to Dion, then to Cruma Tower. On the first floor of the tower, find and kill monsters:

Porta Lv. 40
Excuro Lv. 41
Mordeo Lv. 42

Until you collect:

Porta's Eye NG x 10
Excuro's Scale NG x 10
Mordeos Talon NG x 10

12. Return to Orim Shadow and talk to him.

Orim will give Braki's Remains NG

13. Teleport to Oren. Find Pekiron Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Pekiron will give Pekiron's Totem NG

14. Teleport from Oren to Plains of the Lizardmen. Find and kill monsters:

Leto Lizardman Overlord Lv. 40
Leto Lizardman Shaman Lv. 39

Until you collect:

Tonar's Skull NG
Tonar's Rib Bone NG
Tonar's Spine NG
Tonar's Arm Bone NG
Tonar's Thigh Bone NG

15. Return to Pekiron Seer and talk to him.

Pekiron will give Tonar's Remains NG

16. Teleport to Dion. Find Somak Seer in the Orc Guild and talk to him.

Somak will give Vendetta Totem NG

17. Teleport to Hunter's Village. Exit the village through the western gate and follow the road down the hill. Find and kill monsters:

Tamlin Orc Lv. 41
Tamlin Orc Archer Lv. 42

Until you collect Tamlin Orc Head NG x 13

18. Return to Somak Seer and talk to him.

Somak will give:

Warspirit Totem NG
Braki's Remains NG
Tonar's Remains NG
Hermodt's Remains NG
Kiruna's Remains NG

19. Teleport to Orc Village, then to Cave Of Trials. Head inside and keep the right side. Inside the cave find Ancestor Martankus and talk to him.

Martankus will complete the quest and give Mark of Warspirit NG
