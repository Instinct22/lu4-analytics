Lu4: Quest Coins of Magic 40+

Useful quests 17.02.2026

Basics

The Quest Chain Coins of Magic is one of the main quests for obtaining C-grade Armor, Jewelry and Weapon Parts. The Lu4 team has reworked the quest chain and rewards while maintaining the overall concept.

The quest involves hunting monsters and collecting Magic Coins, which can be exchanged for Equipment at an NPC.

Lu4 introduces important changes to a quest familiar to many players. Features of the quest on Lu4:

  • The list of monsters for hunting has been reworked
  • All types of Magic Coins drop from all monsters included in the hunting list
  • Coin exchange is only required to increase the Coin Collector rank
  • Increasing the Coin Collector rank grants access to more expensive goods and continuation of the quest chain
  • To purchase Equipment, only Coins obtained from monster hunting are required
  • Coins can be exchanged and traded with other players

In the subsequent quests of the chain, you can obtain B-grade weapon recipes. To purchase them, you will need Magic Coins, which can only be obtained in this quest.

Join the Coin Collectors and collect Magic Coins!

Who would have thought that coin collecting could lead to such horrific chaos throughout the kingdom? Once you start, you cannot escape its grip. Do you think you can get the 1st Class Coin Collector Membership?

Introductory quest

First, you need to get Coin Collector Membership level III. To do this, complete the Sorint task.

Obtaining Coin Collector Membership level III

1. Talk to Sorint Warehouse Keeper in the Hunter's Village Warehouse Building.

2. Head to the central building of Hunter's Village. Find Bernard Guild President and talk to him.

3. Teleport to the Field of Silence, find and kill monsters:

Lienrik Lv. 39
Lienrik Lad Lv. 40

Until you receive Kaldis Gold Dragon NG

4. Return to Sorint Warehouse Keeper and talk to him. Sorint will give you a reward — Coin Collector Membership level III.

Collect Magic Coins to increase your Coin Collector rank and purchase C-grade Armor, Jewelry, and Weapon Parts.

Increasing rank

Coin Collectors can increase their rank to purchase more expensive goods and continue the quest chain.

The Coin Collector Membership Level determines the range of available C-grade Armor, Jewelry, and Weapon Parts for purchase:

  • Level III — Low C
  • Level II — Low C + Mid C
  • Level I — Low C + Mid C + Top C and access to the next quest

Increasing your Coin Collector rank requires collecting a different type of Magic Coins, which can only be obtained through exchange at an NPC.

Gather the required amount of Coins, then talk to Sorint Warehouse Keeper in Hunter's Village to increase your rank:

To raise the Coin Collector rank to I, you need 220 Coins of each type, obtained from hunting monsters, namely:

Gold Wyvern NG x 220

To purchase Equipment, only Coins collected from hunting monsters are required.

Other types of Magic Coins are only needed to raise the Coin Collector rank.

Purchasing Equipment

Coin Collectors can purchase valuable goods using collected Magic Coins and Adena. The Coin Collector's rank determines the range of available items.

Talk to Sorint Warehouse Keeper in Hunter's Village to purchase C-grade Armor, Jewelry, and Weapon Parts.
