Lizardmen's Conspiracy Level: 25 ~ 34
The Maille Lizardmen slowly and menacingly approach the castle. Their secret power will soon be revealed.
Start NPC
Praga Guard
Location
Rewards
Flexible Reward NG

1: The Guard's Report

Magister Rohmer of the Town of Gludio has been observing the activities of the Maille Lizardmen on the west side of town. Guard Praga asks you to deliver the data and reports his men have collected to Magister Rohmer.

Patrol's Report NG

2: Search for Beads

Master Romer asks to hunt Maille lizardmen and spiders to get beads. Monsters to Hunt: Maille Lizardman Warriors, Maille Lizardman Shamans, Maille Lizardman Matriarchs, Poison Arachnids and King Arachnids.

Shining Gem NG (999 pcs.)
Shining Red Gem NG (999 pcs.)

3: Collection Complete

You've discovered clues to the conspiracy between the Maille Lizardmen and the arachnids. Return to Magister Rohmer.
