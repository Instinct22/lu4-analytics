Lu4 Eternal MasterWork
Forgotten Truth Level: 10 ~ 15
The infamous Orc Brigand has stolen Kaysha's Revelations! Retrieve this vital document to secure the future of all Dark Elves!
Start NPC
Thifiell Tetrarch
Location
Rewards
Lesser Healing Potion NG (100 pcs.) Eldritch Dagger NG Apprentice's Spellbook NG Question Mark NG

1: Kaysha's Revelations

Dark Elven Tetrarch Thifiell asked Abyssal Celebrant Kartia to translate an ancient manuscript. He now asks you to retrieve the translated copy of 'Kaysha's Revelations'. You must be very discreet.

2: In Search of the Stolen Revelations

You hear that 'Kaysha's Revelations' was stolen by Tumran Orcs! Kill the Tumran Orc brigands that were last seen camping near the Altar of Rites, and recover the original copy of 'Kaysha's Revelations' and a clay tablet.

Ancient Scroll NG
Ancient Clay Tablet NG

3: Return to Abyssal Celebrant Kartia

You have recovered the ancient scroll and clay tablet. Abyssal Celebrant Kartia is waiting for you.

4: Translation of the Completed Revelations

The translation of 'Kaysha's Revelations' is complete. Tetrarch Thifiell is waiting for you.
