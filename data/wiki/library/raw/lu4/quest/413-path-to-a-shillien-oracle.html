Path of the Shillien Oracle 18+

Quests to change class 13.07.2026

Shillien Oracles seek truth and enlightenment in the darkness that surrounds us. Not all are worthy of such a noble profession.

Pay Attention!

1. Lu4 is currently in the alpha stage. NPC names and quest item titles may differ from those mentioned in the guide. Some teleportation points might be unavailable.

2. On Lu4, all quest items drop for all party members simultaneously. Collaborating with other players makes the quest easier for all party members.

3. The guides are written with a focus on speed and convenience when completing quests, and Adena costs for teleportation are not important. Ways to save Adena include:

  • Teleporting short distances. For example, Gludio > Dion > Giran is cheaper than Gludio > Giran directly
  • Using teleportation scrolls to specific towns, such as Scroll of Escape: Town of Giran NG
  • Travelling on foot

Path of the Shillien Oracle Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Sidra Magister is located in the Dark Elf Guild of Gludio. Talk to Sidra to start the quest.

Sidra will give Sidra's Letter NG

2. Teleport to Gludin Village. Find Talbot Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to him.

3. Teleport to Dark Elf Village, then to Spider Nest. Head west towards the mountains. Find and kill monsters:

Dark Succubus Lv. 20

Until you collect Bloody Rune NG x 5

4. Return to Talbot Magister and talk to him.

Talbot will give Prayer of Adonius NG

5. Head to Gludin Temple. Find Adonius Priest and talk to him.

6. Teleport to Gludio, then to Ruins of Despair. Find and kill monsters:

Skeleton Infantryman Lv. 16
Shield Skeleton Lv. 15
Zombie Soldier Lv. 19
Zombie Warrior Lv. 22

Until you collect Ashen Bones NG x 10

7. Return to Adonius Priest and talk to him.

Adonius will give Andariel Book NG

8. Teleport to Gludio. Find Sidra Magister in the Dark Elf Guild and talk to her.

Sidra will complete the quest and give you Orb of Abyss NG

9. To change your profession, talk to Tobias Grand Master in the Dark Elf Guild of Gludio.

A completed quest opens access to the profession: Shillen Elder
