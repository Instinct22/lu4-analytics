Quest Pursuit of Clan Ambition! (5 level)

Useful quests 09.07.2025


Pursuit of Clan Ambition!
Starting NPC Sir Gustav Athebaldt
Starting location Oren
Level 0 ~ 85
Type One-time
Reward
Seal of Aspiration NG
SP NG x 250 000

Quest guide

1. The starting NPC Sir Kristof Rodemai is near the Oren Warriors Guild. Talk to Sir Gustav to start the quest.

Sir Gustav Athebaldt will give Gustav's 1st Letter NG

Only a Clan Leader of level 4 can start the quest.

2. Teleport to Giran. Find Martien at the northeastern exit and talk to him.

In the second point of the quest there is a difference between early and late stages of the server.

Early Interlude stages

2.1.1. Teleport from Giran to Antharas' Lair — Entrance. Head to Dragon Valley to the northern path and find Corpse Of Kurtz . Search the body to get:

Mist Drake's Egg NG x 6
Brooch of the Magpie NG

2.1.2. Move along the northern path, find and kill monsters:

Drake Lv. 57
Thunder Wyrm Lv. 56

Until you collect:

Drake's Egg NG x 10
Thunder Wyrm Egg NG x 10

2.1.3. On the northern trail of Dragon Valley find Corpse Of Lutz . Search the body to get:

Mist Drake's Egg NG x 4
Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 3

Two monsters will appear near the body:

Blitz Wyrm Quest Monster Lv. 60

Kill Wyrms and get Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 2

2.1.4. In the center of Dragon Valley find Corpse Of Fritz . Search the body to get:

Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 3

Two monsters will appear near the body:

Blitz Wyrm Quest Monster Lv. 60

Kill Wyrms and get Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 2

To continue the quest you must have:

Drake's Egg NG x 10
Thunder Wyrm Egg NG x 10
Mist Drake's Egg NG x 10
Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 10
Brooch of the Magpie NG

Late High Five stages

2.2.1. Teleport from Giran to Dragon Valley. Head west to Death Pass and find Corpse Of Fritz . Search the body to get:

Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 3

Two monsters will appear near the body:

Blitz Wyrm Quest Monster Lv. 60

Kill Wyrms and get Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 2

2.2.2. Near Flitz's Corpse, find and kill monsters:

Drake Lv. 57
Thunder Wyrm Lv. 56

Until you collect:

Drake's Egg NG x 10
Thunder Wyrm Egg NG x 10

2.2.3. Head north to Death Pass and find Corpse Of Lutz (Corpse of Lutz). Search the body to get:

Mist Drake's Egg NG x 4
Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 3

Two monsters will appear near the body:

Blitz Wyrm Quest Monster Lv. 60

Kill Wyrms and get Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 2

2.2.4. Head north to Death Pass and find Corpse Of Kurtz . Search the body to get:

Mist Drake's Egg NG x 6
Brooch of the Magpie NG

To continue the quest you must have:

Drake's Egg NG x 10
Thunder Wyrm Egg NG x 10
Mist Drake's Egg NG x 10
Blitz Wyrm Egg NG x 10
Brooch of the Magpie NG

3. Teleport to Giran. Find Kusto Head Blacksmith in the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Kusto will give Black Anvil Coin NG

4. In Giran, at the northeastern exit, find Martien and talk to him.

5. Teleport to Oren. Find Sir Gustav Athebaldt near the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sir Gustav Athebaldt will give Gustav's 2nd Letter NG

6. Teleport to Hunters Village. Find Balthazar near the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

Balthazar will give you Recipe: Titan's Powerstone NG

In the sixth point of the quest there is a difference between the early and late stages of the server.

Early Interlude stages

6.1.1 Teleport to Aden, then to the Giant's Cave. Find and kill monsters:

Lesser Giant Scout Lv. 63
Lesser Giant Soldier Lv. 62

Until you collect:

Nebulite Crystals NG x 10
Titan's Powerstone NG x 10

Killed monsters can drop Broken Titan's Powerstone NG
Using the recipe obtained earlier, you can turn 1 Broken Giant Machine into 1 Giant Machine.

Late High Five stages

6.2.1 Teleport to Aden, then in the Fields of Massacre find and kill monsters:

Spiteful Soul Leader Lv. 65

Until you collect Spiteful Soul Energy NG x 10

There are no differences in the following points.

7. Teleport to Hunters Village. Find Balthazar near the Blacksmith Shop and talk to him.

8. Teleport to Oren near the Warriors Guild. Find Sir Gustav Athebaldt and talk to him.

Sir Gustav Athebaldt will give Gustav's 3rd Letter NG

9. Teleport to Aden. Find Sir Eric Rodemai in the central square and talk to him.

10. Leave Aden through the eastern gate. Follow the road towards the Cemetry. At the first fork, find Cleo Witch and talk to her.

After the conversation Kalis Witch will appear

11. Return to Sir Eric Rodemai and talk to him.

12. Teleport from Aden to The Forbidden Gateway, then head to the Cemetery. Find and kill monsters:

Grave Guard Lv. 54

Until the quest monster appears:

Grave Keymaster Quest Monster Lv. 65

Kill quest monsters until you collect Imperial Key NG x 6

13. Head to the western part of the Cemetery. Find two pits with monsters. Inside the pits, find and kill monsters Imperial Gravekeeper Quest Monster Lv. 60 , until the following appear:

Imperial Coffer
Imperial Slave Quest Monster Lv. 56 x 4

Open the Emperor's Chest to receive Scepter of Judgment NG

There is no need to kill the Imperial Slave.

Characters who attack the Imperial Gravekeeper may be teleported outside the Cemetery.

14. Teleport to Aden. Find Sir Eric Rodemai in the central square and talk to him.

15. Teleport to Oren. Find Sir Gustav Athebaldt near the Warriors Guild and talk to him.

Sir Gustav Athebaldt will complete the quest and give you Seal of Aspiration NG

Use the Seal of Aspiration to develop your clan to level 5.
