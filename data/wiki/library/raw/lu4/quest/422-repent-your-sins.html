Repent Your Sins (PK removal)

Useful quests 14.01.2026

The Black Judge gives sinners a chance for redemption. Will you walk the path of penitence, sinner? Beware, it is filled with trials and tribulations!

Starting NPC Black Judge
Starting location

Wasterland, Gludin Territory

Floran Village, Dion Territory

Hardin's Private Academy, Giran Territory
Level 1 ~ 85
Type Repeatable
Condition PK count ≥ 1
Reward Reduces PK counter by 1 - 10 per pet level

If you obtain or change subclass during the quest or while leveling up your pet, the quest will be canceled and the manacles will disappear.

Quest guide

1. Talk to Black Judge to start the quest.

You can find her in three locations:

Wasteland. Teleport to the Wasteland from Gludin. Turn your back to the Wasteland entrance and run along the right side past the vendors. The Black Judge is standing near a rocks.

Screenshot with map

Floran Village. Teleport to Floran from the Newbie Helper or Club Manager. Leave the village through the southern gate and run down the road to the mill. The Black Judge is standing near the mill.

Screenshot with map

Hardin's Private Academy. Teleport from Giran to Hardin's Private Academy. Turn your back to the cave entrance and run deeper into the area. On your right, you'll see a rocks between the trees — the Black Judge is standing on one of the rocks.

Screenshot with map

Levels 1 ~ 20

2. The Black Judge asks you to help Katari. Teleport from the Dark Elf Village to the Swampland.

Run east through the swamp; near two tall cliffs, find Katari and talk to her. Quest tracking will help you locate Katari.

3. Run or teleport to the Neutral Zone from the Dark Elf Village.

Find and kill the following monster:

Scavenger Wererat Lv. 18

Collect Ratman Scavenger's Skull NG x 10.

4. Return to Katari and talk to her. Katari is located in the northeast of the Swampland. Quest tracking will help you find her.

5. Return to the Black Judge and talk to her to receive the next task.

6. Teleport to Giran and find Pushkin Master Blacksmith in the blacksmith shop. Talk to him.

The blacksmith asks you to bring materials to craft the manacles:

  • Varnish NG x 15
  • Animal Skin NG x 15
  • Iron Ore NG x 15
  • Cokes NG x 1
  • Steel NG x 1

Gather the resources and talk to Pushkin Master Blacksmith to receive Penitent's Manacles NG .

7. Return to the Black Judge and talk to her to receive the Sin Eater.

Level up the pet to reduce your PK count.

For each level gained, the Black Judge will randomly decrease your PK by 1–10.

The summoned pet takes 100% of the experience from killing monsters. You don't need to feed the pet.

Levels 21 ~ 30

2. The Black Judge asks you to help Piotur. Teleport to the Orc Barracks from Gludin.

Head east and find Piotur near a house and talk to him.

3. On the hill near Piotur, find and kill the following monsters:

Turek War Hound Lv. 24

Collect Turek War Hound's Tail NG x 10.

4. Return to Piotur and talk to him.

5. Return to the Black Judge and talk to her to receive the next task.

6. Teleport to Giran, find Pushkin Master Blacksmith in the blacksmith shop, and talk to him.

The blacksmith asks for materials to craft the manacles:

  • Varnish NG x 15
  • Animal Skin NG x 15
  • Iron Ore NG x 15
  • Cokes NG x 1
  • Steel NG x 1

Gather the materials and talk to Pushkin Master Blacksmith to receive Penitent's Manacles NG .

7. Return to the Black Judge and talk to her to receive the Sin Eater.

Level up the pet to reduce your PK count.

For each level gained, the Black Judge will randomly decrease your PK by 1–10.

The summoned pet takes 100% of the experience from killing monsters. You don't need to feed the pet.

Levels 31 ~ 40
