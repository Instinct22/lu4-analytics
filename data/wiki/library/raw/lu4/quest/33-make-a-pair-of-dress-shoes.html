Main Quests (Lu4) Make a Pair of Dress Shoes
Lu4 Eternal MasterWork
Make a Pair of Dress Shoes Level: 60 ~ 85
While making formal wear, Leikar asks you to go to Trader Woodley get some dress shoes. Woodley doesn't make them, however.
Start NPC
Woodley Armor Merchant
Location

1: Meet Leikar

Woodley asks you to meet Leikar and get the materials for the formal wear she's making. Find Leikar and get some fabric.

2: Meet Woodley

Leikar doesn't have any more of the formal wear fabric, but she's willing to make some for Woodley if you tell him it's the one that she made long ago.

3: Gather Ingredients

Woodley needs materials to make the dress shoes and asks you to bring him 200 leather, 600 thread and 500,000 adena.

Leather NG (200 pcs.)
Thread NG (600 pcs.)
Adena NG (500 000 pcs.)

4: Woodley's Errands

Woodley asks you to deliver 300,000 adena to Ian to pay off the balance on his account while he's making the dress shoes. Trader Ian located in Ivory Tower.

5: Meet Woodley

Ian accepts the payment and asks you to tell Woodley that he will charge interest next time.
