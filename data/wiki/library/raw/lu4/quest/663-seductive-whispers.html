1. The starting NPC Wilbert Blacksmith is located in the Aden Blacksmith Shop. Talk to Wilbert to get the quest.

2. Go hunting and kill monsters to collect Spirit Bead NG to play with Wilbert.

3. After collecting enough medals, return to the Blacksmith Shop in Aden and talk to Wilbert Blacksmith to play cards.

The game costs 50 Spirit Bead NG . In each round, you and Wilbert reveal one card each. If your card is higher, you win; if it's lower, you lose.

After winning a round, you can either claim your reward or take a risk and play another round. Be careful: if you lose, you’ll lose everything you’ve won in this game.
